  <h1>About</h1>
  <p>I'm Mads. My wife and I have three kids, and we love getting out to local events.</p>
  <p>Eastside Family Calendar is free, with no ads. It lists family events across 15 Eastside cities. The blurbs are in our own words, and each one links to the source.</p>
  <p>To suggest an event, email <a href="mailto:suggestions@eastsidecalendar.com">suggestions@eastsidecalendar.com</a>. Photos are credited, and we'll remove one on request.</p>
  <h2>Other things we made</h2>
  <p>We started <a href="https://mathtable.fun/">Math Table</a> to help our own kids learn their times tables. It's now free games for math facts, geography and history, made for phones and tablets.</p>
  <p>I also write about family life at <a href="https://afterpickup.com">After Pickup</a>.</p>
