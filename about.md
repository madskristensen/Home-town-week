  <h1>About</h1>
  <p>Eastside Family Calendar lists upcoming family events for Eastside cities. Parks, markets, the library, and the plans worth making, as far ahead as we have them. Past events come off the list.</p>
  <p>Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Sammamish, Issaquah, Bothell, Woodinville, Kenmore, Mercer Island, Renton, and Maple Valley each have a page. The home page can point you to the nearest of those cities.</p>
  <p>Newcastle is not on the calendar yet.</p>
  <p>Mads Kristensen publishes Eastside Family Calendar.</p>
