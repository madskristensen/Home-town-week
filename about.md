<article class="page wrap prose">
  <h1>About</h1>
  <p>Hometown Week is a weekly family digest for the town you live in. Each issue covers one Monday-through-Sunday span: parks, markets, the library, and the plans worth making.</p>
  <p>Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Utah, Colorado, and Texas each have a state page. The home page can point you to the nearest city, and the footer lists every one.</p>
  <p>Mads Kristensen publishes Hometown Week.</p>
