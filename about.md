<article class="page wrap prose">
  <h1>About</h1>
  <p>Hometown Week lists upcoming family events for the town you live in. Parks, markets, the library, and the plans worth making, as far ahead as we have them. Past events come off the list.</p>
  <p>Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Utah, Colorado, Texas, and California each have a state page. The home page can point you to the nearest city. Each state page lists its cities.</p>
  <p>Mads Kristensen publishes Hometown Week.</p>
</article>
