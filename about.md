  <h1>About</h1>
  <p>Hometown Week is a weekly family digest for the town you live in. Each issue covers one Monday-through-Sunday span: parks, markets, the library, and the plans worth making.</p>
  <p>Redmond, Washington is the first city. The same pattern can hold another town later, at a path like <code>/wa/kirkland/</code>.</p>
