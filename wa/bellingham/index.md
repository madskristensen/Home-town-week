### Pajama Storytime
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 5:15–5:45 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library</p>

Ages 3–8, with siblings and family. Books, songs, and felt. Bring a stuffed animal. Wear pajamas if you want. The storytimes page lists this Wednesday and does not list another pajama storytime in this span.

[Storytimes](https://bellinghampubliclibrary.org/storytimes)

### Open Roller Skate
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 6:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Depot Market Square, 1100 Railroad Ave</p>

Free drop-in from Bellingham Parks and Recreation. All ages. Anyone under 18 needs an adult. Bring your own skates or inlines. Helmets and pads (elbow, knee, and wrist) are recommended. It is the first Friday of the month, and the listing runs through Dec 4.

[Open Roller Skate](https://www.bellingham.org/eventdetail/32936/open-roller-skate)

### GAK Crawl
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Gabriel's Art Kids, 2215 D St</p>

Free community art day, all ages. Pick up a passport at the first stop, try a hands-on activity, and collect stamps. Three or more stops earn a small prize. Every stop enters you for Spooktacular tickets. It overlaps the farmers market, so pick a morning route.

[GAK Crawl](https://www.bellingham.org/eventdetail/33211/gak-crawl)

### MegaZapper Electrical Show
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 2:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">SPARK Museum of Electrical Invention, 1312 Bay St</p>

A live science show, about an hour, every Saturday and Sunday at 2:30 p.m. Five dollars extra per person. Not for children under 5. The Cage of Doom at the end is for guests 18 and older. The museum says the show often sells out and asks you to call (360) 738-3886 ahead. The market and the art crawl are both over at 2:00, so this can be the afternoon.

[MegaZapper](https://www.sparkmuseum.org/megazapper-electrical-show/)

### BSO: Star-Cross'd Dreams
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Mount Baker Theatre, 104 N Commercial St</p>

   src="/assets/images/bellingham/mount-baker-theatre.webp"
   alt="The Mount Baker Theatre marquee and stone front on Commercial Street in Bellingham."
   credit="Photo: Singalto1, CC BY-SA 4.0"

Bellingham Symphony Orchestra with violinist Dawn Posey and Shoestring Circus. Run time 84 minutes, with an intermission. Doors at 1:45 p.m. A pre-concert talk at 2:00 p.m. is free for ticket holders. Tickets start at $20, including the historic-venue fee. Members get that fee waived at checkout. Limited tickets. Better for kids who can sit through an orchestra concert.

[BSO: Star-Cross'd Dreams](https://www.mountbakertheatre.com/events-tickets/bso-star-crossd-dreams/)
