### 'Twas the Week After Christmas auditions
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 6:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Northwest Theatre Lab, 1416 East Main Ave, Ste E</p>

Auditions for a youth holiday comedy, ages 7–14. This is a placement audition, not a public performance. Bring a memorized musical-theatre song of 45–60 seconds and a memorized monologue of 30–60 seconds. Check-in starts 30 minutes early. Wear clothes you can move in, and sneakers or jazz shoes. A video audition is an option if you are out of town: info@nwtheatrelab.com. The season page does not list a public performance date inside this span.

[Auditions](https://www.nwtheatrelab.com/upcoming-auditions-update)

### Oktoberfest Northwest
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · noon–midnight; Sat Oct 10 · 11:00 a.m.–midnight; Sun Oct 11 · 11:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Washington State Fair Event Center, 110 9th Ave SW</p>

A public fairgrounds event, not the September fair. All ages until 6:00 p.m. on Friday and Saturday, and all day Sunday. Friday noon–3:00 p.m. is free admission, with no re-entry stamp in that window. Sunday is half-price family day, with wiener dog races, a kids' root beer run (pre-registration), and Manuela Horn's Kinderfest. Kids' activities include pumpkin decorating, kids hammerschlagen, a Kinder Root Bier Garden, games, and free face painting on Saturday and Sunday. Parking is free in the Blue Lot. A regular ticket price is not printed on the details page. Baby and child food and drinks are the exception to the outside-food rule.

[Oktoberfest details](https://www.thefair.com/oktoberfest/oktoberfest-event-details/)
