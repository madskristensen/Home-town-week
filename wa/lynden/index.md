### Lynden Lions Club Model Train and Toy Show
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Northwest Washington Fairgrounds, Henry Jansen Ag. Bldg., 1775 Front St</p>

The 41st annual show: running model train layouts, exhibits, and toy vendors. The chamber calls it a family event, with trains of all gauges. Adults $10, seniors $8, children ages 6 to 12 $5, under age 6 free, and families of six or fewer $25. Sunday runs 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. at the same building.

[Chamber listing](https://www.lynden.org/events/details/lynden-lions-club-model-train-and-toy-show-10-03-2026-8952)

### The Unknowns
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 7:00–9:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Jansen Art Center, 321 Front St</p>

The Lynden Tribune community calendar lists this concert: original songs influenced by jazz, blues, rock, swing, and pop, plus covers, with three-part vocal harmony, keyboard, guitar, ukulele, acoustic bass, and drums. The Jansen ticket page did not load a price or a seating note. This is an evening concert, not a kids' show. It starts after the train show closes on Saturday.

[Tribune community calendar](https://www.lyndentribune.com/community/community-calendar-sept-23-2026/article_28d4e8e1-7227-4852-aca6-27de3d2d08c8.html)
