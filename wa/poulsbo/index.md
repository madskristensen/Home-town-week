### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Poulsbo Library, Community Room, 700 NE Lincoln Road</p>

Ages 3–5. Stories, songs, rhymes, and movement, with a playgroup afterward.

[Preschool Storytime](https://krl.bibliocommons.com/events/6a67b129cca66c2f00a65b9b)

### Kids Club
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · Time not posted</p>
<p class="event-place">Poulsbo Farmers Market, 18901 8th Ave NE</p>

Free, on the first Saturday from April through October. Oct 3 is the last Kids Club date on the 2026 list. Town & Country sponsors it. No separate clock is posted. The market that day is 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m., so plan the club inside those hours.

[Kids Club](https://www.poulsbofarmersmarket.org/kidsclub)

### Poulsbo Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">18901 8th Ave NE</p>

Saturdays from March 28 through Dec 12. Kids Club is the extra on this Saturday. It does not repeat on Oct 10.

[Poulsbo Farmers Market](https://www.poulsbofarmersmarket.org/)

### Fix-it Fair
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Poulsbo Library, 700 NE Lincoln Road</p>

Free repair for small appliances, lamps, and clothing. The last items are accepted at 1:30 p.m. Limit of two belongings, and you stay while they are repaired. The library files this under adults. It is at the library, not at 8th Ave NE, and it overlaps the late market hours.

[Fix-it Fair](https://krl.bibliocommons.com/events/68d84fb5a0570c396d570f14)

### Norwegian Pancake Breakfast
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 9:00–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Sons of Norway, 18991 Front St NE</p>

First Sunday of the month. Vern's pancakes and lingonberries, bacon, sausage, scrambled eggs, and potatoes. $15 a person, $7 for ages 6–10, and $3 for ages 5 and under.

[Norwegian Pancake Breakfast](https://visitpoulsbo.com/event/norwegian-pancake-breakfast/2026-10-04/)

### LEGO Lounge

<p class="event-place">Poulsbo Library, Community Room, 700 NE Lincoln Road</p>

Ages 5 and up. Bricks are provided. Meet the monthly challenge or build your own.

[LEGO Lounge](https://krl.bibliocommons.com/events/6a727c4db46787079ead9e78)

### Trick or Tree
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 5:30–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Olmsted Tree Farm, 17526 Clear Creek Rd NW</p>

A glow-in-the-dark candy egg hunt in the Christmas tree field. Geared toward hunters under 13, with a separate area for ages 4 and under. Ages 2 and under are free. Tickets are in advance. The evening also has a family stage show, a DJ, a trolley ride, a candy grab, a hay tower, games, and a fire truck. Spooky Woods is easy to skip. Rain or shine. No dogs. A ticket price in dollars is not printed on the Visit Poulsbo page.

[Trick or Tree](https://visitpoulsbo.com/event/trick-or-tree-glow-in-the-dark-egg-hunt/2026-10-09/)
