### A Murder Is Announced

### Write on the Sound
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2–Sun Oct 4 · Time not posted</p>
<p class="event-place">Frances Anderson Center</p>

The City of Edmonds Arts Commission has run this small writers' conference since 1985. Sessions, workshops, and panels on the craft. The in-person days are Oct 2, 3, and 4. The keynote is Debra Magpie Earling. An online pre-conference was Sat Sep 26. The city page says registration for Oct 2–4 closed Sun Sep 27. Daily start times are on the conference schedule, not in the short city blurb. Phone (425) 771-0228.

[Write on the Sound](https://edmondswa.gov/government/departments/cultural_services/write_on_the_sound)
