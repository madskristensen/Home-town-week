### Little Science Lab with Imagine Children's Museum
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 2:00–2:30 p.m. and 3:00–3:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lynnwood Library, 19200 44th Ave W</p>

### Nicho Altar
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 1:00–2:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lynnwood Library, 19200 44th Ave W</p>

Teens and adults make a nicho, or altar box, to honor someone who has died, with a short history of Día de Muertos. ArtMaranth Mobile School leads it. You can bring a photo of a pet or family member, but you do not have to. Registration is required. The library is open 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. that day.

[Nicho Altar](https://sno-isle.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6a971668be148200298baa7f)
