### Spooky movie night in the PGA District
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2, Fri Oct 9, and Fri Oct 16 · Time not posted</p>
<p class="event-place">PGA District</p>

Visit Frisco lists spooky movie nights on Oct. 2, 9, 16, 23, and 30, and it puts the September Friday series at 7:00 p.m. It does not repeat a clock on the October dates. It names Hocus Pocus, The Haunted Mansion, and more as the October mix, not as a title for one Friday. Blankets help. Parking in the district is free. Oct. 23 is after this window.

[Visit Frisco](https://www.visitfrisco.com/blog/fall-frisco-your-guide-to-autumn-events)

### Frisco Rotary Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3, Sat Oct 10, and Sat Oct 17 · 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">3000 Internet Blvd</p>

Every Saturday, rain or shine, next to Kaleidoscope Park. Produce, eggs, meat, honey, and baked goods. The market page does not print a closing date for the season. High wind, lightning, or hail can close it, so check the site that morning.

[Frisco Rotary Farmers Market](https://www.friscorotaryfarmersmarket.org/)

### Family Story Time
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Storytime Room, Frisco Public Library, 8000 Dallas Parkway</p>

Songs, rhymes, and books for walkers through age 5. Space is limited. Tickets are at the Youth Services desk starting an hour before. The Rotary market is still open then, at a different address.

[Family Story Time](https://friscolibrary.bibliocommons.com/events/6a57b29bf4e5db3d00c1e6a7)

### Frisco Oktoberfest
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">The Star</p>

Seventh year. Live music, dancing, and beer. Visit Frisco calls it a fall tradition. Parking in the Star District is free. Beer is part of the day, so it is a family outing with an adult side. It overlaps the camp-out check-in at Frisco Commons.

[Visit Frisco](https://www.visitfrisco.com/blog/fall-frisco-your-guide-to-autumn-events)

### Frisco Camp Out
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 2:00 p.m. through Sun Oct 4 · 10:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Frisco Commons Park, 8000 McKinney Road</p>

One night outside. The city page includes movies, s'mores, and says dinner and a light breakfast are covered. Camp Out swag is included. The city calendar prints 2:00 p.m. Saturday through 10:30 a.m. Sunday.

[Camp Out](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=7671)

### Haunted Harmonies
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 1:00–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Frisco Public Library, 8000 Dallas Parkway</p>

The Allen Philharmonic Orchestra. Live music and pre-concert activities. The library says there will not be jump scares. Camp Out ends at 10:30 a.m. the same morning, so a family that stayed the night can still make the concert.

[Haunted Harmonies](https://friscolibrary.bibliocommons.com/events/6aa076482dac6e003724ea40)

### Plaza Live
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 5:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">4th Street Plaza, 8800-8840 4th St</p>

Local musicians in the plaza while you eat and shop. The city says the series is the first and third Wednesdays, 5:00–7:00 p.m. Oct. 7 is the first Wednesday.

[Plaza Live](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=7708)

### Goosebumps The Musical: Phantom of the Auditorium
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 8 and Fri Oct 9 · 7:00 p.m. · Sat Oct 10 · 3:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m. · Sun Oct 11 · 3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Black Box Theatre, Frisco Discovery Center, 8004 N. Dallas Parkway</p>

CenterStage Theatre Works. Recommended for ages 8 and up. About 90–100 minutes with an intermission. Adults 19 and up are $32. Seniors 60 and up are $24. Students 12–18 are $20. Kids are $15. The city calendar lists the run Oct. 8–18 as all day and does not print these showtimes. Use the theatre page for the clock.

[CenterStage](https://www.cstheatreworks.com/goosebumps-)

### Second Saturday: Fall Fun at Heritage
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Frisco Heritage Center, 6455 Page Street</p>

Fall and Halloween crafts for kids: pumpkins, leaves, and decorations. The Rotary market that morning is 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. at 3000 Internet Blvd, a different stop. Saturday's Goosebumps matinee is 3:00 p.m., when this ends.

[Fall Fun](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=7439)

### Fall Market at PGA Frisco
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">PGA District</p>

More than 50 vendors. Kids can sign up for pumpkin decorating from 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Parking in the district is free. This is the same hours as Fall Fun at Heritage, so pick one Saturday morning.

[Visit Frisco](https://www.visitfrisco.com/blog/fall-frisco-your-guide-to-autumn-events)

### Goosebumps The Musical: Phantom of the Auditorium

<p class="event-place">Black Box Theatre, Frisco Discovery Center, 8004 N. Dallas Parkway</p>

Last weekend of this run. Same prices and the same ages 8 and up note. Sunday, Oct. 18, at 3:00 p.m. is the posted close.

### DFW Fall Fest
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Frisco Fresh Market</p>

Visit Frisco lists a farmers market, DIY craft stations, photo ops, and live music. Free admission and free parking. The Rotary market that morning is a different address, 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. Goosebumps that afternoon is 3:00 p.m. at the Discovery Center.

[Visit Frisco](https://www.visitfrisco.com/blog/fall-frisco-your-guide-to-autumn-events)

### Pumpkin Dunk
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 12:30–3:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Frisco Athletic Center, 5828 Nancy Jane Lane</p>

Swim in the indoor pool and pick a pumpkin from a floating patch, then paint it and take it home. The admission includes one pumpkin per person, decorating, swimming, face painting, and candy. No advance registration. Free for athletic-center members. Non-members need a day pass, bought at the front desk. The city page does not print the day-pass price. It overlaps DFW Fall Fest and the Goosebumps matinee.

[Pumpkin Dunk](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=7696)
