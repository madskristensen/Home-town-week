### Beetlejuice JR
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1 · 7:00 p.m. · Fri Oct 2 · 7:00 p.m. · Sat Oct 3 · 2:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Rodenbaugh Theatre, Willow Bend Center of the Arts, 6121 W Park Blvd, Ste. B216</p>

North Texas Performing Arts. A youth cast. This is the last weekend. Auditions were for grades 3–10. Tickets are $15 starting Sep. 20. The earlier advance price was $13. Front-row premium seats are $25, plus a $2 Save Our Stages fund. Saturday at 2:00 p.m. overlaps the Hubble program at 3:00 p.m. Friday and Saturday at 7:00 p.m. overlap Family Fest.

[Beetlejuice JR](https://ntpa.org/event/beetlejuice-jr-plano/)

### Family Day: Spooky Buddies
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 1:00–2:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Painting with a Twist, 1713 Preston Rd, Ste B</p>

Paint together. Ages 5 and up. $35 per person. No drop-offs, infants, or toddlers. Guests under 12 need a parent or guardian. The guardian does not have to paint. Alcohol-free. An optional soy candle is $20 if you add it when you register and arrive 15 minutes early. It ends well before Beetlejuice and the jazz night.

[Visit Plano](https://events.visitplano.com/event/family-day-spooky-buddies)

### Family Fest DFW: A Night of Jazz
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 6:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Red Tail Pavilion, 2801 E Spring Creek Pkwy</p>

Visit Plano lists doors and a DJ at 6:00 p.m. and the show at 6:30 p.m. Tickets from $43.99. Each night needs its own ticket. Children 5 and under are free with a parent. Ages 6 and up need a ticket. No pets. Coolers and lawn chairs stay in your seating spot. Free parking is first come. Lots open 60 minutes before the start. The festival home page does not print a clock. Eventbrite lists doors at 5:30 p.m. and 6:30–10:30 p.m. Use the ticket page if those door times differ. Friday's 7:00 p.m. Beetlejuice curtain is the same evening.

[Visit Plano](https://events.visitplano.com/event/family-fest-dfw-weekend-a-night-of-jazz-under-the-stars)

### Hubble: Space Telescope
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 3:00–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Schimelpfenig Library program room, 5024 Custer Road</p>

Grades K–5. Pictures from the Hubble telescope, and the mysteries they helped answer. Tickets are not online. Get them at the library starting 30 minutes before. Phone: 972-769-4200. The Beetlejuice matinee is 2:00 p.m., so a child in that show would miss this hour.

[Hubble](https://plano.libnet.info/event/16724871)

### Family Fest DFW: Neo Soul Night
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 5:45 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Red Tail Pavilion, 2801 E Spring Creek Pkwy</p>

Visit Plano lists doors at 5:15 p.m. and the DJ and concert at 5:45 p.m. Tickets from $50.39. Same kids rule as Friday: ages 5 and under free with a parent, ages 6 and up need a ticket. The festival home page does not print a clock. Hubble ends at 4:00 p.m., so the library program can come first. Beetlejuice at 7:00 p.m. is the same night.

[Visit Plano](https://events.visitplano.com/event/family-fest-dfw-weekend-neo-soul-night-under-the-stars)

### Let's Go... Somewhere Spooky!
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Robinson Fine Arts Center, 1800 Alma Dr</p>

Plano Symphony family concert. Brass and percussion, with puppets from Le Theatre de Marionette: Beauty and the Beast, In the Hall of the Mountain King, and How to Train Your Dragon. Visit Plano calls it a 45-minute adventure and does not print an end time. Tickets are $16–$20. Costumes are encouraged.

[Visit Plano](https://events.visitplano.com/event/lets-go-somewhere-spooky)

### Homeschool Day: Charlotte's Web
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 10:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Heritage Farmstead Museum, 1900 W 15th St</p>

Wilbur, Charlotte, and Templeton go to the fair. Farm chores, fair games, demonstrators, animals, and a wagon ride. Activities run 10:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. The farm stays open until 1:00 p.m. Bring a lunch. Tickets are online only. The museum page does not print a dollar price. If it sells out, there are no door tickets. Free parking on Pitman Drive and the east side of 1300 Custer. Do not park in front of businesses or houses.

[Heritage Farmstead](https://www.heritagefarmstead.org/event/homeschool-day-charlottes-web-2/)

### Downtown Plano Barktoberfest
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 11 · 11:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown Plano Arts District, 1021 E 15th Street</p>

Free. A dog-friendly fall market: pet vendors, handmade goods, and a dog costume contest. Leave the dog home if a crowd of costumes is too much, and the people-side shopping still works.

[Visit Plano](https://events.visitplano.com/event/barktoberfest)
