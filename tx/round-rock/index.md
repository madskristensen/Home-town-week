### Family Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 6:30–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Youth Program Space, 2nd floor</p>

Books, songs, and rhymes. Best for 18 months to 6 years. Other ages are welcome. ASL interpreting is offered at the Monday evening session. The date list includes Sep. 28 and Oct. 5.

[Monday storytime](https://roundrocktexas.libcal.com/event/17124392)

### Clue
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1, Fri Oct 2, and Sat Oct 3 · 7:00–9:00 p.m. · Sun Oct 4 · 1:30–3:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Penfold Theatre, 2120 N Mays St, Suite 290</p>

Same PG-13 comedy, through Oct. 11. Penfold lists about 90 minutes, tickets $29–$39, and pay-what-you-can Thursdays. The show page does not print a clock. The Round Rock visitors bureau lists these hours. A review lists Fridays and Saturdays at 7:30 p.m. and Sundays at 2:00 p.m., so check the ticket page the day you go.

[Penfold](https://www.penfoldtheatre.org/event/clue/2026-10-11/) · [Round Rock CVB](https://goroundrock.com/series/clue-at-the-penfold-theatre/)

The library is at 200 E Liberty Ave. The youth programs are in the Youth Program Space on the 2nd floor, which closes 15 minutes before the building.

### Round Rock National Night Out
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 · 6:00–9:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Neighborhoods across Round Rock</p>

The city's National Night Out is block parties, cookouts, and visits from police and emergency crews. The city page says this year's night is Tuesday, Oct. 6. The button to register a neighborhood party closed Sep. 4, so a block that did not register then may be quiet. Ask a neighbor if your street is hosting one.

[City of Round Rock](https://www.roundrocktexas.gov/event/round-rock-national-night-out-2/)
