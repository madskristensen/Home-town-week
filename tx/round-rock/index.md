### Round Rock Pumpkin Festival
<p class="event-when">Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays, and Mondays · 10:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m. · Fri Oct 2 through Sun Oct 18</p>
<p class="event-place">Old Settlers Park, 3300 E Palm Valley Blvd</p>

Fifth year, around the pioneer cabins. Pumpkins, a corn pit, bounce houses, farm animals, a barrel train, face painting, and games. Admission starting Oct. 2 is $12. Ages 2 and under are free. Ages 65 and over are free. Teachers, military, and first responders are free with ID. Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are closed for restock. Mondays include pumpkin painting if you buy a pumpkin. The same Friday-through-Monday hours continue through Oct. 31. Trailside Market is a different park on Saturday mornings.

[Pumpkin festival](https://roundrockpumpkinfestival.com/)

### Hairy Man Festival
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 12:00–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Cat Hollow Park, 8320 O'Connor Drive</p>

32nd year. Free admission. The organizer asks for a suggested donation of two non-perishable food items or $2 per person for the Round Rock Serving Center. Live music, crafts, carnival games, a petting zoo, inflatables, face painting, a costume contest at 2:00 p.m., and a reading of the legend at 4:15 p.m. A 5K starts at 9:00 a.m., before the festival opens. The visitors bureau venue line lists 8600 O'Connor Drive, so use the organizer address if the two disagree. Trailside Market ends at noon, when this starts. The pumpkin festival is open the same day at Old Settlers Park.

[Brushy Creek MUD](https://www.bcmud.org/hairyman)
