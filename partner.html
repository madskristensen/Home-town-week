Eastside Family Calendar is where local parents check what to do with their kids this weekend. It covers 15 cities, from Bothell and Kirkland to Snoqualmie and Maple Valley, plus the no-school days for ten school districts. It's free, it's made by a local family, and it has no ad networks.

A few local businesses help keep it that way. If you run a place that Eastside kids love, we'd be glad to help more families find you.

Who you'll reach

  • Parents of toddlers through teens in Bellevue, Bothell, Carnation, Duvall, Issaquah, Kenmore, Kirkland, Maple Valley, Mercer Island, North Bend, Redmond, Renton, Sammamish, Snoqualmie, and Woodinville.
  • Families planning the weekend, the next no-school day, the holidays, or the summer.
  • Readers of the weekly Eastside Family Calendar email, every Thursday, with hand-picked weekend events.
  • More than 400 upcoming family events on the calendar right now, updated every day.

What sponsors get

  • A card that looks like the rest of the calendar, with a real photo, a short blurb, and a link straight to your site.
  • A clear "Sponsored" label, so parents know exactly what they're looking at and keep trusting it.
  • A link tagged with our name, so your own site stats show the visits that came from us.
  • A real person to work with. Mads, a Redmond dad of three, reads and answers every email.

Ways to partner

Featured sponsored card

Your card in the weekend picks on the home page and on This weekend, or near the top of one city page. One sponsor per spot at a time.

$150 a month in the weekend picks. $60 a month on a city page.

Seasonal sponsor

Be the only sponsor of a seasonal page for its whole season, with a "Sponsored by" line and your card at the top.

  • Christmas and the Christmas lights map, November and December: $400 for the season
  • Fall, Halloween, and the decorations map, September and October: $300 for the season
  • Spring break, mid-March through spring break week: $150 for the season
  • Summer camps guide, January through June: $300 for the season

Weekly email sponsor

The weekly Eastside Family Calendar email goes out every Thursday with hand-picked weekend events. A sponsored spot in that email is one option: one short, labeled line. One sponsor per issue.

$35 an issue, or $120 for four issues in a row.

Enhanced summer camp listing

Basic camp listings are free. An enhanced listing adds a photo, a longer description, and the top spot for its town, labeled Sponsored. It stays at the top of that age group too.

$49 per camp for the season.

Our standards

  • Every paid spot says "Sponsored." Always.
  • Family only: camps, classes, farms, museums, venues, and kids' activities we'd happily take our own three kids to.
  • We say no when something isn't a fit, and we don't take alcohol, cannabis, vaping, gambling, or anything meant for adults only.
  • Free events stay free. Nobody pays to have an event listed, and sponsoring doesn't change what we list or what we write about anyone.
  • No ad networks, and we never collect kids' data.
  • Sponsored spots are few, so the calendar stays a calendar.

Get in touch

Email mads@eastsidecalendar.com with your business name, your town, and what you'd like to promote. We'll reply with open dates and help you put your card together.
