### Spooktacular Broadway 2
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Firehouse Arts Center, 4444 Railroad Ave</p>

Tri-Valley Theatre Company. Broadway songs with witches, phantoms, and monsters. $24–$34 before fees. Everyone, including children, needs a ticket. The Firehouse page does not print an end time. The box office opens two hours before the show. Cash or a check also works at the library recreation counter and the senior center, without the card fee.

[Firehouse listing](https://www.firehousearts.org/calendar-event/spooktacular-broadway-2/)

### Pleasanton Farmers' Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">46 W Angela St</p>

Year-round, every Saturday, rain or shine. The Pacific Coast Farmers' Market Association runs it with the Pleasanton Downtown Association. Parking is in the lots and on the surrounding streets. A vendor page puts the market at 400 Main Street. This page uses the association's address.

[PCFMA](https://www.pcfma.org/index.php/market/pleasanton-farmers-market)
