### American Landscapes
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 5:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Harris Center, 10 College Pkwy</p>

The Pops Chorale and Orchestra. Choir, small groups, and instruments, built around American film and stories. It is a concert, not a kids show. The Harris Center is on the Folsom Lake College campus.

[Folsom Times listing](https://folsom.biz/events/american-landscapes/2026-10-03)
