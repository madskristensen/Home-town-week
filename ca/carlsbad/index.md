### State Street Farmers' Market
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 2:30–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">State Street, between Carlsbad Village Drive and Grand Ave</p>

Produce, flowers, and prepared food. The Village Association lists this Wednesday, then Oct. 7, Oct. 14, Oct. 21, Oct. 28, and Nov. 4, same clock. The page gives both 2970 State St and 2695 State Street. The block is the useful address.

[Market dates](https://www.carlsbad-village.com/do/state-street-farmers-market-5)

### Super Hero Obstacle Race
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 8:00–10:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Alga Norte Community Park, 6565 Alicante Rd</p>

Parents and kids run a themed 2K together, in costume. Ages 4 and up. $33 a person. Groups leave every 10 minutes, and the city says the course takes about an hour. You pick a start inside the 8:00–10:00 a.m. window. Registration stays open until 5:00 p.m. Friday, Oct. 2, or at the park until 9:30 a.m. if it is not full. Each runner gets a cape, a bib, and a medal. The city can change or cancel it for weather.

[City page](https://www.carlsbadca.gov/departments/parks-recreation/programs-and-events/special-events/super-hero-obstacle-race)

### Pumpkin Plunge
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 5:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Alga Norte Aquatic Center, 6565 Alicante Rd</p>

All ages. City of Carlsbad pool night: games, floating obstacles, and the splash pad for little kids. A dry side has a bounce house, lawn games, and a floating pumpkin patch where each child lassos a pumpkin to keep. $15 a person. Ages 3 and under are free and still need a registration. Food is for sale. Online presale ends at 5:00 p.m. Oct. 16. Wristbands can be picked up at Alga Norte the week before.

[KPBS listing, credited to the city](https://www.kpbs.org/events/2026/10/17/pumpkin-plunge)

### Día de los Muertos at Leo Carrillo Ranch
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 31 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Leo Carrillo Ranch Historic Park, 6200 Flying Leo Carrillo Ln</p>

Free. All ages. The ranch house is open 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Storytelling in the hacienda is 10:00 a.m. to noon. Face painting is 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Crafts, a community ofrenda, and photo stops run 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Ballet folklórico is at 11:00 a.m. Grupo Bella plays at 12:30 p.m. Food is for sale. KPBS credits the listing to the city. A KPBS line gives the zip as 92677, which is not a Carlsbad zip. This page uses the street and skips that zip. Halloween in the Village starts at 11:00 a.m., so the two overlap.

[KPBS listing, credited to the city](https://www.kpbs.org/events/2026/10/31/dia-de-los-muertos-at-leo-carrillo-ranch)

### Halloween in the Village
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 31 · 11:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Carlsbad Village</p>

Trick-or-treating at shops that post a candy-corn sign. The costume contest is at Village Faire, 300 Carlsbad Village Drive. Sign-up starts at 11:30 a.m. The contest is noon to 1:00 p.m. Carnival games and a bounce house are at the Boys & Girls Clubs of Carlsbad, 3115 Roosevelt Street, with no entry fee. Lily Field Realty is listed for sensory-friendly activities and allergy-friendly treats.
