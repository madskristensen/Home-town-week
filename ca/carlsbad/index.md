### Pumpkin Plunge
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 5:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Alga Norte Aquatic Center, 6565 Alicante Rd</p>

All ages. City of Carlsbad pool night: games, floating obstacles, and the splash pad for little kids. A dry side has a bounce house, lawn games, and a floating pumpkin patch where each child lassos a pumpkin to keep. $15 a person. Ages 3 and under are free and still need a registration. Food is for sale. Online presale ends at 5:00 p.m. Oct. 16. Wristbands can be picked up at Alga Norte the week before.

[KPBS listing, credited to the city](https://www.kpbs.org/events/2026/10/17/pumpkin-plunge)

### Halloween in the Village
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 31 · 11:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Carlsbad Village</p>

Trick-or-treating at shops that post a candy-corn sign. The costume contest is at Village Faire, 300 Carlsbad Village Drive. Sign-up starts at 11:30 a.m. The contest is noon to 1:00 p.m. Carnival games and a bounce house are at the Boys & Girls Clubs of Carlsbad, 3115 Roosevelt Street, with no entry fee. Lily Field Realty is listed for sensory-friendly activities and allergy-friendly treats.

[Village Association](https://www.carlsbad-village.com/post/halloween-in-the-village-returns-october-31-for-a-village-wide-celebration-1)
