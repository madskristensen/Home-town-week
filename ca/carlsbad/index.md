### Super Hero Obstacle Race
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 8:00–10:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Alga Norte Community Park, 6565 Alicante Rd</p>

Parents and kids run a themed 2K together, in costume. Ages 4 and up. $33 a person. Groups leave every 10 minutes, and the listing says the course takes about an hour. You pick a start inside the 8:00–10:00 a.m. window. Registration stays open until 5:00 p.m. the Friday before, or at the park until 9:30 a.m. if it is not full. Each runner gets a cape, a bib, and a medal. The listing says the city can change or cancel it for weather. The city special-events page returned a block on this crawl. This clock and price are from the Carlsbad Patch listing of the city race.

[Carlsbad Patch](https://patch.com/california/carlsbad/calendar/event/20261004/148bb517-5660-4331-ba37-0540e907f322/super-hero-obstacle-race)

### Pumpkin Plunge
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 5:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Alga Norte Aquatic Center, 6565 Alicante Rd</p>

All ages. City of Carlsbad pool night: games, floating obstacles, and the splash pad for little kids. A dry side has a bounce house, lawn games, and a floating pumpkin patch where each child lassos a pumpkin to keep. $15 a person. Ages 3 and under are free and still need a registration. Food is for sale. Online presale ends at 5:00 p.m. Oct. 16. Wristbands can be picked up at Alga Norte the week before. KPBS credits the listing to the city.

[KPBS listing, credited to the city](https://www.kpbs.org/events/2026/10/17/pumpkin-plunge)
