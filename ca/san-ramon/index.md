### Fall Nature Storytime: Apples
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 10:15–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Crow Canyon Gardens</p>

Built for ages 2–5. All ages can come. A story, a craft, and a sensory game. The city asks you to register. The storytime list does not print a fee, and it does not print a street number for the gardens. The Fridays after this one, still inside this window, are leaves on Oct. 9 and corn on Oct. 16. The same table also prints pumpkins on Oct. 23 and a Halloween special on Oct. 30.

[Nature programs](https://www.sanramon.ca.gov/our_city/departments_and_divisions/parks_community_services/nature_programs/upcoming_nature_events)

### San Ramon Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">City Center Bishop Ranch, 6000 Bollinger Canyon Rd</p>

Growers, food trucks, and music. The event page prints 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. and also lists Oct. 10, Oct. 17, and Oct. 24 at that clock. Its add-to-calendar link stamps that hour as America/Denver. Use the 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. clock printed on the event page. Fun on the Farm starts at 11:00 a.m., at Forest Home Farms, so the two overlap for the last two hours.

[Oct. 3 market](https://citycenterbishopranch.com/event-all/san-ramon-farmers-market/2026-10-03/)

### Fun on the Farm: Farm Horse
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Forest Home Farms</p>

Free. Meet the model horse Ramon, see the old horse equipment, and sit in a saddle. The city and the San Ramon Historic Foundation run these Saturdays. The events page does not print a street number. Critter Crawl is the same afternoon at 4:00 p.m.

[Farm events](https://www.sanramon.ca.gov/our_city/departments_and_divisions/parks_community_services/forest_home_farms_historic_park/events)

### Run for Education
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 11 · 8:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Iron Horse Middle School, 12601 Alcosta Blvd</p>

{% include event-photo.html
   src="/assets/images/san-ramon/mount-diablo.webp"
   alt="Mount Diablo seen across a field from San Ramon."
   credit="Photo: OettingerCroat, public domain"
   source="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mt_Diablo_From_San_Ramon.jpg" %}

The San Ramon Valley Education Foundation's 43rd run. The expo and kids zone at Iron Horse run 8:00 a.m. to noon, with inflatables, a face painter, a fire engine, and a patrol car. Face painting is 9:00 a.m. to noon. The 5K and the 1.5 mile start at 8:30 a.m. The Friendship Run is 9:45 a.m. The mini run, for ages 2–6, starts in heats at 10:15 a.m. Recognition is at 11:00 a.m. The 10K starts at 8:15 a.m. at Charlotte Wood Middle School, 600 El Capitan Drive, Danville, and finishes here. Registration is required. The page does not print a spectator price. The photo is Mount Diablo from San Ramon, not the school.

[San Ramon Valley Education Foundation](https://srvef.org/the-run/)
