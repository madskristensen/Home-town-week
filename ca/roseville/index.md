### Floating Pumpkin Patch
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 18 · 9:00–10:00 a.m., 10:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m., and 1:00–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Mike Shellito Indoor Pool</p>

$17 a person, and the ticket includes a pumpkin. Children under 2 are free with a paying adult. Everyone registers, including people who are not swimming. The 9:00 a.m. hour is sensory-friendly, capped at 75, and that ticket also covers the 10:00 a.m. session. A second session is 1:00–3:00 p.m. Costumes are encouraged. No refunds. The page also lists games, bounce houses, and crafts. The city page does not print a street for the pool. The Maidu market is the same morning, at 1550 Maidu Drive. James and the Giant Peach is at 2:00 p.m., while the last swim session is still going.
