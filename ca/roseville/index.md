### OAKtober Festival
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 9:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Maidu Museum and Historic Site, 1970 Johnson Ranch Dr</p>

{% include event-photo.html
   src="/assets/images/roseville/maidu-museum.webp"
   alt="The Maidu Museum and Historic Site, a round earth-toned building with a grass-covered roof."
   credit="Photo: Cullen328, CC BY-SA 4.0"
   source="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maidu_Museum_%26_Historic_Site.jpg" %}


Free. Gates at 9:00 a.m. Native garden tours at 10:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m. UC Master Gardeners at 11:00 a.m. Compost talk at noon. A book talk on Native California foodways at 1:00 p.m. The museum, a native plant sale, a pumpkin patch, crafts, and acorn pounding run through the day. It ends at 3:00 p.m.

The city page calls this Saturday, October 4. October 4, 2026 is a Sunday, which is also the farmers market day. This page uses the date the city printed and the weekday that date actually is.

The Maidu Park market that morning is at 1550 Maidu Drive, not at the museum door.

[City OAKtober page](https://cityofroseville.hosted.civiclive.com/residents/city_events/o_a_ktober_festival)

### Trunk or Treat
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 16 · 5:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Mahany Fitness Center, 1545 Pleasant Grove Blvd</p>

Free. All ages. Decorated trunks and booths. The city asks displays to stay whimsical, not gory. Weather permitting. After the candy, the Spooky Story Walk is in the Mahany Park courtyard. The city does not print a separate clock for the walk.

Boo Boxes are a paid take-home kit, $29 a child and $25 for a sibling, for pickup at this building Oct. 5–16 during facility hours, and also for sale at this event. The box is not a second event.

### Floating Pumpkin Patch
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 18 · 9:00–10:00 a.m., 10:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m., and 1:00–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Mike Shellito Indoor Pool</p>

$17 a person, and the ticket includes a pumpkin. Children under 2 are free with a paying adult. Everyone registers, including people who are not swimming. The 9:00 a.m. hour is sensory-friendly, capped at 75, and that ticket also covers the 10:00 a.m. session. A second session is 1:00–3:00 p.m. Costumes are encouraged. No refunds. The city page does not print a street for the pool. The Maidu market is the same morning, at 1550 Maidu Drive.

[City page](https://www.roseville.ca.gov/programs_events/floating_pumpkin_patch.php)
