### OAKtober Festival
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 9:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Maidu Museum and Historic Site, 1970 Johnson Ranch Dr</p>

Free. Gates at 9:00 a.m. Native garden tours at 10:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m. UC Master Gardeners at 11:00 a.m. Compost talk at noon. A book talk on Native California foodways at 1:00 p.m. The museum, a native plant sale, a pumpkin patch, crafts, and acorn pounding run through the day. It ends at 3:00 p.m.

The city page calls this Saturday, October 4. October 4, 2026 is a Sunday, which is also the farmers market day. This page uses the date the city printed and the weekday that date actually is.

The Maidu Park market that morning is at 1550 Maidu Drive, not at the museum door.

[City OAKtober page](https://cityofroseville.hosted.civiclive.com/residents/city_events/o_a_ktober_festival)
