### Fort Collins Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>

### First Friday Art Walk
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 6:00–9:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown Fort Collins</p>

The Downtown Development Authority says every first Friday, 6:00–9:00 p.m. Oct. 2 is that Friday. A self-guided walk of galleries and shops, for all ages. Friday's 7:30 p.m. curtain at the Lincoln Center overlaps the last part of the walk.

[Downtown Fort Collins](https://downtownfortcollins.com/play/first-friday-art-walk)

### Pastries and Pirouettes
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 7:30 p.m. · Sat Oct 3 · 7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Magnolia Theatre, The Lincoln Center, 417 W Magnolia St</p>

High Performance Dance Theatre's bakery-themed concert, with the junior company on the same bill. Regular tickets are $21. Seniors 60+, students, and children 12 and under are $18. A group of 10 or more is $14.50. Limited complimentary tickets for children 2 and under are available by calling the box office at 970-221-6730. The Lincoln Center does not list an end time.

[Lincoln Center](https://www.lctix.com/shows-tickets/pastries-pirouettes/)

### National Geographic LIVE: Chasing Monarchs
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Performance Hall, The Lincoln Center, 417 W Magnolia St</p>

### FoCo Fall Fest
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 4:00–9:00 p.m. · Sat Oct 10 and Sun Oct 11 · 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Old Town Square</p>

Free. Friday is the kickoff in Old Town Square. Saturday and Sunday add a larger footprint, with makers, food, music, and kids' activities. Craft drinks are part of the festival. The kids' activities are the family reason to go early. The Downtown Development Authority page says an outside group produces it.

[Downtown Fort Collins](https://downtownfortcollins.com/do/foco-fall-fest-1)
