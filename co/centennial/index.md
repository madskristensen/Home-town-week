### Spanish Family Storytime / Hora de cuentos para la familia
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 4:00–4:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Smoky Hill Library, 5430 S Biscay Circle</p>

Reading, singing, rhymes, and movement. Planned for ages 0–5. Older children are welcome. Children under 8 need an adult. Child and Family Library Services: 303-542-7279.

[Spanish Family Storytime](https://arapahoelibraries.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6ab13eaa19094f0c6939215d)

### Vejigantes: Masks with a Story
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Koelbel Library, 5955 S Holly St</p>

Puerto Rican vejigante masks, then make one and join a short parade. Offered in Spanish and English, for kids about 5 to 12. Meeting rooms A and B.

[Vejigantes](https://arapahoelibraries.bibliocommons.com/events/6a96efeb392e9c02993d3dd9)

### Try Hockey for Free
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 1:00–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">South Suburban Sports Complex</p>

Ages 4–9, coed. Skate, play, and learn the basics. No skating or hockey experience required. South Suburban supplies the hockey equipment. The news post does not print a separate rink street. The district contact address on that page is 4810 E County Line Rd, Littleton. South Suburban covers the parks district west of I-25.

[South Suburban](https://www.ssprd.org/News-And-Events/Post/20717)

### Tbilisoba Festival
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 12:00–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Centennial Center Park, 13050 E Peakview Ave</p>

A Georgian culture day from the Colorado Georgian Community Association: music, dance, and food. The concert block is 3:00–5:00 p.m. Children under 12 are free. The ticket page lists general admission at $15 online and $20 at the door. Georgian wine is part of the afternoon, so it is a family festival with an adult table.

[Tickets](https://ticketstripe.com/events/5665108868207288)

### Little Explorers
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 · 10:00–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Meeting Room A, Smoky Hill Library, 5430 S Biscay Circle</p>

Ages 2–5. A monthly theme that mixes science, engineering, math, art, and literacy. Register each child and adult. Drop-ins if there is room.

[Little Explorers](https://arapahoelibraries.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6a96cdc529a7dd0d67482dae)

### The Snowy Day and Other Stories
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 · 6:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">PACE Center, 20000 Pikes Peak Ave, Parker</p>

This is in Parker, not Centennial. Shadow puppetry from Ezra Jack Keats: The Snowy Day, Whistle for Willie, Goggles!, and A Letter to Amy. Parker Arts lists it for pre-K through 3rd grade. Tickets are $29. Every child needs a ticket. The page does not list an end time. It starts after Little Explorers at Smoky Hill, so the two are not the same stop.

[Parker Arts](https://parkerarts.org/event/the-snowy-day-and-other-stories/)

### Meet author Lily Williams
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 11:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. and 1:00–2:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Meeting Rooms A and B, Smoky Hill Library, 5430 S Biscay Cir</p>

The morning storytime is for children and families, on her series If Animals Disappeared. Families get one free copy of the featured book while supplies last. The afternoon session is for tweens, ages 9–12, on her graphic novel Exposures, with a signing after. That session also gives a free copy of Exposures while supplies last. The library lists the building open 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. that Saturday.

[Children and families](https://arapahoelibraries.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6a835cbb5d8bc300315ab4ee) · [Tweens](https://arapahoelibraries.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6a835cd0386764026a1747a4)

Snacks, pumpkin painting, and mask making. It starts after the Lily Williams afternoon session at Smoky Hill.

[Fall Fun Drop-In](https://arapahoelibraries.bibliocommons.com/events/6a96d6c460ccaf01c0228215)
