### Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 6:30–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner, Castle Rock Library, 100 S Wilcox St</p>

All ages, with ages 0–5 listed as the group. Allergen-free, like the other storytimes. Phone: 303-791-7323.

[Monday storytime](https://go.dcl.org/event/14287140)

### Restaurant Week
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 through Sun Oct 11 · Time not posted</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown Castle Rock</p>

The town calendar lists the run as all day, and the downtown page says "all week," with no clock. Participating restaurants add a special item that is not on the regular menu. The downtown page says to check social media in October for each dish. The town listing says the event itself is free.

[Downtown restaurant week](https://www.downtowncastlerock.com/restaurantweek)

### Bark for Books
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:15–11:45 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Castle Rock Library, 100 S Wilcox St</p>

Ages 6–12 read aloud to a therapy dog. Sign up for one 15-minute session. The same branch page also lists a 10:30–11:00 a.m. storytime in Kids Corner, so a child in a reading slot during that half hour would miss part of storytime.

[Bark for Books](https://go.dcl.org/event/15395932)

### Festival Park Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Festival Park, 300 Second St</p>

Free. The town market page and the LOCAL Colorado both say Sundays, May 24 through Oct. 11, 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.

[Market page](https://www.crgov.com/3178/Festival-Park-Farmers-Market)

### Festival Park Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 11 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Festival Park, 300 Second St</p>

Free. The town calendar lists this Sunday, 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., and the market page says the Sunday run ends Oct. 11. The Fall Festival is the next Sunday at the same park.

[Oct. 11 listing](https://www.crgov.com/calendar.aspx?EID=10771)

### Spooktacular
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 1:00–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Philip S. Miller Park, 1375 West Plum Creek Parkway</p>

Free and outdoors. Games, prizes, food, and candy. Family-friendly costumes are encouraged. The town lists 1:00–2:00 p.m. as sensory-friendly, 2:00–3:00 p.m. for last names A through L, and 3:00–4:00 p.m. for M through Z. New this year, a Children's Entrepreneur Market of handmade goods from young residents.

[Town calendar](https://www.crgov.com/Calendar.aspx?EID=10953)

### Fall Festival
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 18 · 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Festival Park, 300 Second St</p>

Free. Seasonal vendors, roasted chilies, fall produce, pumpkin decorating, outdoor games, crafts, a photo booth, and pumpkins for sale. Douglas Land Conservancy brings Creepy Crawlies Around the Rock, with snakes, toads, and other invertebrates. Food trucks and beverage vendors are on site. This is the Sunday after the farmers market season ends.

[Town calendar](https://www.crgov.com/FallFestival)
