    Holly Days Window Fest

    • Free
    • Outdoor
    Downtown North Bend storefronts

    Help decorate downtown North Bend for winter by sticking snowflake clings on shop windows. The North Bend Downtown Foundation brings all the supplies, and the clings mean no mess. Free and good for families.

    Holly Days Window Fest

    Wed Nov 18 · 6:00–6:45 p.m.

    Dig into Dinos with Mikey the Rad Scientist

    • Free
    • Kids
    • Indoor
    North Bend Library, 115 E 4th Street

    A musical dinosaur show where kids sing, dance, and roar along to songs about dinos and other fierce animals. Ages 2 to 8 with an adult. Free, no registration.

    Dig into Dinos with Mikey the Rad Scientist

    Sat Dec 5 · 5:00–8:00 p.m.

    Holly Days Winter Festival and Tree Lighting

    • Free
    • Outdoor
    Downtown North Bend, North Bend Way and Main Avenue

    North Bend's winter festival downtown, with the mayor lighting the tree, fireside s'mores, local music, and free photos with Santa. Free.

    Holly Days Winter Festival and Tree Lighting

    Weekends Oct 17–18, Oct 24–25, and Oct 31–Nov 1

    Halloween Train

    $16 to $32
    • Outdoor
    • Sign-up needed
    North Bend Depot, 205 E McClellan Street

    The Northwest Railway Museum boards this fall ride at the North Bend Depot, behind the Shell station, and also at the Snoqualmie Depot. The trip is just under two hours, with a stop for cider, apple pressing, crafts, and music. Costumes are welcome. Ages 2 and up need a ticket. North Bend departures start at 10:30 a.m. and leave every two hours. Arrive about 30 minutes early.

    Halloween Train

    Tue Nov 3 · 9:30–11:00 a.m.

    Play and Learn

    • Free
    • Toddlers
    • Indoor
    Cascade Covenant Church, 13225 436th Ave SE, North Bend

    Free drop-in play for children 12 to 36 months and a caregiver. Encompass uses play to practice social skills. No registration. Use the entrance off Cedar Falls Way. The group follows the Snoqualmie Valley School District calendar.

    North Pole Limited Santa Train

    $39
    • Outdoor
    • Sign-up needed
    North Bend Depot, 205 E McClellan St

    A Santa train for families from the North Bend Depot, $39 for ages 2 and up, and no ticket under 2. The trip is about two hours and includes the ride, the Train Shed, a visit with Santa in the Chapel Car, a small gift, cookies, and a hot drink. It runs Nov 28–29, Dec 5–7, Dec 12–13, and Dec 19–20. Buy tickets ahead. Trains run rain or shine, and the museum does not refund tickets unless the train does not run.

    Santa Train
