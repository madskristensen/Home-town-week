For toddlers and babies, the Covington Library has Baby Story Time on Mondays at 1:00 p.m., and the Maple Valley Library has Pajama Story Time on Tuesday evenings and Family Story Time on Wednesday mornings, plus crafts for 3s, 4s, and 5s. All are free.
