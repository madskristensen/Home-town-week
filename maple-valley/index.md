### Zombie Dash
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 9:45–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lake Wilderness Park, Beach Lawn</p>

A mile-long family fun run through the park, with playful obstacles and zombie actors who try to grab flags. Waves start every 15 minutes from 9:45 to 11:00 a.m. Check in on the Beach Lawn at least 15 minutes before your wave. The fee is $35. The recommended age is 13 and older. Runners younger than 13 may come with an adult. Ages 10 and up can join the 11:00 a.m. friendlier wave. The page says register by 8:00 a.m. on Mon Sep 28 for a guaranteed t-shirt. Registration closes Mon Oct 12 at 8:00 a.m., or when a wave fills.

[Zombie Dash](https://www.maplevalleywa.gov/government/departments/parks_and_recreation/special_events/zombie_dash.php)
