### Music and Movement in Mandarin
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28, Mon Oct 5, and Mon Oct 12 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lake Oswego Public Library, 706 Fourth Street</p>

Sharon Gan of Heart Strings Mandarin sings, moves, plays instruments, and tells stories. Beginners and native speakers are both welcome. Recommended for ages 2–5. Space is limited, first come. It repeats every Monday.

[Music and Movement in Mandarin](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/kids/music-and-movement-mandarin-4)

### Lake Oswego Farmers' Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 8:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Millennium Plaza Park</p>

Music is 10:30 a.m.–12:30 p.m. Farm Fresh Kids is at the market, and SNAP matching is up to $20 a week. The season runs Saturdays through Oct. 31, except July 4.

[Farmers' Market](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/lofm)

### First Sunday Market: Back to School Block Party
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 11:00 a.m.–2:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Hallmark Drive, Lake Oswego</p>

The city's 2026 themes name Oct. 4 as the Back to School Block Party: carnival games, face painting, and the regular vendors. Live music is 12:00–2:00 p.m. Nov. 1 is Pet-a-Palooza, which is outside this span.

[First Sunday Market](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/lgfm)

### Día de los Muertos Celebration
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 1:00–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lake Oswego Public Library, 706 Fourth Street</p>

The block party ends at 2:30, so the second hour of this craft is after the market. Folklórico artist Luisa Valentín Pelagio talks about the tradition, then families make papel picado and tissue-paper flowers for a mini ofrenda. All ages. Photos of loved ones are welcome.

[Día de los Muertos](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/library/dia-de-los-muertos-celebration)
