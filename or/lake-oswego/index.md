### Music and Movement in Mandarin
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>

### First Sunday Market: Back to School Block Party
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 11:00 a.m.–2:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Hallmark Drive, Lake Oswego</p>

The city's 2026 themes name Oct. 4 as the Back to School Block Party: carnival games, face painting, and the regular vendors. Live music is 12:00–2:00 p.m. This is not a smaller copy of the Saturday market. Nov. 1 is Pet-a-Palooza, which is outside this span.

[First Sunday Market](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/lgfm)

The same Sunday, the library hosts a Día de los Muertos celebration from 1:00 to 3:00 p.m. The block party ends at 2:30, so the second hour of the craft is after the market. Folklórico artist Luisa Valentín Pelagio talks about the tradition, then families make papel picado. The library is at 706 Fourth Street.

[Día de los Muertos](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/library/dia-de-los-muertos-celebration)
