### Bend Fall Festival
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:00 a.m.–10:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown Bend</p>

BEAT Children's Theater is 11:00 a.m.–noon. School of Rock follows at 12:30 p.m. The rest of the day is improv, trivia, and bands through Low Down Brass Band at 8:30 p.m. Art in Action has slime, a pen bar, and pumpkin painting. The Harvest Bake-Off is new this year, with pie, bread, and chili.
