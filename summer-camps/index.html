A few things that help

Put the resident morning on the calendar the week the guide comes out. The general day is the backup, not the plan.

A full day covers a work day. A half day still needs a ride at noon.

When a row says 2027 dates are not posted yet, last year's weeks are a hint. Check the camp before you plan a vacation around them.
