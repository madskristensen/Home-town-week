{%- comment -%} Group published issues relative to today in the site timezone. Expects include.issues. Sets this_week_issues, this_week, coming_up, past_issues, featured_issue. This week: Monday–Sunday span contains today. Coming up: week starts after this week's Monday, soonest first. Past issues: Sunday is before today, newest first. Featured: the current issue (this week, else nearest past, else soonest future). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign today = site.data.calendar.today -%} {%- assign monday = site.data.calendar.monday -%} {%- assign grouped_issues = include.issues -%} {%- assign this_week_issues = grouped_issues | where_exp: "item", "item.week_start <= today and item.week_end >= today" | sort: "week_start" -%} {%- assign this_week = this_week_issues | last -%} {%- assign coming_up = grouped_issues | where_exp: "item", "item.week_start > monday" | sort: "week_start" -%} {%- assign past_issues = grouped_issues | where_exp: "item", "item.week_end < today" | sort: "week_start" | reverse -%} {%- include current-issue.html issues=grouped_issues -%} {%- assign featured_issue = current_issue -%}
