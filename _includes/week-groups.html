{%- comment -%} Group published issues relative to today in the site timezone. Expects include.issues. Sets this_week_issues, this_week, coming_up, past_issues, featured_issue. This week: Monday–Sunday span contains today. Coming up: week starts after this week's Monday, soonest first. Past issues: Sunday is before today, newest first. Featured: this week, otherwise the soonest coming up, otherwise the newest past. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign today = site.data.calendar.today -%} {%- assign monday = site.data.calendar.monday -%} {%- assign grouped_issues = include.issues -%} {%- assign this_week_issues = grouped_issues | where_exp: "item", "item.week_start <= today and item.week_end >= today" | sort: "week_start" -%} {%- assign this_week = this_week_issues | last -%} {%- assign coming_up = grouped_issues | where_exp: "item", "item.week_start > monday" | sort: "week_start" -%} {%- assign past_issues = grouped_issues | where_exp: "item", "item.week_end < today" | sort: "week_start" | reverse -%} {%- if this_week -%} {%- assign featured_issue = this_week -%} {%- elsif coming_up.size > 0 -%} {%- assign featured_issue = coming_up | first -%} {%- elsif past_issues.size > 0 -%} {%- assign featured_issue = past_issues | first -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign featured_issue = nil -%} {%- endif -%}
