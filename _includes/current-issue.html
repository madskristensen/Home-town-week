{%- comment -%} Pick the issue to treat as current for a city. Expects include.issues (one city's issues). Sets current_issue. Today is site.data.calendar.today in America/Los_Angeles (YYYY-MM-DD). Week bounds are week_start / week_end from the date-span slug. 1. The Monday–Sunday span that contains today. 2. Otherwise the nearest published past week (latest week_end before today). 3. Otherwise the soonest future week. Never the farthest-ahead draft. Sorting by date descending is the max future week, and that is not this week. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign today = site.data.calendar.today -%} {%- assign pool = include.issues -%} {%- assign current_span = pool | where_exp: "item", "item.week_start <= today and item.week_end >= today" | sort: "week_start" | last -%} {%- assign current_past = pool | where_exp: "item", "item.week_end < today" | sort: "week_end" | reverse | first -%} {%- assign current_future = pool | where_exp: "item", "item.week_start > today" | sort: "week_start" | first -%} {%- if current_span -%} {%- assign current_issue = current_span -%} {%- elsif current_past -%} {%- assign current_issue = current_past -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign current_issue = current_future -%} {%- endif -%}
