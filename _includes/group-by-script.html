{%- comment -%} The Group by pills for a camp-directory list (summer camps and birthday parties). Rows carry data-{mode}, data-{mode}-label or -name, and data-{mode}-order. "ages" is the camps' multi-group mode. A card list sets data-row, data-list, and data-meta on the .camp-directory root. "distance" is one flat list, closest first, from data-lat and data-lng. Rows with no coordinates go last under the list's data-no-address heading. "open" (farmers markets) reads data-season-start, data-season-end, and data-season-extra (or data-season-ranges for a row with several seasons) and compares them with today in America/Los_Angeles in the browser: Open this season, Closed for the season, then Check before you go for rows with no season dates. {%- endcomment -%} {%- include near-me.html -%}
