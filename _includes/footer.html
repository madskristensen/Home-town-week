Install app

  1. Tap Safari's Share button in the browser toolbar (the square with an up arrow, next to the address bar). On iPhone it is at the bottom of the screen. On iPad it is at the top right. This is Safari's button, not the share icons on this page.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
