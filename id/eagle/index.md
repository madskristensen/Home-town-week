### The Fantasticks community gathering
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Eagle Performing Arts Center, 1125 E State St</p>

Eagle Theatre Company lists a community gathering to meet the director, actors, and crew of The Fantasticks. Public performances are Nov. 6–8, Nov. 14–15, and Nov. 21–22, with Friday and Saturday curtains at 7:30 p.m. and Sunday matinees at 2:00 p.m. Those dates are after this span.

[Theatre events](https://eagletc.org/events)

### Friends of the Eagle Library Book Sale
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. · Sat Oct 10 · 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Community Hall</p>

The city calendar lists the fall Friends sale both days at those hours. Saturday overlaps Harvest Fest. The listing names Community Hall and does not add a street number.

[City calendar](https://cityofeagle.org/calendar.aspx?month=10&year=2026)

### Eagle Harvest Fest
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Heritage Park, 185 E State St</p>

The Great Pumpkin Giveaway is 1,500 pumpkins for kids to decorate and take home. The city says to come early. Also listed: kids' crafts from the Eagle Public Library, fiddle music, blacksmith demos, food vendors, and a visit from the Idaho Liberty Bell. The Saturday market itself ended Sep. 26. Night of a Thousand Pumpkins is later, Oct. 24, 6:00–9:00 p.m., at Eagle City Hall Campus, 660 E Civic Lane.

[Harvest Fest](https://cityofeagle.org/303/Harvest-Fest)

### Mother Son Monster Mash
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 6:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Community Hall at Eagle City Hall</p>

Treats, crafts, games, and a DJ. Costumes welcome. The city invites young boys with a mom, grandma, neighbor, or another woman who is important to them. Pre-registration is required. The events page says registration opened Aug. 17.

[City events](https://www.cityofeagle.org/430/Events)
