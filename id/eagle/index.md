### Eagle Fire open house, confirm first
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:00 a.m. on AllEvents</p>
<p class="event-place">966 E Iron Eagle Drive</p>

AllEvents lists the Eagle Fire annual open house at 11:00 a.m. this Saturday at that address. The department's own page says the open house is each October, during Fire Prevention Week, and it does not print a 2026 date or hours. Do not plan the morning on the AllEvents clock until the station confirms it.

[AllEvents listing](https://allevents.in/eagle/eagle-fire-annual-open-house/200030732618632) · [Eagle Fire](https://www.eaglefireid.gov/Fire-Prevention-Education)

### Eagle Harvest Fest
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Heritage Park, 185 E State Street</p>

The Great Pumpkin Giveaway is 1,500 pumpkins for kids to decorate and take home. The city says to come early. Also listed: kids' crafts from the Eagle Public Library, fiddle music, blacksmith demos, food vendors, and a visit from the Idaho Liberty Bell. The Saturday market itself is over. This is the fall festival in the same park.

[Harvest Fest](https://cityofeagle.org/303/Harvest-Fest)
