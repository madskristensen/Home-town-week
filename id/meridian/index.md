### Meridian Oktoberfest
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown Meridian, Idaho Avenue and 2nd Street</p>

The chamber's family block is 10:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. at Generations Plaza, with crafts from the Children's Museum of Idaho. A youth strudel contest for ages 8–14 is at 11:00 a.m. on the main stage. Boise Edelweiss plays German band music from noon to 3:45 p.m. Beer service is 21+ with an ID and a wristband. No cover for the music.

[Meridian Oktoberfest](https://meridianchamber.org/meridian-oktoberfest/)

The Main Street Market is the same morning, a short walk away, and it closes at 1:30 p.m.
