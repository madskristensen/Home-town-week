  <h2 id="good-to-know">Good to know</h2>
  <p class="hub-section-intro">State law keeps kids out of the bar area, so even at the friendliest taproom you'll sit at a table or out on the patio. Some taprooms are 21 and over at one location and all ages at another. Black Raven's Redmond taproom is adults only, while the Woodinville one welcomes kids, so we only list the all-ages ones here. Flatstick lets kids in until 9 p.m.</p>
