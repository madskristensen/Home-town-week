For toddlers, the Mercer Island Library has Family Story Time on Tuesdays at 10:15 a.m. and Sundays at 11:15 a.m., and Mandarin Story Time on Thursdays at 10:30 a.m. Tot Shabbat meets at the Stroum Jewish Community Center on Friday mornings. All are free.
