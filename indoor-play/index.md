  <h2 id="meet-parents">Meeting other parents</h2>
  <p class="hub-section-intro">The community center toddler mornings are the easiest place to meet other families with little ones. They're cheap, the same faces come back every week, and the grown-ups stay. Si View's Indoor Playground in North Bend is set up for exactly that, and Si View also runs a Tuesday play hour for grandparents and toddlers. The Bilingual Kaleidoscope Play & Learn group at the Newport Way Library is a weekly circle of caregivers and kids in English and Mandarin.</p>
