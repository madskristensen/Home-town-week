Navigation mockup

This is a preview of a new menu. Nothing on the live site links here, and the header on every other page is unchanged.

The wordmark is its own row. Cities, Seasons, Guides, and About sit in a bar under it, and each opens below that bar. A single Menu button is used only when those four labels do not fit. The footer repeats the same four groups.

Seasons lists Fall, Halloween, Christmas, Winter break, Spring break, Summer camps, and Worth the drive, and only when that hub exists. Halloween is the Halloween section of Fall. Winter break and Summer camps are left out until those hubs exist. Guides lists the articles in season today, then All articles, Playgrounds, School calendars, and bucket lists that are in season. Winter break remains an article under Guides when its dates include today.
