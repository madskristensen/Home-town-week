The dates below are the 2026 listings. A downtown walk or a library craft usually comes back the next October, but the day and the hours change. Read the organizer's page the week you go. Every Halloween and trunk-or-treat row in the city files is also on the [Fall hub](/fall/#halloween).

## Trunk-or-treats and community walks

The cards at the bottom are the free walks and trunk-or-treats that already have a photo of the place. A few more are posted and worth planning around.

[Little Monsters on Main Street](https://www.visitoldbellevue.com/events/little-monsters-on-main-street-2026) is Saturday, October 31, 1:00 to 3:00 p.m., on Main Street in Old Bellevue. The Old Bellevue Merchants Association says businesses, restaurants, and residential communities along Main Street hand out candy. It is free. The photo is Main Street in daylight.

[Pumpkins on the Plaza](https://www.sammamish.us/news/events/events/pumpkins-on-the-plaza/2026-pumpkins-on-the-plaza/) is Thursday, October 29, 4:00 to 7:00 p.m., on the upper plaza at Sammamish Commons. The city lists a scavenger hunt, an animal encounter, and trick-or-treat at city booths and trunks, with allergy-friendly candy at every other stop. The library hosts a craft inside. It is free. The photo is City Hall and the plaza at night, not the party.

[Trunk-or-Treat at City Hall](https://www.redmond.gov/2642/Focus---Fall-2026) is Friday, October 30, at Redmond City Hall, 15670 NE 85th Street. The city's fall newsletter lists trick-or-treating, games, and community fun, and it does not print a start time. Check the 2026 schedule. It is free. The photo is City Hall in daylight.

[Trick-or-Treat on Main Street](https://www.bothellwa.gov/1203/Trick-or-Treat-on-Main-Street) is Friday, October 30, 5:00 to 7:00 p.m., on Main Street and 101st Ave NE in downtown Bothell. The city moved it to that Friday because of I-405 work and feedback from Main Street businesses. It is free. The photo is Main Street in daylight.

[Trick-or-Treat Street](https://www.northbenddowntown.org/trick-or-treat-street) is Saturday, October 24, 3:00 to 5:00 p.m., on the downtown blocks in North Bend. The North Bend Downtown Foundation says the time can change, so check the page before you go. It is free. The same afternoon, [Great Si View Spooktacular](https://www.siviewpark.org/family-fun.phtml) at Si View Community Center, 400 SE Orchard Drive, runs 12:00 to 1:15 p.m. and 1:30 to 2:45 p.m. Si View calls the haunted house PG and aims it at preschool and elementary children with their families. Pick one session. Resident tickets are $18 and nonresident tickets are $21.60. Children under 2 are free and still need a registration. The downtown walk starts when the second session ends. The photo on the North Bend card is downtown with Mount Si, not the community center.

[Town Center Trick or Treat](https://www.mercerisland.gov/parksrec/page/town-center-trick-or-treat-2026) is Friday, October 30, 4:00 to 6:00 p.m., a free stroll through Town Center. Businesses with the two little ghost posters in the window are the ones handing out treats. The city says the business list keeps growing up to the day, so check the page again that week.

[Olde Fashioned Halloween Party](https://www.rentondowntown.com/events/halloween) is Saturday, October 24, 1:00 to 4:00 p.m. The Renton Downtown Partnership starts with trick-or-treating at downtown businesses, then moves the party to Legacy Square for makeup, photos, games, and prizes. At 3:00 p.m. the crowd learns the Thriller dance. The page does not list a ticket price.

[Spooktacular Halloween](https://www.snoqualmiewa.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=3412) is Saturday, October 24, 1:30 to 4:00 p.m. The city lists three stops. Begin on Snoqualmie Ridge at 35018 SE Ridge Street, walk to the trunk-or-treat in the Snoqualmie Valley YMCA parking lot (that stop runs until 3:00 p.m.), and continue into historic downtown. It is free.

[Trick or Tree](https://www.woodinville.gov/calendar.aspx?EID=765) is Thursday, October 29, 3:00 to 6:00 p.m., at Woodinville City Hall, 17301 133rd Ave NE. The city lists a treat bag, crafts, games, face painting, creepy crawlies, and a free tree to take home. It is free.

Three YMCA fall festivals share one page, and each asks adults not to wear masks and asks for family-friendly costumes. [Bellevue Family YMCA Fall Festival](https://www.seattleymca.org/programs/youth-family/family-activities-events/fall-festivals-activities) is Saturday, October 24, 6:00 to 8:00 p.m., at 14230 Bel-Red Road, with trunk-or-treat, a robotics station, and a pumpkin pool. Our calendar lists that one as indoors. Northshore Y Fall Family Festival is the same Saturday, 5:30 to 8:00 p.m., at 11811 NE 195th Street in Bothell, with carnival games, trunk-or-treat from decorated cars, and a bounce house. Sammamish YMCA Fall Family Festival is 4:00 to 7:00 p.m. at 831 228th Ave SE, with a haunted walk, a bounce house, and carnival games. All three are free.

[Village Halloween Celebration](https://thevillageattotemlake.com/events/village-halloween-celebration) is Saturday, October 24, 12:00 to 2:00 p.m., at The Village at Totem Lake, 11901 NE Village Plaza. Characters, a stage performance, and a craft. It is not a trick-or-treat, and it goes on rain or shine. Parking in the Green Garage is free.

[Trick or Treat at Marymoor Village](https://app.amilia.com/store/en/city-of-redmond/shop/activities/7084082) is Saturday, October 24, 4:30 to 7:30 p.m., indoors at Redmond Community Center at Marymoor Village, 6505 176th Ave NE. Room-to-room trick-or-treating and crafts. Free, all ages, and children under 12 come with an adult. Register ahead. The same building has a separate [Haunted House](https://app.amilia.com/store/en/city-of-redmond/shop/activities/7084090) from 5:00 to 9:00 p.m. that night. Ages 12 and older are recommended. Expect dim light, strobes, loud noise, fog, and stairs. Tickets are $5 to $6 at the door.

[Halloween Train](https://trainmuseum.org/upcoming-events/halloween/) boards at the Snoqualmie Depot and the North Bend Depot on the weekends of October 10 and 11, 17 and 18, 24 and 25, and October 31 through November 1. Costumes are welcome. Ages 2 and up need a ticket. North Bend departures start at 10:30 a.m. Snoqualmie departures start at 11:00 a.m. The trip is just under two hours, with a stop for cider, apple pressing, crafts, and music. Tickets are $16 to $32. The photo we use elsewhere for the depot is the Snoqualmie Depot in daylight, not the Halloween ride.

A few more community listings, each on the organizer's page. [Treats and Tails](https://issaquahhighlands.com/event/treats-tails-halloween-event-4/) is Saturday, October 17, 12:00 to 2:00 p.m., at Blakely Hall in Issaquah Highlands, for ages 3 to 10 with an adult. The standby line is free. [Zombie Walk](https://downtownissaquah.com/events/zombie-walk-17/) is Saturday, October 24, 1:30 to 5:30 p.m., from the Historic Shell Station on Front Street. It is free and open to all ages. Optional makeovers are sold separately. [Halloween Factoria](https://marketplaceatfactoria.shopkimco.com/events) is Saturday, October 31, 2:00 to 4:00 p.m., at The Marketplace at Factoria. [Spooky Family Fun](https://www.mcmenamins.com/events/278333-spooky-family-fun) is Saturday, October 31, 3:00 to 8:00 p.m., at Anderson School, 18607 Bothell Way NE. Games, kid-friendly movies, trick-or-treating on the property, and a kids costume contest at 6:00 p.m. Admission is free. Food and drink are separate. [Captain Jack's Treasure Run](https://www.orcarunning.com/race/captain-jacks-treasure-run/) on Sunday, October 25, starts the kids race at 9:45 a.m. on the Sammamish River Trail in Redmond. Trunk-or-treat begins at 10:15 a.m. The kids race is $10 for ages 12 and under. Cougar Mountain Zoo lists Howl-O-Ween for Saturday, October 31, and does not post a time. Check the 2026 schedule.

## Not too scary for little kids

[Pee Wee Monster Bash](https://secure.rec1.com/WA/city-of-kirkland/catalog/index?filter=c2VhcmNoPW1vbnN0ZXIlMjBiYXNoJnJlbnRhbCU1QmZyb20lNUQ9JnJlbnRhbCU1QnRvJTVEPSZkYWU2MGY5ZjQyMmY5MjczODdkZWRiNjU2ODM3Zjk2Mz1mOWE4NDdjY2JiNzNiMWExOGM4Y2NhNTc2ODc1MjczMA%3D%3D) is Friday, October 30, 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., at North Kirkland Community Center, 12421 103rd Ave NE. Carnival games, a cookie stroll, bowling, and skee ball for ages 1 to 5. Kirkland Parks calls it kooky, not scary. It is $18 per child, and it sells out.

[Monster Mash Halloween Bash](https://bellevuewa.gov/city-government/departments/parks/activity-guide) is Friday, October 30, 5:30 to 8:00 p.m., at Northwest Arts Center, 9825 NE 24th Street. A free indoor trick-or-treat, themed crafts, and the toddler play gym. No registration. The activity guide lists it for all ages.

[KidsQuest Halloween Spooktacular](https://www.bellevuedowntown.com/do/spooktacular) is Saturday, October 31, 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., at KidsQuest Children's Museum, 1116 108th Ave NE. This year the museum calls it a Haunted Hoedown. Costumes are encouraged. General admission is $16.75 for adults and children. Members are free. It is indoors, so it is the morning half of Halloween if you also want Main Street at 1:00 p.m.

[Trick or Treat with the Farm Animals](https://bellevuewa.gov/city-government/departments/parks/community-centers/kelsey-creek-farm/events/trick-treat-farm-animals) is Saturday, October 24, 1:00 to 3:00 p.m., at Kelsey Creek Farm, 410 130th Pl SE. Costumed animals, a bag to decorate, farm crafts, and pumpkin bowling. Most of it is outdoors. Pets are not allowed in the barnyard. Registration is $30 for Bellevue residents and $36 for everyone else.

[Haunted Forest Family Walk](https://hauntedforestofmaplevalley.org/ticket-info) is Sundays October 4, 11, 18, and 25, 12:00 to 3:00 p.m., at Royal Arch Park in Maple Valley. A daylight walk with no jump scares, and trick-or-treating while the candy lasts. Admission is $7. Ages 3 and under are free. The Friday and Saturday night show is a separate ticket.

[BOO at the Barn](https://www.issaquahwa.gov/calendar.aspx?EID=14732) at Pickering Barn splits the day. Saturday, October 24, 2:00 to 4:00 p.m. is carnival games and crafts. The 6:00 to 8:00 p.m. haunted hay barn and trail are not recommended for children under 6. Door admission is $15. Children 3 and younger are free.

[Sleepy Hollow](https://bellevuewa.gov/city-government/departments/parks/community-centers/bellevue-youth-theatre/bellevue-youth-theatre-productions) at Bellevue Youth Theatre is a family ghost story with music. Tickets are $15. The sensory-friendly performance, with brighter house lights and lower sound, is Saturday, October 31, at 2:00 p.m.

[Spooky Spectacular](https://www.redmond.gov/calendar.aspx?EID=3560) is Monday, October 26, 6:00 to 7:30 p.m., at Redmond Senior and Community Center. All ages. A photo booth, face painting, crafts, pumpkin decorating, and mini games. Bring your own pumpkin. There is no carving. It is free.

Libraries stay the calm option. [Pumpkin Painting](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6a63e186d23de82955c8fba5) is Tuesday, October 13, 5:30 to 6:30 p.m., at Mercer Island Library. Kids and teens, and ages 7 and under need an adult. [Sensory Dough: Monster Makers](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6aa0a2324cb69d003e26b5fc) is Monday, October 5, 4:15 to 5:30 p.m., at Kirkland Library, for not-so-scary monsters in a relaxed room. [Mini pumpkin decorating](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6aa324b6f9ed2e00384fb019) is Thursday, October 22, 4:15 to 5:30 p.m., at Carnation Library, for ages 9 and older. [Halloween Story Time](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6ab1c1ccab7a8e0037c37c20) is Thursday, October 29, 10:30 to 11:15 a.m., at Snoqualmie Library, stories and play for young children with an adult. [The Great Pumpkin Hunt](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6aa8a328ab7a8e0037c1d44f) is Saturday, October 31, 4:00 to 5:30 p.m., at Woodinville Library. All of those are free. [Halloween Costume Swap](https://www.kirklandwa.gov/costumeswap) is Saturday, October 10, 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., at Kirkland City Hall. Take home a clean costume. Donating one is welcome and not required.

## Rain, timing, and a teal pumpkin

If the afternoon is wet, stay with the listings that are already indoors. Marymoor Village trick-or-treat is room to room inside the community center. Monster Mash Halloween Bash is indoors at Northwest Arts Center, and the toddler play gym is open. KidsQuest is inside the museum all day on October 31. Renton's [Truck or Treat](https://www.rentonwa.gov/TruckorTreat) on Friday, October 30, has a session inside the community center and a session outside with the trucks. The sensory-friendly stroll is 5:00 to 6:00 p.m., with flashing lights, noise, and music kept down. The walk-through is 6:00 to 7:30 p.m. Registration is $15 for Renton residents and $18 for everyone else. The Great Pumpkin Hunt stays at Woodinville Library unless the weather is good enough to move part of it outside. Village Halloween at Totem Lake goes on rain or shine, which is not the same as indoors.

Saturday, October 24, is the crowded day: Totem Lake at noon, the farm at 1:00, Renton downtown at 1:00, Snoqualmie at 1:30, North Bend at 3:00, then the Y festivals and Marymoor in the evening. Thursday, October 29, is Sammamish Commons and Woodinville City Hall. Friday, October 30, stacks Mercer Island Town Center, Bothell Main Street, Redmond City Hall, the Bellevue arts center, and Renton Community Center. Saturday, October 31, is KidsQuest in the morning and Old Bellevue Main Street from 1:00 to 3:00 p.m. Redmond has not posted a start time for City Hall. North Bend says its downtown time can change.

A teal pumpkin on a porch means that house has non-food treats for kids with food allergies. Paint one, or buy one, if you are handing out candy at home. Sammamish already puts allergy-friendly candy at every other stop at Pumpkins on the Plaza. The Y festivals ask adults not to wear masks. After dark, a small light or something reflective on the costume helps drivers. Stay with the group the organizer named, and read that page the day you go.
