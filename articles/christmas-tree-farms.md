Once the Halloween costumes are put away, it is time to start thinking about the tree. Every farm below is checked on its own site. Most open the weekend before Thanksgiving or the day after, and the u-cut fields at the busy farms thin out fast over the first two weekends. Bring boots, a tarp for the car, and a rope or two. Most farms lend saws and will shake and bale the tree.

When a farm has not posted its 2026 dates yet, we say so and give last year's pattern instead. Check the farm's page before you drive out.

## Redmond

[McMurtrey Farm](https://www.mcmurtreyfarm.com/christmastrees) at 13925 Redmond-Woodinville Road NE opens for the season on Friday, November 27. Cutting your own tree takes a reservation, and the farm releases [u-cut reservations](https://www.mcmurtreyfarm.com/u-cutreservations) on Sunday, November 15, at 8:00 a.m. The farm says u-cut usually sells out the first and second weekend. Pre-cut trees need no reservation, and you can come between 10:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. The u-cut trees are mostly 5 to 7 feet. The pre-cut lot runs from small tabletop trees up to about 15 feet. Wagon rides, hot cocoa, and apple cider are free, and wreaths are in the selling shed. Saws are provided, baling is a small fee, and the crew can carry the tree to your car but cannot tie it down. Leashed dogs are welcome. The farm takes cash, Venmo, cards, and Apple Pay. Prices are not posted yet.

[Serres Farm](https://serresfarm.com/christmas-trees) at 20306 NE 50th Street is a small farm on the outskirts of Redmond. The farm says it opens for u-cut trees in December, and its site still shows last season as closed. The trees are grown there from seedlings, tagged with the price, and run 6 feet and up. Hand saws are there to borrow. Check the page for the 2026 opening day.

[Buttonwood Farm](https://www.buttonwoodfarm.com/) at 14500 NE 116th Street is a certified organic tree farm, open by appointment only. Last season it closed after December 23. You can cut your own or use the farm's white-glove delivery, priced at checkout. The farm grows Douglas, Turkish, Nordmann, Noble, and Grand firs and Blue Spruce, and it sells organic wreaths and tree stands. The farm says most trees are $120 to $200. Dogs are welcome on leash. Expect mud. The farm has not opened 2026 appointments yet. Its mailing list sends word when they do.

## Issaquah

[Trinity Tree Farm](https://www.trinitytreefarm.com/christmas-tree-farm) at 14237 228th Avenue SE, just off May Valley Road, opens Saturday, November 21, at 8:00 a.m. Black Friday and weekends run 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., weekdays 11:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and it is closed Thanksgiving Day. It stays open until the trees run out. No appointment is needed. Saws, loading help, and tie-down are free. U-cut trees run 4 to 10 feet, and fresh-cut trees go up to 12 feet. The farm lists u-cut and pre-cut trees from $85 to $195, with pink-tag trees from $10. You can pre-tag a tree on November 14 and 15, from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and come back to cut it by December 7. Santa photos are free, so bring your own camera: Saturday, November 28, 10:00 a.m. to noon, then Sunday, November 29, and Saturday and Sunday, December 5 and 6, 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. The Newfoundland Club of Seattle brings its tree-pulling dogs on Saturday, November 28, 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. There is a 1955 fire engine for photos, two fire pits, a log cabin gift shop open every day, and a cafe and espresso stand on Black Friday and weekends. Cash, Visa, and Mastercard.

[Enchanted Winds Tree Farm](https://www.enchantedwinds.net/) at 18021 Issaquah-Hobart Road SE, in the foothills south of Issaquah, opens Saturday, November 21. It is open Friday through Sunday, 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., and closes when the trees sell out, usually the second or third weekend after Thanksgiving. Greeters hand out saws and kneepads (adults only on the saws), and the crew shakes, bales, and ties the tree to your car for free. The red gift barn has ornaments, wreaths, free cookies, and hot cider. The farm's FAQ says Evergreen Corn Co. sells kettle corn, hot cocoa, coffee, and hot dogs on opening day and every weekend. The farm lists 2026 prices by the foot: $12 for Douglas fir, $14 for Grand fir, and $17 for Noble, Turkish, and Nordmann firs under 10 feet. Leashed dogs are welcome. Cash, checks, and cards.

## Carnation and Duvall

[Carnation Tree Farm](https://www.carnationtreefarm.com/) at 3851 Tolt Avenue is a historic family farm that some families have visited for generations. It offers u-cut Noble, Nordmann, Fraser, and Grand firs and Norway spruce, a lot of pre-cut Noble firs, wreaths, and garland. Its page lists daily hours of 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. but does not name a year, and 2026 dates are not posted yet. One of its tenants, [Zizumbo's Farm](https://zizumbosfarm.com/), grows organic u-cut trees on the same land. Last season Zizumbo's ran November 28 to December 23, 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., with free hot drinks. Leashed dogs are welcome at both.

[Novelty Hill Farm](https://noveltyhillfarm.com/christmas-season) at 26617 NE 124th Street sells pre-cut trees, not u-cut. It opens Friday, November 27, 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. After that it is open Saturday and Sunday 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. and Monday through Friday 3:00 to 6:00 p.m., until the trees are gone. The firs are all grown in Washington. Hot cider and cocoa are out while you look around the 1930s barns, and local makers sell at a holiday market on weekends. The farm's tree lighting and holiday sing-along is Saturday, November 28, 5:00 to 7:30 p.m., with a bonfire, s'mores, and Santa leading the countdown. The farm will post a ticket link closer to the date.

## Fall City

[Fall City Farms](https://fallcityfarms.com/christmas-trees/) at 3636 Neal Road has a 5-acre u-cut field next to its lodge, mostly Grand, Noble, and Nordmann firs, with some Douglas and Fraser. This year it is open one weekend only: Saturday and Sunday, December 5 and 6, 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., for u-cut and pre-cut trees and an indoor holiday market.

[Celebration Farms](https://celebrationfarmsfallcity.com/christmas-trees) at 31022 SE Redmond-Fall City Road runs a pre-cut tree lot and lists the season as November 21 through December 23. It is about 10 minutes from Issaquah. The farm lists Noble, mountain Noble, Fraser, Nordmann, and Douglas firs, tree shaking, and help tying the tree to your roof. There is hot cocoa, Santa photos, a wood-fired s'mores pit, and a farm shop with ornaments and gifts. They also rent live trees in pots for the season and pick them up after Christmas to grow another year. Daily hours are not posted yet.

## North Bend

[Keith and Scott Tree Farm](https://www.kandstreefarm.com/christmas-trees) opens its Thrasher Road field at 42999 SE 120th Street on Friday, November 27. After that it is open weekends only, 9:00 a.m. until dusk, until the trees sell out. The farm is a family ranch along the Middle Fork of the Snoqualmie, with horses and cattle in the fields and views of Mt. Si. The Thrasher Road field has all five of its firs, Noble, Douglas, Grand, Fraser, and Turkish, plus pre-cut trees and handmade wreaths. Saws, twine, shaking, baling, and help loading are free, and so is the hot spiced cider by the covered bonfire. The farm says prices start at $70 at Thrasher Road. It takes cash or check only. There is no Santa here, and quiet dogs on leash are welcome. The smaller Mt. Si Road field is marked closed for the season.

[Christmas Creek Tree Farm](https://www.christmascreektreefarm.com/) at 15515 468th Avenue SE is right off I-90 at exit 34. Look for the giant candy canes. It calls itself the largest u-cut farm in the Snoqualmie Valley. Its 2026 dates are not posted yet. Last season it had pre-season days the weekend before Thanksgiving, opened on Black Friday weekend, and wrapped up in mid-December. Santa Nights ran on three December evenings, with Santa photos, a nighttime hayride, cider and cookies, crafts, and a holiday movie by the fire. Last year it also had a ticketed Santa breakfast, flocking, wreaths and garland, free hot cider and coffee, fire pits, and heated restrooms. Prices started at $68 for a Douglas fir. Leashed dogs are welcome.

[Cedar Falls Tree Farm](https://cedarfallstreefarm.com/) at 15200 Cedar Falls Road SE, off I-90 exit 32, says it plans to open the day after Thanksgiving, Friday, November 27, with u-cut and pre-cut trees. Hours are Friday through Sunday, 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. The farm is a forest woodland with fish ponds, streams, and paths, so wear hiking boots. Every tree is marked with its price, height, and type. The farm provides saws, helpers, twine, and candy canes for everyone, plus hot cider and a warming fire with a view of the valley. It takes cards, cash, and checks.

## Maple Valley

[Wilson Hill Tree Farm](https://www.wilsonhilltreefarm.com/2026-choose-and-cut-season.html) at 20241 SE 232nd Street is open 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. on November 21 and 22, Thanksgiving weekend November 27 through 29, and December 5, 6, 12, and 13, or until it sells out. The farm grows hand-sheared Noble, Nordmann, Douglas, Grand, and Fraser firs. You can also come early to pick a tree on its [pre-select days](https://www.wilsonhilltreefarm.com/), October 24 and 25 and November 7 and 8, 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., and pick it up later. No appointment is needed. Wreaths are limited, so message the farm to hold one. The farm posts Santa's schedule on its Facebook page.

## A little farther

[Christmas at the Farm Dairyland](https://www.christmasatdairyland.com/) at 12125 Treosti Road in Snohomish is a holiday market and tree farm with free admission. The market opens November 14, pre-cut trees and the greenery barn open November 20, and the u-cut Douglas fir field opens Thanksgiving Day from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. After that, u-cut is weekends only. A u-cut tree is $79.99 plus tax for any size, and u-cut is cashless. Pre-cut trees are priced by the foot. Hours are 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Monday through Thursday, to 9:00 p.m. Friday and Saturday, and to 6:00 p.m. Sunday. Santa is there 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. on November 27 through 29 and the December weekends through the 19th and 20th. There is a fire for roasting marshmallows, plus food trucks, and dogs are welcome.

Honeytree Farm in Auburn and Pfaff's in Kent are on [Worth the drive](/worth-the-drive/#trees). Both open the day after Thanksgiving.

Tree lightings, Santa visits, and the rest of the season are on the [Christmas hub](/christmas/).
