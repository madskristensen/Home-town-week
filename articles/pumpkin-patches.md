Every farm below posted its 2026 hours on its own site, and most stay open through Saturday, October 31. Rain turns every field to mud, so pack boots. A few patches let you in free and charge only for the pumpkin. That is noted in each listing.

## Redmond, Woodinville, and Issaquah

[McMurtrey Farm](https://www.mcmurtreyfarm.com/pumpkin-patch) at 13925 Redmond-Woodinville Rd NE is open Wednesday through Sunday, 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Entry is $9 and covers wagon rides, the corn field hunt, duck races, and games. Barrel car rides are $6, and face painting runs Friday through Sunday at an extra charge. Leashed dogs are welcome. The farm has not posted a last day for the patch.

[The Pumpkin Farm and Puzzle Patch](https://www.jbfamilygrowers.com/) at JB Family Growers, 14063 NE 145th Street in Woodinville, is open Thursday 2:00 to 7:00 p.m. and Friday through Sunday 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. The final day is Saturday, October 31. It has a 10-acre corn maze with three trails, a 7-acre sunflower walk, and u-pick pumpkins, squash, and gourds. Admission tickets are sold through the farm's site, and pets are welcome on leash. Sunday, October 18, is the [Pup-kin Patch](/woodinville/#pup-kin-patch) dog day, included with regular admission. The farm says some GPS apps miss the entrance, so it posts coordinates on its home page.

[Fletcher Farm](https://www.fletcherfarmissaquah.com/pumpkin-patch) at 18712 SE May Valley Road in Issaquah is open Saturdays and Sundays, 11:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., through Halloween. Admission is $14 for adults and $12 for children. Children under 1 are free, and leashed dogs are free. It covers a scavenger hunt, the farm animals, a storybook trail, mini-golf, yard games, and a children's obstacle course. A bonfire runs when county fire rules allow. Pumpkins are priced separately, by the pound for the big ones.

## Carnation

[Remlinger Farms](https://remlingerfarms.com/fall-harvest-festival/) at 32610 NE 32nd Street runs its Fall Harvest Pumpkin Festival Friday through Sunday, 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., through Sunday, November 1. Last admission is 5:00 p.m., and it is closed Monday through Thursday. One wristband covers more than 70 rides and attractions, the corn maze, and Pumpkin Village. Pony rides are a $7 add-on. Halloween weekend, October 30 to November 1, adds a costume contest. The [u-pick pumpkin field](https://remlingerfarms.com/upick-pumpkin-patch/) is separate. It needs no ticket, parking is free, and pumpkins are priced by size. The farm lists it open through November 2.

[Jubilee Farm](https://www.jubileefarm.org/october) at 229 W Snoqualmie River Road NE holds a harvest festival on Saturdays and Sundays in October, 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Admission and parking are free. Tractor rides out to the patch are free, and a draft-horse carriage runs when the weather allows. Kids can help press cider and visit the pigs, goats, and chickens. Weekdays are a quieter u-pick patch. Pets stay home, because it is a working farm with animals.

[Oxtober](https://www.oxbow.org/visit/events/oxtober/) at Oxbow Farm and Conservation Center, 10819 Carnation-Duvall Road NE, is Saturday and Sunday, October 17 and 18, and again October 24 and 25, 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Tickets are $6 in advance and $7 at the door. Ages 0 to 3 are free. Expect organic pumpkins to buy, tractor hayrides, two apple slingshots, a hay play barn and corn crib, nature crafts, and scavenger hunts. Workshops are a $5 add-on.

## Duvall and Fall City

[Novelty Hill Farm](https://noveltyhillfarm.com/pumpkin-patch-%26-fall-fun) at 26617 NE 124th Street is open Saturday and Sunday, 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The self-serve patch is open Monday through Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Walking the patch and visiting the goats, pigs, and cows is free. The hay mow, corn maze, trike track, and corn crib are $5 for ages 5 and under and $10 for ages 6 and up. Infants are free. The maze is about 1.5 miles, short enough for younger kids. Leashed dogs are welcome.

[Muddy Boots Pumpkins](https://muddybootspumpkins.com/pumpkin-patch) at 15505 W Snoqualmie River Road NE is open every day through Saturday, October 31. Hours are Sunday through Thursday 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., and Friday and Saturday 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. for the night maze. The corn maze is free under age 5, $12 for ages 6 to 15, and $17 for ages 16 and up. It hides ten scarecrow scenes, and finding all ten earns a prize. Apple cannons run Friday afternoon through Sunday, at 5 apples for $5. Pumpkins are priced by size. After rain the maze gets very muddy.

[Fall City Farms](https://fallcityfarms.com/pumpkin-patch/) at 3636 Neal Road is open Friday and Saturday 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and Sunday 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., through October 31. Hot mini doughnuts, cider, and farm animals. Leave pets at home.

[Celebration Farms](https://celebrationfarmsfallcity.com/) at 31022 SE Redmond-Fall City Road is open Friday through Sunday, 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Admission and parking are free. A vintage tractor pulls you out to the patch. There are Valais Blacknose sheep to meet, pumpkin bowling and giant bubbles, and a farm shop with specialty pumpkins and apples. It is about 10 minutes from Issaquah.

The bigger farms up in Snohomish and down past Enumclaw are on [Worth the drive](/worth-the-drive/). Every dated pumpkin and corn maze listing also lands on the [Fall hub](/fall/#patches).

## Trick-or-treat by town

The free downtown walks and trunk-or-treats for 2026 are below. Each one links to its card on the city page. The [Halloween guide](/articles/halloween/) has the full details plus quieter plans for little kids.

- Bellevue: [Little Monsters on Main Street](/bellevue/#little-monsters-on-main-street), Saturday, October 31, 1:00 to 3:00 p.m. Also the [Bellevue Family YMCA Fall Festival](/bellevue/#bellevue-family-ymca-fall-festival) trunk-or-treat, Saturday, October 24, 6:00 to 8:00 p.m.
- Redmond: [Trick or Treat at Marymoor Village](/redmond/#trick-or-treat-at-marymoor-village), indoors, Saturday, October 24, 4:30 to 7:30 p.m., register ahead. [Trunk-or-Treat at City Hall](/redmond/#trunk-or-treat-at-city-hall), Friday, October 30, 5:00 to 7:30 p.m.
- Kirkland: [Treat Street at Kirkland Urban](/kirkland/#treat-street-at-kirkland-urban), Saturday, October 24, 5:00 to 8:00 p.m.
- Bothell: [Trick-or-Treat on Main Street](/bothell/#trick-or-treat-on-main-street), Friday, October 30, 5:00 to 7:00 p.m. The [Northshore Y Fall Family Festival](/bothell/#northshore-y-fall-family-festival) trunk-or-treat is Saturday, October 24, 5:30 to 8:00 p.m.
- Sammamish: [Pumpkins on the Plaza](/sammamish/#pumpkins-on-the-plaza), Thursday, October 29, 4:00 to 7:00 p.m., with allergy-friendly candy at every other stop.
- Issaquah: [Treats and Tails](/issaquah/#treats-and-tails-halloween-event) in Issaquah Highlands, Saturday, October 17, 12:00 to 2:00 p.m.
- Woodinville: [Trick or Tree](/woodinville/#trick-or-tree) at City Hall, Thursday, October 29, 3:00 to 6:00 p.m.
- Mercer Island: [Town Center Trick or Treat](/mercer-island/#town-center-trick-or-treat), Friday, October 30, 4:00 to 6:00 p.m.
- Snoqualmie: [Spooktacular Halloween](/snoqualmie/#spooktacular-halloween), Saturday, October 24, 1:30 to 4:00 p.m., from the Ridge to the YMCA trunk-or-treat and downtown.
- North Bend: [Trick-or-Treat Street](/north-bend/#trick-or-treat-street), Saturday, October 24, 3:00 to 5:00 p.m.
