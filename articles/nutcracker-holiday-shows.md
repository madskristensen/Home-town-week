For a first Nutcracker with a preschooler, the 11 a.m. one-hour show from Issaquah Dance Theatre is the shortest and easiest. For the full ballet, International Ballet Theatre in Bellevue runs the longest, from December 11 to 23. The [Christmas](/christmas/#holiday-show) hub keeps every dated show, and [Book ahead](/book-ahead/) has the ones that sell out.

### Issaquah

[Issaquah Dance Theatre's Nutcracker](https://www.issaquahdance.com/nutcracker) is Saturday, December 5, and Sunday, December 6, 2026, at Longman Performing Arts Center, Issaquah High School, 700 2nd Avenue SE. Each day has a one-hour show at 11 a.m. for a young audience, then full shows of about two hours at 1:30 and 5:30 p.m. The ticket price is the same for the short and the full show. Children under 2 on a lap do not need a ticket. Sales are final, and an exchange costs $15.

### Mercer Island

Youth Theatre Northwest plays [The Nutcracker and the Mouse King](https://youththeatre.org/auditions/) on Saturdays and Sundays at 2 p.m. from December 5 to 13, 2026. The cast is in grades 1 through 8. In this telling, a grown-up Clara tells her daughter how she met the Nutcracker. The same theater runs [It's a Wonderful Life as a radio play](https://youththeatre.org/auditions/) December 4 to 6, Friday and Saturday at 7 p.m. and Sunday at 4 p.m., with a cast in grades 5 through 12. That one suits older kids.

### Renton

[Scrooge!](https://www.rentoncivictheatre.org/shows/scrooge) runs December 4 to 20, 2026, at Renton Civic Theatre, 507 S Third Street. It is a musical A Christmas Carol told with hand-made puppets, and the theater pitches it for all ages. Monday, December 14 is a pay-what-you-can night.

### Bellevue

International Ballet Theatre dances [The Nutcracker](https://www.ibtbellevue.com/nutcracker-dec-2026) at Meydenbauer Theatre, 11100 NE 6th Street, from December 11 to 23, 2026. Reserved seats include fees: $45 for youth through 14, $55 for military and seniors 60 and older, and $70 for everyone else. Every person needs a ticket, and babies in arms are not admitted. The show runs 1 hour 50 minutes plus a 15-minute intermission. Fridays, December 11 and 18, are at 6 p.m. Saturdays, December 12 and 19, and Sunday, December 20, are at 1 and 5 p.m. Sunday, December 13, is at 1 p.m. only. Monday through Wednesday, December 21 to 23, are at 1 p.m.

### Kirkland

Studio East's youth cast, grades 3 through 12, sings [A Christmas Story](https://studio-east.org/auditions/) from December 11 to 20, 2026, at 10718 NE 68th Street. Studio East says the run may be extended.

Grand Kyiv Ballet brings [The Nutcracker: Symphony of Lights](https://www.kpcenter.org/event/the-nutcracker-symphony-of-lights/) to Kirkland Performance Center, 350 Kirkland Avenue, on December 17, 18, and 19, 2026, all at 7 p.m. The costumes light up. Youth tickets are $64 and adult tickets are $74. The late start suits school-age kids more than toddlers.
