These are places on the Eastside that publish their own party or rental page. Nothing here is a paid listing. Dollar amounts stay on the venue page, because they change, and they are not tags on these cards.

[KidsQuest Children's Museum](https://www.kidsquestmuseum.org/partiesandmuseumrentals/) at 1116 108th Ave NE hosts private parties, with a party room and time in the exhibits. The party page has the packages and how to book.

The [Bellevue Indoor Climbing Wall](https://bellevuewa.gov/city-government/departments/parks/community-centers/sbcc/climbing_wall) is at South Bellevue Community Center, 14509 SE Newport Way. The city lists a climbing party for ages 6 and up, with staff to belay and a party space. The wall page has the package.

[Elevated Sportz](https://www.elevatedsportz.com/partypackages) is a trampoline park at 18311 Bothell Everett Hwy in Bothell. Parties use the trampoline courts and a party room. The party page has what is included.

Park shelters are the outdoor option. [Bellevue](https://bellevuewa.gov/city-government/departments/parks/rentals/outdoor-rentals) rents small private sites for birthdays and picnics, including shelters, and a request needs to be in at least 10 days ahead. Lewis Creek Park is one of the parks with shelters. [Kirkland](https://www.kirklandwa.gov/Government/Departments/Parks-and-Community-Services/Park-Facility-Field-Rentals/Rent-an-Outdoor-Parks-Facility) rents picnic shelters, including at Juanita Beach Park, and asks for at least 7 days' notice. That same page lists Peter Kirk Pool as a facility you can reserve, with at least 14 days' notice. [Redmond](https://www.redmond.gov/188/Rentals) rents picnic shelters from April through September, including at Grass Lawn Park, from 7 to 90 days ahead. Fees and the list of sites are on each city's page.
