### ASL Story Time
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 11:15 a.m.–12:15 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kenmore Library, 6531 NE 181st Street</p>

### Family Story Time

### Dia de los Muertos
<p class="event-when">Sun Nov 1 · 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">The Hangar, Kenmore Town Square, 6728 NE 181st St</p>

The third annual celebration, on Sunday, November 1. Free, with an ofrenda, performances, crafts, and activities.

### Alice in Wonderland
<p class="event-when">Fri Nov 6 through Sat Nov 14</p>
<p class="event-place">Inglemoor High School Little Theatre, 15500 Simonds Road NE</p>
