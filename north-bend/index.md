### Shaun the Sheep: The Beast of Mossy Bottom
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 8 · 6:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">North Bend Theatre, 125 Bendigo Blvd N</p>

A PG animated film, about an hour and a half. On Halloween Eve at Mossy Bottom, the Farmer wrecks the pumpkin patch and Shaun's fix goes sideways when a beast shows up in the woods. Doors open 30 minutes before the show. Tickets are $8 to $10.

[Shaun the Sheep](https://tix.northbendtheatre.com/event/shaun-the-sheep-the-beast-of-mossy-bottom-thursday-oct-8th-6pm)

### Halloween Train
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 and Sun Oct 11, then weekends Oct 17–18, Oct 24–25, and Oct 31–Nov 1</p>
<p class="event-place">North Bend Depot, 205 E McClellan Street</p>

The Northwest Railway Museum boards this fall ride at the North Bend Depot, behind the Shell station, and also at the Snoqualmie Depot. The trip is just under two hours, with a stop for cider, apple pressing, crafts, and music. Costumes are welcome. Ages 2 and up need a ticket. North Bend departures start at 10:30 a.m. and leave every two hours. Arrive about 30 minutes early.

[Halloween Train](https://trainmuseum.org/upcoming-events/halloween/)

### Great Si View Spooktacular
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 24 · 12:00–1:15 p.m. and 1:30–2:45 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Si View Community Center, 400 SE Orchard Drive</p>

Si View Metro Parks brings back a haunted house marked PG, plus games, pumpkin decorating, a costume contest, and face painting. It is aimed at preschool and elementary children with their families. Pick one session. Resident tickets are $18 and nonresident tickets are $21.60. Children under 2 are free and still need a registration. The downtown trick-or-treat starts when the second session ends.

[Si View family events](https://www.siviewpark.org/family-fun.phtml)

### North Pole Limited Santa Train
<p class="event-when">Sat Nov 28 through Sun Dec 20</p>
<p class="event-place">North Bend Depot, 205 E McClellan St</p>

A Santa train for families from the North Bend Depot, $39 for ages 2 and up, and no ticket under 2. The trip is about two hours and includes the ride, the Train Shed, a visit with Santa in the Chapel Car, a small gift, cookies, and a hot drink. It runs Nov 28–29, Dec 5–7, Dec 12–13, and Dec 19–20. Buy tickets ahead. Trains run rain or shine, and the museum does not refund tickets unless the train does not run.

[Santa Train](https://trainmuseum.org/upcoming-events/santa/)
