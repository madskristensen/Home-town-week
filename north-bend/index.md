### Love Snoqualmie Valley Volunteer Day
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 9:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Several North Bend sites, including the Mt. Si Senior Center</p>

The city's community calendar lists this volunteer morning and names the Mt. Si Senior Center among the North Bend stops. It does not print an end time. LEGO Builders at the library is the same afternoon, 3:00 to 4:30 p.m., so the volunteer block is the morning half.

### Shaun the Sheep: The Beast of Mossy Bottom
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 8 · 6:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">North Bend Theatre, 125 Bendigo Blvd N</p>

A PG animated film, about an hour and a half. On Halloween Eve at Mossy Bottom, the Farmer wrecks the pumpkin patch and Shaun's fix goes sideways when a beast shows up in the woods. Doors open 30 minutes before the show. Tickets are $8 to $10.

[Shaun the Sheep](https://tix.northbendtheatre.com/event/shaun-the-sheep-the-beast-of-mossy-bottom-thursday-oct-8th-6pm)

### Halloween Train
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 and Sun Oct 11, then weekends Oct 17–18, Oct 24–25, and Oct 31–Nov 1</p>
<p class="event-place">North Bend Depot, 205 E McClellan Street</p>

The Northwest Railway Museum boards this fall ride at the North Bend Depot, behind the Shell station, and also at the Snoqualmie Depot. The trip is just under two hours, with a stop for cider, apple pressing, crafts, and music. Costumes are welcome. Ages 2 and up need a ticket. North Bend departures start at 10:30 a.m. and leave every two hours. Arrive about 30 minutes early.

[Halloween Train](https://trainmuseum.org/upcoming-events/halloween/)

### Oaktoberfest and Arbor Day planting
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Meadowbrook Farm Interpretive Center</p>

Oaktoberfest is free and independently organized, not a city festival. It includes children's activities, native plant sales, and booths about local trees. A tree tour starts at 1:00 p.m. At 10:00 a.m. Mayor Miller opens a separate Arbor Day planting on the same grounds. An Eagle Scout candidate leads the planting at 10:30. Children under 13 need an adult. Living Snoqualmie reported the plan on Sep 25 and said the information came from the city.

[Valley Record](https://livingsnoqualmie.com/arbor-day-planting-and-oaktoberfest-set-for-october-17-in-north-bend/)

### Chess Club
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 24 · 1:00–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">North Bend Library, 115 E 4th Street</p>

Casual chess with teacher Lance. All levels. Boards are provided, and the Friends of the library bring snacks. The Si View party and downtown trick-or-treat are the same afternoon, at other addresses.

[Chess Club](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6955b919491b809c6f104708)

### Great Si View Spooktacular
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 24 · 12:00–1:15 p.m. and 1:30–2:45 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Si View Community Center, 400 SE Orchard Drive</p>

Si View Metro Parks brings back a haunted house marked PG, plus games, pumpkin decorating, a costume contest, and face painting. It is aimed at preschool and elementary children with their families. Pick one session. Resident tickets are $18 and nonresident tickets are $21.60. Children under 2 are free and still need a registration. The downtown trick-or-treat starts when the second session ends.

[Si View family events](https://www.siviewpark.org/family-fun.phtml)

### Trick-or-Treat Street
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 24 · 3:00–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown North Bend</p>

The North Bend Downtown Foundation's free afternoon for costumes on the downtown blocks. The foundation's event list gives 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. and says the time can change, so check the page before you go.

[Trick-or-Treat Street](https://www.northbenddowntown.org/trick-or-treat-street)

### All Ages Hoedown
<p class="event-when">Fri Nov 6 · 6:30–8:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Si View Community Center, 400 SE Orchard Drive</p>

A family dance at Si View with instructor Amanda Johnson. The parks page describes it as western-themed dancing that works for beginners and for people who already know a few steps. All ages.

[Si View family events](https://www.siviewpark.org/family-fun.phtml)
