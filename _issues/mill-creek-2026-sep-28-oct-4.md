Mill Creek Library is at 15429 Bothell-Everett Hwy. Parking may be limited through November 2026 because of construction. Monday through Thursday the branch is open 10:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. Friday and Saturday it closes at 6:00 p.m. Sunday is 1:00–5:00 p.m.
