Tuesday is National Night Out in neighborhoods across town. Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday are still storytime at the library.

## Tuesday night

### Round Rock National Night Out
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 · 6:00–9:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Neighborhoods across Round Rock</p>

The city's National Night Out is block parties, cookouts, and visits from police and emergency crews. The city page says this year's night is Tuesday, Oct. 6. The button to register a neighborhood party closed Sep. 4, so a block that did not register then may be quiet. Ask a neighbor if your street is hosting one.

[City of Round Rock](https://www.roundrocktexas.gov/event/round-rock-national-night-out-2/)

## At the library

200 E Liberty Ave. Youth programs are on the 2nd floor.

### Family Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Oct 5 · 6:30–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Youth Program Space</p>

Books, songs, and rhymes. Best for 18 months to 6 years. ASL interpreting at this Monday session.

[Monday storytime](https://roundrocktexas.libcal.com/event/17124393)

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 11:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Youth Program Space</p>

Ages 3–6, with caregivers. Siblings outside that range can come. Stories, songs, movement.

[Preschool Storytime](https://roundrocktexas.libcal.com/event/17124420)

<p class="event-place">Youth Program Space</p>
