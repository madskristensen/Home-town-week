Taste of Fort Collins is Saturday and Sunday in Washington Park. Kids 12 and under are free. Tuesday's storytime is still cancelled.

## Weekend

### Taste of Fort Collins
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 12:00–9:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Washington Park, 301 Maple Street</p>

Food vendors, business booths, and a concert. The Saturday music list on the downtown page runs through Modest Mouse. Kids 12 and under are free. Adult day tickets are on the festival site. A clear bag is required. The grounds also take in Civic Center Park. Re-entry is allowed that day.

### Taste of Fort Collins
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 12:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Washington Park, 301 Maple Street</p>

Same festival, shorter Sunday. The downtown lineup ends with Lil Jon, so the evening is a louder concert than the afternoon food walk. A Sunday ticket is separate from Saturday unless you bought the both-days pass.

[Taste of Fort Collins](https://tasteoffortcollins.com/) · [Downtown listing](https://downtownfortcollins.com/do/taste-of-fort-collins)

Council Tree Library is at 2733 Council Tree Ave. The storytime phone is (970) 221-6740. Groups of 6 or more should call ahead for indoor programs.

## Tuesday

### Ages 4+ storytime, cancelled
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 10:00–10:30 a.m. and 11:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Storytime Room, Council Tree Library</p>

Both sessions are marked cancelled on the library calendar. The series continues on Tue Sep 29 and Tue Oct 6. Do not go Tuesday expecting that storytime.

[10:00 session, cancelled](https://poudrelibraries.librarycalendar.com/event/council-tree-ages-4-storytime-32375) · [11:00 session, cancelled](https://poudrelibraries.librarycalendar.com/event/council-tree-ages-4-storytime-32390)

## Saturday

### All Ages Storytime with ASL
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 10:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Council Tree Library</p>

Sep. 26 is the 4th Saturday. The storytimes page says ASL interpretation is offered on the 4th Saturday at Council Tree, with a few exceptions, and lists all-ages storytime at 10:00 a.m. The week calendar labels this one "Council Tree All Ages Storytime with ASL."

[Storytimes](https://poudrelibraries.org/storytime/) · [Week of Sep. 22](https://poudrelibraries.librarycalendar.com/events/week/2026/09/22)

The district's posted storytime breaks for 2026 include Aug. 17 through Sep. 7, which is already over, and Dec. 21 through Jan. 3.
