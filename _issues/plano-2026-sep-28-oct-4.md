Saturday afternoon at Schimelpfenig is a Hubble program for grades K to 5. Tickets are in person, 30 minutes before.

### Hubble: Space Telescope
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 3:00–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Schimelpfenig Library program room, 5024 Custer Road</p>

Grades K–5. Pictures from the Hubble telescope, and the mysteries they helped answer. Tickets are not online. Get them at the library starting 30 minutes before. Phone: 972-769-4200.
