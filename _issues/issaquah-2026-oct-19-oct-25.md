Saturday is the Fall Farm Fresh Market again, 9 to 2 at the Shell Station. Ragtime's Issaquah run is over. The apple food tour downtown is still on through the end of the month.

The regular fall Saturday, not the apple festival. That was Oct 17. This series runs through Dec 5, 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m., with live music 11:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. Ayala Farms is the farm named on the Visit Issaquah listing.

[Fall market](https://www.visitissaquahwa.com/event/fall-farm-fresh-market-2/2026-10-10/)

The Downtown Issaquah Association's apple food tour, tied to Issaquah Goes Apples, is posted through Oct 31. That is restaurant specials, not a second festival. Check each kitchen. The association said menus can change.

## What left town

Ragtime's last Issaquah day was Oct 18. Opening night in Everett is Sat Oct 24 at 7:30 p.m. at the Everett Performing Arts Center, 2710 Wetmore Ave. That is the same Saturday as this market, in another city, and it is still not a preschool show.

The Issaquah library branch page did not list a kids' program for this week. Front Street after the tents is the walk.
