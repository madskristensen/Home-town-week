Wednesday is sensory storytime, and registration is required. Saturday is a baby and toddler gear swap. Drop-off is Friday.

Baby storytime is still Tuesday. Toddler storytime is still Thursday. Preschool storytime returns Friday, Oct. 2, on the newsletter's Friday list. LEGO club (From Books to Bricks, ages 6–12) is Tuesday at 4:00 p.m.

[Autumn children's programs](https://cdalibrarykids.substack.com/p/autumn-childrens-programming-at-the)

## Wednesday

### Sensory Storytime
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 10:00–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Story Room (Room 103), Coeur d'Alene Public Library</p>

Ages 3–8, and caregivers. The same listing also names Oct. 28 and Nov. 25. Registration is required. Space is about 10. Low light, a visual schedule, announced transitions, headphones, and weighted stuffed animals. Children can stand, move, arrive late, or step out. The Oct. 28 page is the registration link the library posted for the series.
