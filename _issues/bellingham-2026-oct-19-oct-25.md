Friday morning is the registered Baby Time at the Central Library. The Mount Baker Theatre has Herb Alpert, Ingrid Michaelson, and a Twin Peaks conversation on three different nights. Saturday is still the market.

## The library week

### Little Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Oct 19 and Tue Oct 20 · 10:05, 10:35, and 11:05 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library, 210 Central Ave</p>

Birth to age 3 with an adult. Mondays in this series run through Nov 16. Tuesdays run through Nov 17. The storytimes page lists the third sitting at 11:05 a.m. A September programs note said 11:35 a.m.

### Library Explorers
<p class="event-when">Mon Oct 19 · 3:30–4:15 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lecture Room, Central Library</p>

Ages 6–10, families welcome.

### Bellis Fair
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 21</p>
<p class="event-place">Multipurpose Room, Bellis Fair branch</p>

Little Storytime at 10:05, 10:45, and 11:25 a.m. Preschool Storytime at 12:05–12:35 p.m. Registered Baby Time at 2:30–3:30 p.m. The next skip on the library note is Nov 11, not this week. Use the Target or food-court entrance before 11:00 a.m.

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 22 · 10:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library</p>

Ages 3–5 with an adult. LibCal lists 10:30–11:00 a.m. The overview page lists 10:30–11:30 a.m.

[Preschool Storytime](https://bellinghampubliclibrary.libcal.com/event/17269877)

### Baby Time
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 23 · 10:30–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library</p>

Last Friday but one in the registered series. The storytimes page ends the Central Library class on Oct 30.

[Storytimes](https://bellinghampubliclibrary.org/storytimes)

## Three theatre dates

The season index lists these, without start times on that page:

- Thu Oct 22, Herb Alpert & The Tijuana Brass
- Sat Oct 24, Ingrid Michaelson
- Sun Oct 25, Twin Peaks: A Conversation with the Stars

All three are at the Mount Baker Theatre, 104 N Commercial St. Ticket times are on the individual event pages, not in the season headline. These are evening shows for grownups and older kids who already know the act. They are not a substitute for Thursday morning storytime.

[What's on](https://www.mountbakertheatre.com/events-tickets/)
