Monday night is the market at Storey Park. Saturday morning is the Main Street Market, and Saturday stays open downtown for Oktoberfest.

## Monday night

### Meridian Monday Night Market
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 4:00–9:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Storey Park, 205 E Franklin Road</p>

Free. The chamber page for this Monday says the market runs every Monday, June 1 through Oct. 5, except Labor Day. Playground and picnic shelters are on site.

[This Monday](https://business.meridianchamber.org/events/details/meridian-monday-night-market-09-28-2026-16635)

## Saturday

### Meridian Oktoberfest
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown Meridian, Idaho Avenue and 2nd Street</p>

The chamber's family block is 10:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. at Generations Plaza, with crafts from the Children's Museum of Idaho. A youth strudel contest for ages 8–14 is at 11:00 a.m. on the main stage. Boise Edelweiss plays German band music from noon to 3:45 p.m. Beer service is 21+ with an ID and a wristband. No cover for the music.

[Meridian Oktoberfest](https://meridianchamber.org/meridian-oktoberfest/)

The Main Street Market is the same morning, a short walk away, and it closes at 1:30 p.m.

### Meridian Main Street Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 9:00 a.m.–1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">33 E Broadway Avenue</p>

The season runs through Oct. 17. Live music is part of the regular Saturday, not a separate ticket.

[Main Street Market](https://meridianmainstreetmarket.com/home)
