Saturday afternoon is a Taylor Swift tribute matinee. The city's writers' conference fills the Frances Anderson Center the same weekend.

## For kids and families

### Swiftified: A Taylor Swift Tribute
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Center for the Arts, 410 Fourth Ave N</p>

ECA labels this a family matinee. Explore Edmonds describes Emily McVicker with a live band, sing-along moments, and an invitation to dress in a favorite era. An end time is not posted. The box office is (425) 275-9595.

The 2:00 p.m. slot is the same hour as an added Driftwood matinee. Pick one. Driftwood still has an 8:00 p.m. show that night.

[Swiftified](https://www.edmondscenterforthearts.org/events/swiftified-2026/)

### Edmonds Museum Summer Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">118 5th Ave N</p>

Still in the May-through-October Saturday season. About 90 family-owned vendors. The market is over by the time Swiftified starts, so a morning at the stalls still leaves the matinee.

Artisan Alley does not return this Saturday. The next indoor date on the sponsor note is Sat Oct 10.

[Museum market](https://historicedmonds.org/edmonds-museum-summer-market/)

### Library storytimes and Game On!
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 through Thu Oct 1</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Library, 650 Main St</p>

Sno-Isle lists these on the Edmonds branch calendar:

- Baby Storytime with Mady, Tue Sep 29, 9:00–9:45 a.m. and 10:00–10:45 a.m. The 10:00 a.m. page says stories, songs, rhymes, and a play or art time after.
- Game On!, Tue Sep 29, 4:00–5:30 p.m. The listing does not describe the program. A May 26 Game On! at this library, same hours, was Nintendo Switch games and snacks. Do not assume this date is the same until you check the branch page.
- Online Family Pajama Storytime with Ariana, Tue Sep 29, 7:00–7:30 p.m.
- Preschool Storytime, Wed Sep 30, 10:00 a.m.–11:00 a.m.
- Toddler Storytime with Cami, Thu Oct 1, 9:00–9:45 a.m.

Game On! overlaps Orange Shirt Day at ECA, which is also Tue Sep 29 at 4:00 p.m.

[Edmonds Library](https://www.sno-isle.org/locations/edmonds/) · [Baby Storytime](https://sno-isle.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6a347b55154fb14b00225916)

## Writers' weekend

### Write on the Sound
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2–Sun Oct 4</p>
<p class="event-place">Frances Anderson Center</p>

The City of Edmonds Arts Commission has run this small writers' conference since 1985. Sessions, workshops, and panels on the craft, plus a writing contest and manuscript critiques. The in-person days are Oct 2, 3, and 4. An online pre-conference was Sat Sep 26.

Daily start times are on the conference schedule, not in the short city blurb. Register on the city page. Phone (425) 771-0228.

[Write on the Sound](https://edmondswa.gov/government/departments/cultural_services/write_on_the_sound)

## Stage and concerts

### A Murder Is Announced
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1, Fri Oct 2, and Sat Oct 3 · 8:00 p.m.; Sat Oct 3 and Sun Oct 4 · 2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Wade James Theatre, 950 Main St</p>

The season opener continues through Oct 11. Regular shows are Thursday through Saturday at 8:00 p.m. and Sunday at 2:00 p.m. Driftwood added a 2:00 p.m. matinee on Sat Oct 3. About 2 hours with intermission. Mild adult themes, best for teens and up. Tickets $31 or $28, plus a $3 fee. TeenTix is $5.

[Driftwood Players](https://edmondsdriftwoodplayers.org/shows-tickets/)

### Orange Shirt Day
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 · 4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Center for the Arts, 410 Fourth Ave N</p>

ECA files this under Indigenous programming. The event page does not describe the program past the title and the 4:00 p.m. start. It is the same hour as Game On! at the library.

[Orange Shirt Day](https://www.edmondscenterforthearts.org/events/orange-shirt-day/)

### Roger McGuinn
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Center for the Arts, 410 Fourth Ave N</p>

A one-man set from the Byrds guitarist: 12-string, hits including "Mr. Tambourine Man" and "Turn! Turn! Turn!", and stories. TeenTix lists the date. Adult concert, and teens can use TeenTix.

[TeenTix listing](https://www.teentix.org/calendar/event/roger-mcguinn/)

### Kyle MacLachlan
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1 · 7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Center for the Arts, 410 Fourth Ave N</p>

Seattle Arts & Lectures presents a conversation about his memoir. The ECA note names Twin Peaks, Blue Velvet, Sex and the City, and Fallout. This is an evening talk, not a family show.

[Kyle MacLachlan](https://www.edmondscenterforthearts.org/events/kyle-maclachlan/)
