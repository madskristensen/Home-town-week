Wednesday is storytime at Island Books. Sunday is the farmers market, with one Sunday left after this one. Library storytime for Oct 4 was not on the September list.

## Wednesday

### Storytime for Kids
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Island Books, 3014 78th Ave SE</p>

Picture books in the children's section. ParentMap lists this date as open to all ages and ideal for 3–5. Free. The store is open at 9:00 a.m.

Saturday, Oct 3, is the day Nancy Stewart often sings at 9:30 a.m. The store's September calendar stops at Sep 26 and says to check for off weeks. Look at the October page before you promise a 1-to-4-year-old that Saturday slot.

[Island Books](https://islandbooks.com/events/2026/09) · [Sep 30 listing](https://www.parentmap.com/calendar/story-time-kids/2026-09-30/)

## Sunday market

### Mercer Island Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">7700 SE 32nd St</p>

Not the last Sunday. The city lists the season through Oct 11, so next Sunday is the closer. Same hours, 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.

Family storytime at the library was on the calendar for Sep 20 and Sep 27 at 11:15 a.m. Oct 4 was not on that grid. Do not plan the library at 11:15 unless the branch page shows it that morning. The market does not depend on it.

[City page](https://www.mercerisland.gov/community/page/mercer-island-farmers-market-0) · [Mercer Island Library](https://kcls.org/locations/mercer-island/)
