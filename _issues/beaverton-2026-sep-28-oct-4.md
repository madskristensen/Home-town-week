Monday is Spanish storytime, then Japanese. Saturday is Tiny Tales before the market. Sunday is Korean storytime.

The Main library is at 12375 SW 5th Street. The weekly age storytimes (baby, toddler, and preschool) keep the same clocks as last week. This issue is the sessions that do not happen every week.

## Monday at Main

### Spanish Storytime / Hora de cuentos en español
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 10:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main storytime room</p>

Ages 0–6. Mondays from Sep. 14 through Dec. 7, except Sep. 7.

### Japanese Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 11:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main storytime room</p>

Ages 6 and under with family. The select Mondays include Sep. 28 and Oct. 12.

[Storytimes](https://www.beavertonlibrary.org/381/Storytimes)

## Saturday

### Tiny Tales Together
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

Birth to 24 months. The select Saturdays are Sep. 5, Oct. 3, Nov. 7, and Dec. 5.

### Family Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

All ages.

### Beaverton Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 8:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">SW Hall Blvd between 3rd and 5th</p>

The fall hours run through Nov. 21. The market is next to the Main library.

[Beaverton Farmers Market](https://www.beavertonfarmersmarket.com/)

## Sunday

### Korean Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main storytime room</p>

Ages 6 and under with family. The select Sundays are Sep. 6, Oct. 4, Nov. 1, and Dec. 6.
