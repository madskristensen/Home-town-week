Monday is Spanish storytime, then Japanese. Tuesday night is Fiesta on First. Saturday the market has Richie Bean, and Tiny Tales is at the library before that.

The book and media sale at Main, 12375 SW 5th Street, is still going. It opened Thursday, Sep. 24, and closes Sunday, Oct. 4.

## Monday at Main

### Spanish Storytime / Hora de cuentos en español
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 10:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main storytime room</p>

Ages 0–6. Mondays from Sep. 14 through Dec. 7, except Sep. 7.

### Japanese Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 11:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main storytime room</p>

Ages 6 and under with family. The select Mondays include Sep. 28 and Oct. 12.

[Storytimes](https://www.beavertonlibrary.org/381/Storytimes)

## Tuesday night

### Fiesta on First
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 · 6:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">1st Street Dining Commons</p>

The Downtown Association's monthly Latino market: live music, artisan vendors, food, and family activities. It is the last Tuesday of the month. Admission is free.

[Fiesta on First](https://downtownbeaverton.org/event/fiesta-on-first-street-monthly-latino-market-community-event-in-downtown-beaverton/)

## Friday at Murray Scholls

### Friday Art Fun
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 4:00–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Murray Scholls, 11200 SW Murray Scholls Place</p>

Ages 2–8 with family. Same Friday art hour as last week, through Oct. 30.

[Youth flyer](https://www.beavertonlibrary.org/DocumentCenter/View/13360/2026-09-10-Sept-Oct-ADA)

## Saturday

### Beaverton Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 8:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">SW Hall Blvd between 3rd and 5th</p>

Richie Bean and the Midnight Travelers play soul, blues, and swing. The market's music list names this Saturday, then says it usually skips music later in October. Hours run through Nov. 21.

[Market music calendar](https://www.beavertonfarmersmarket.com/event-calendar)

### Tiny Tales Together
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

Birth to 24 months. The select Saturdays are Sep. 5, Oct. 3, Nov. 7, and Dec. 5.

### Family Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

All ages.

### Chess and backgammon
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 3:00–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

Grades 6–12. A relaxed games hour. The flyer also lists Oct. 17 and Oct. 31.

### Wendy Wagner, for teens who write
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

Grades 6–12. Young Willamette Writers hosts horror author Wendy Wagner. Registration is required, and a Zoom seat is listed too. This overlaps Tiny Tales, so a family with a teen and a baby would split up.

[Youth flyer](https://www.beavertonlibrary.org/DocumentCenter/View/13360/2026-09-10-Sept-Oct-ADA)

## Sunday

### Korean Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main storytime room</p>

Ages 6 and under with family. The select Sundays are Sep. 6, Oct. 4, Nov. 1, and Dec. 6. The book sale ends this afternoon.
