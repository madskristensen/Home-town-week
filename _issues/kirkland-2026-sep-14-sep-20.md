A kids' business fair and a mini parade at City Hall on Saturday, with waterfront markets on Wednesday and Friday.

## Saturday at City Hall

### City Hall for All
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 19 · 12:00–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kirkland City Hall, 123 5th Ave</p>

Free family afternoon for Welcoming Week. The city schedule, all on site:

- 12:00 p.m., jumbotron by the south entrance: Welcoming Week and Hispanic Heritage Month proclamations.
- 1:00 p.m., Council Chambers: story time with Mayor Kelli Curtis and the Library Board, plus a free book swap.
- 2:00 p.m.: Mini Belonging Parade around City Hall, led by the Lake Washington High School Marching Band, V amolá, and Brazilian Swag. No registration. Wagons and strollers are welcome.
- 3:00 p.m., Council Chambers: Ella Bella Bee & the Pollinators, a 45-minute kids' musical about bees, bats, and hummingbirds, with puppets. 4Culture sponsors the entertainment.

Young entrepreneurs ages 5–12 sell from their own booths in the south parking lot. Registration for those booths is closed. Inside, the Young Palette show hangs work from local students in grades K–12, and Seattle Reconomy runs a fixer fair for clothes and small appliances.

Food vendors on the city's list include Cocinas, Conchas de La Abuela, Dippy's Ice Cream, Green Steamer, J&Y Mobile Creperie, Magic Maggie's Treats, and Saap Saap Mochi. Truck-a-Palooza lines up fire, parks, police, and public works vehicles on 5th Avenue, plus a Metro coach and a Lake Washington School District bus.

Bike valet is free. The 255 stops at 4th Ave. Kirkland Urban parking at 425 Urban Plaza can be validated, then it is a short walk. ADA parking is reserved along 1st St. A Bloodworks Northwest bloodmobile is on 5th Avenue. Appointments are recommended, photo ID is required, and visitors under 16 are asked to stay out of the bloodmobile.  
[City Hall for All](https://www.kirklandwa.gov/Government/City-Managers-Office/City-Hall-for-All)

### Kirkland Health & Wellness Fair
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 19 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Juanita High School, 10601 NE 132nd St</p>

Free fair in the morning, before City Hall. The noon-to-2:00 p.m. window overlaps the start of City Hall for All, so pick one or split the family.  
[UW Medicine listing](https://equity.uwmedicine.org/event/2026-kirkland-health-wellness-fair/)

<p class="event-place">Marina Park, 25 Lakeshore Plaza</p>
