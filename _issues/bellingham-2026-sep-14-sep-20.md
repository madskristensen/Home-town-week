Thursday morning is a librarian-led StoryWalk at Boulevard Park. Friday is Pokémon Club at the Central Library. Saturday is the farmers market at Depot Market Square.

## Thursday on the water

### Water Week StoryWalk
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 17 · 10:30–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Boulevard Park, meet at the play structure</p>

{% include event-photo.html
   src="/assets/images/bellingham/boulevard-park.webp"
   alt="The shoreline and lawn at Boulevard Park in Bellingham, with the bay in the background."
   credit="Photo: Fernando Gonzalez, CC BY-SA 4.0"
   source="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bellingham,_Washington_Boulevard_Park.jpg" %}

For ages 3–8. A librarian reads the StoryWalk along Taylor Dock, with songs and nature crafts. The library and City of Bellingham Public Works are doing this together for Whatcom Water Week, which runs Sep 12–20. Free.

The same Thursday, the Central Library lists Growing Community Through Food at 4:00–5:45 p.m. in the Lecture Room, doors at 3:30. Short films, bites from local chefs, and conversation. Free and open to all. It is not a kids' storytime. If the morning StoryWalk is the family plan, this is the optional early evening.

[September library programs](https://bellinghampubliclibrary.org/news/september-2026-bellingham-public-library-programs-and-events)

## Friday at the library

### Pokémon Club
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 18 · 2:00–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lecture Room, Central Library, 210 Central Ave</p>

Ages 7–11. Crafts, games, and a meet-up. All experience levels. Free, like the rest of the library's September list.

Hispanic Heritage Month displays, Sep 15 through Oct 15, are up at every branch. That is a shelf, not a ticketed program.

[September library programs](https://bellinghampubliclibrary.org/news/september-2026-bellingham-public-library-programs-and-events)

## Saturday market

### Bellingham Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 19 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Depot Market Square, 1100 Railroad Ave</p>

Every Saturday through December 19, 2026, on the tourism calendar. Produce, crafts, and ready-to-eat food. The Wednesday waterfront market was a summer series and is not on the fall calendar.

[Bellingham events](https://www.bellingham.org/events)

Little Storytime and Library Explorers start the following Monday, Sep 21. The Friends book sale is Thu Sep 24 through Sat Sep 26 at the Central Library.
