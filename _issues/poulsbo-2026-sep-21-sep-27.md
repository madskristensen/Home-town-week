Saturday is the farmers market. Kids Club is the first Saturday of the month, so it is next week, not this one. Library storytime starts Tuesday, Sep. 29.

## Saturday market

### Poulsbo Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">18901 8th Ave NE</p>

The market lists Saturdays from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., Mar. 28 through Dec. 12, 2026. Live music is posted for April through October. September 26 is a market Saturday.

Kids Club is free, on the first Saturday from April through October, sponsored by Town & Country. The September date was Sep. 5. The last date of the year is Oct. 3. No separate clock is posted inside market hours, so Kids Club sits inside the 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. market when it happens. It does not happen this Saturday.

[Poulsbo Farmers Market](https://www.poulsbofarmersmarket.org/) · [Kids Club](https://www.poulsbofarmersmarket.org/kidsclub)

## At the library

Poulsbo Library is at 700 NE Lincoln Road. Preschool storytime on the calendar is Tuesday, Sep. 29, at 10:30 a.m. Baby storytime that day is 1:00 p.m. Neither falls inside Sept. 21–27. Do not plan a Tuesday storytime this week.

Library hours are not copied here.
