Saturday morning is family storytime at Smoky Hill. Registration is open, and drop-ins can stay if there is room.

Smoky Hill Library is at 5430 S Biscay Circle. The event page says the building is open 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. that Saturday.

### Family Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Smoky Hill Library</p>

Short books, songs, and rhymes. Planned for ages 0–5. Register each child and each adult. Sensory support kits are available in the library for children who are anxious or overwhelmed. Registration closes at 10:30 a.m. that morning.
