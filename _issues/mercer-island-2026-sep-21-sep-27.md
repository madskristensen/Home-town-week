Wednesday and Saturday are at Island Books. Sunday is the farmers market again, with family storytime at 11:15 while the stalls are open.

## Island Books

### Storytime for Kids
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 23 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Island Books, 3014 78th Ave SE</p>

Picture books with a bookseller. Best for about ages 3–5. Free. The store opens at 9:00 a.m.

### Nancy Stewart
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 9:30–10:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Island Books, 3014 78th Ave SE</p>

Singing and stories for about ages 1–4. Sep 26 is on the store's September calendar. The next Saturday, Oct 3, is not on that September page. Check the October calendar before you count on it.

[September events](https://islandbooks.com/events/2026/09)

## Sunday market

### Mercer Island Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">7700 SE 32nd St</p>

The season runs through Oct 11, so two Sundays remain after this one, including next week. Storytime at 11:15 overlaps the market. Do the bookstore on Saturday, the library at 11:15 on Sunday, and the stalls on either side of that hour.

[Market](https://www.mifarmersmarket.org/)
