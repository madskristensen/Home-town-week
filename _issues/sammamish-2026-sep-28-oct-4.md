Monday is Mandarin storytime at the library. Wednesday afternoon is Reading Buddies. Thursday is a graphic novel club for ages 11 to 14, and Saturday is a codebreaking workshop. The Wednesday market does not come back this fall.

## At the library

### Mandarin Story Time
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 11:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Sammamish Library, 825 228th Ave SE</p>

On the branch calendar as a family storytime in Mandarin. Monday hours are 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. The short listing does not add a registration line. Check the event page if the room has been capping.

### Reading Buddies
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 4:00–6:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Sammamish Library, 825 228th Ave SE</p>

A two-hour reading block on the branch list. Wednesday the library is open noon–8:00 p.m. The same afternoon also lists Talk Time at 1:00, an English class at 3:00, and Tech Help at 4:00. Reading Buddies overlaps Tech Help. They are different rooms if the branch is running both. Read the event note for the grade range. It was not in the one-line calendar blurb.

[Sammamish Library](https://kcls.org/locations/sammamish/)

## Thursday and Saturday

### Middle School and Tween Graphic Novel Club
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1 · 4:00–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Sammamish Library, 825 228th Ave SE</p>

Ages 11 to 14. This month's book is The Circuit, the graphic novel by Francisco Jiménez. Stay after the talk and draw your own. The library provides drawing and writing materials while they last. Registration is not required.

Tutors, drop-in homework help for grades K–12, runs the same afternoon from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. The club and the tutors share the 4:00 hour. They are different stops in the building. A sibling can use the tutors while someone else is in the club.

[Graphic Novel Club](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6945dbef4be11d3d00d90b6c) · [Tutors](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6aa06462d94765189bb9fb1a)

### STEM Saturday: Codebreaking with Mathematics
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 2:00–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Sammamish Library, 825 228th Ave SE</p>

The library marks this workshop as a date change, so use the event page if the room moved again. Teen presenters introduce cryptography for kids and tweens: Caesar and Vigenère ciphers, and the math underneath them.

[STEM Saturday](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a76b561e30fe4845967de60)

## Not the market

Wednesday, Sep 30, is not a farmers market. The market site's last posted 2026 hours are September 2 and September 9, 4:00–7:00 p.m., and the next season line is May 5, 2027. The Commons, 801 228th Ave SE, is next door to the library. You can be on that lawn. You should not expect vendors.

[Market day](https://www.sammamishfarmersmarket.org/market-day/)
