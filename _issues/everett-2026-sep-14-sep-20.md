Thursday is pay-what-you-can night at Imagine Children's Museum. Sunday morning is Sensory Time for families who need a quieter floor, and the farmers market opens downtown at 10:30.

## Thursday at the museum

### Community Access Night
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 17 · 3:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Imagine Children's Museum, 1502 Wall St</p>

Families with children 12 and under. Pay what you can. Donations support nights like this one. The exhibits are the usual floor: hands-on rooms built for about ages 1–12. Everett Tourism lists this date.

The museum's Neighborhood Harvest Party was Saturday, Sep 12, the weekend before this issue. It is not on this week's calendar.

[Everett events](https://www.visiteverett.com/9/Events) · [Imagine Children's Museum](https://www.imaginecm.org/)

## Sunday, two clocks

### Sensory Time
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 20 · 9:00–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Imagine Children's Museum, 1502 Wall St</p>

The third Sunday of the month. Exclusive use of the north side of the museum for families with children on the autism spectrum, children with sensory processing disorders, and children with other special needs. Social stories, sound-reducing earmuffs, sunglasses, and sensory balls are available. Free. Registration required. Exhibits are aimed at ages 1–12. Questions go to Reservations@ImagineCM.org.

[Sensory Time](https://www.imaginecm.org/inclusion)

### Everett Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 20 · 10:30 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Wetmore Ave between Hewitt and Pacific. Landmark: 2930 Wetmore Ave</p>

The 33rd season, Sundays from Mother's Day weekend through the end of October. Farmers, producers, and handmade goods. Sensory Time and the market overlap from 10:30 to 11:00. The museum is on Wall Street, a few blocks from Wetmore, so a family that needs the quiet hour should finish there before the stalls. A family that wants both can leave the museum at 11:00 and still have the market.

[Everett Farmers Market](https://everettfarmersmarket.com/) · [Visit Everett](https://www.visiteverett.com/9/Events)
