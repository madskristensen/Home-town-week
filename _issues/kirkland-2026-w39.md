Free dance and music you can take kids to, both waterfront markets, and a fringe weekend spread across Kirkland.

## Umbrella Fringe

### Umbrella Fringe Festival
<p class="event-when">Fri–Sun Sep 25–27</p>
<p class="event-place">Kirkland Arts Center and partner sites</p>

Kirkland Arts Center's festival of short shows. Many are free. Some bills are adult, experimental, or horror. These are the family-leaning picks from the posted schedule.  
[Festival schedule](https://kirklandartscenter.org/kirkland-fringe-festival/)

### Step. Feel. Tell.
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 12:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kirkland Woman's Club</p>

Kuchipudi, classical Indian dance told in three parts: rhythm, expression, then both together.

### Potluck of Improv Fun
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kirkland Woman's Club</p>

Short-form improv. The schedule calls it fast, fun, and family friendly.

### Odissi Dance
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 2:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kirkland Woman's Club</p>

Technical Odissi, then expressive storytelling.

### Odissi Margam
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kirkland Woman's Club</p>

A traditional Odissi repertoire. Free admission is noted on the schedule.

### From India, to the World
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kirkland Arts Center, 620 Market St</p>

Bharatanatyam set to a mix of classical and newer music. Free.

### Saraband
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 12:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kirkland Urban</p>

Folk through rock, including Americana and blues. Free. Easy to catch on the way through downtown.

### Lexi Nguyen & band
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kirkland Urban</p>

Pop covers and originals. The listing calls it upbeat.

### Aakriti Dance Academy
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 12:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">The Village at Totem Lake</p>

Bollywood and Bharatanatyam. Free.

### Strings By Crista
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 12:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">The Village at Totem Lake</p>

Solo electric violin. Free.

### Tlalokan
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">The Village at Totem Lake</p>

Mexican Danza. Free.

### Manoa, the Heartbeat of Tahiti
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 2:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">The Village at Totem Lake</p>

Polynesian dance. Free.

### BelArts Ballet
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">The Village at Totem Lake</p>

Student ballet.

Skip the Saturday horror reading and the classic sideshow at Kirkland Arts Center if you have younger kids. The schedule is the place to check the rest.  
[Full schedule](https://kirklandartscenter.org/kirkland-fringe-festival/)

### Juanita Friday Market
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 3:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Juanita Beach Park, 9703 NE Juanita Dr</p>

{% include event-photo.html
   src="/assets/images/kirkland/juanita-beach.webp"
   alt="Juanita Beach Park on Lake Washington, with a sandy beach, lawn, and the Seattle skyline across the water."

The city posts the season as June through September. Sep 25 is the last Friday inside that window. Fringe shows start the same evening downtown, so a Juanita afternoon still leaves time to get back for a 6:30 or 7:00 p.m. set.  
[Juanita Friday Market](https://www.kirklandwa.gov/Government/Departments/Parks-and-Community-Services/Special-Event-Services/Juanita-Friday-Market)

## Night out

### Drink The Sea
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kirkland Performance Center, 350 Kirkland Ave</p>

Rock supergroup with members of R.E.M., Screaming Trees, and Queens of the Stone Age. The calendar blurb does not post an age rule or a price. Check the ticket page.  
[KPC calendar](https://www.kpcenter.org/get-tickets/)

### Her Voice, Her Stage
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kirkland Performance Center, 350 Kirkland Ave</p>

Evening showcase with the band Eden, during fringe weekend, plus a browse of women-led local businesses before the show and at intermission. Not listed as a kids program.  
[Fringe schedule](https://kirklandartscenter.org/kirkland-fringe-festival/) · [KPC calendar](https://www.kpcenter.org/get-tickets/)

## For kids in rehearsal

Studio East's Young Frankenstein company has a mandatory meet and greet Tue Sep 22, 5:00–6:00 p.m., then rehearsals. That is for cast, not a public audience. The public run is Oct 30–Nov 8.  
[Studio East auditions](https://studio-east.org/auditions)
