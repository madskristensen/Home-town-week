Wednesday is storytime at Island Books. Thursday evening is a book club for 4th and 5th graders, reading Anne of Green Gables. The Sunday market is over.

## Bookstore

### Storytime for Kids
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 14 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Island Books, 3014 78th Ave SE</p>

Picture books with a bookseller. The October calendar lists this Wednesday at 10:30. The store says the slot is ideal for toddlers through kindergarten.

### Island Kids Book Club
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 15 · 6:00–6:45 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Island Books, 3014 78th Ave SE</p>

Anne of Green Gables, by L. M. Montgomery. The store describes it as a club for 4th and 5th graders, with treats. Thursday the shop is open until 6:00 p.m. on the posted hours (9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.), and the club is listed through 6:45. Plan on staying past the usual lock. Confirm at the desk if the door stays open for the group.

### Saturday Morning Story Time with Nancy Stewart
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 9:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Island Books, 3014 78th Ave SE</p>

On the October calendar. The Oct 3 and Oct 10 entries are 9:30–10:00 a.m. for ages 1 to 4. The Oct 17 line on that same page did not repeat the clock in the short listing. Use 9:30 unless the event page says otherwise.

[October events](https://islandbooks.com/events/2026/10)
