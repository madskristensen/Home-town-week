Saturday is the farmers market at the Legacy Site. The next kids' program at the library is pajama storytime on Wednesday, Sep. 30, outside this span.

## Saturday market

### Maple Valley Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Legacy Site, 25719 Maple Valley Black Diamond Road SE</p>

The market's own page lists Saturdays from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., May 2 through Oct. 31, 2026, at the Legacy Site. The city events page confirms that season. September 26 is one of those Saturdays, and so are Oct. 3 and Oct. 10.

[Maple Valley Farmers Market](https://www.maplevalleyfarmersmarket.com/) · [City events](https://www.maplevalleywa.gov/community/community_events/index.php)

## At the library

Maple Valley Library is at 21844 SE 248th Street. The branch page lists Monday 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., Tuesday and Wednesday noon–8:00 p.m., Thursday and Friday 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., Saturday 11:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., and Sunday closed.

Nothing on the kids' calendar falls inside Sept. 21–27. Pajama storytime is Wednesday, Sep. 30, at 7:00 p.m. Family storytime is Thursday, Oct. 1, at 10:30 a.m.

[Maple Valley Library](https://kcls.org/locations/maple-valley/)

## Outside

Lake Wilderness is the walk if the market is the only stop. No ticketed program is posted at the arboretum for this week.

## Next week

Pajama storytime is Wed Sep 30, 7:00–7:30 p.m., newborn to age 7 with an adult. The market returns Sat Oct 3.
