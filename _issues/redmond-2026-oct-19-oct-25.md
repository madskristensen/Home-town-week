Saturday is the farmers market again, with the Preston Lee Trio from 11 to 1. One Saturday remains after this one, and that last day is the pet parade.

## Saturday

### Redmond Saturday Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 24 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">9900 Willows Rd NE</p>

Hours stay 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. through the last day, Oct 31. The calendar lists the Preston Lee Trio from 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Oct 24. Oct 31, outside this week, is the annual pet parade in that same 11-to-1 window.

[Calendar](http://redmondsaturdaymarket.org/Calendar.cshtml)

## Quiet weekdays

No kids' program was on the Redmond library branch list for this span. Village Theatre's Ragtime has left Issaquah. The Everett opening is Sat Oct 24 at 7:30 p.m. at the Everett Performing Arts Center, the same evening as this market day, not a Redmond show.
