Sunday is the last Festival Park farmers market. Restaurant week is still going downtown. Monday is storytime at the library.

Restaurant week runs through Sunday, Oct. 11. It is a special menu item at participating downtown restaurants, not a single stage show.

[Restaurant Week](https://www.crgov.com/Calendar.aspx?EID=10860)

100 S Wilcox Street. Phone: 303-791-7323.

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Oct 5 · 11:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

Ideal for ages 3–5. Allergen-free, like the other storytimes.

[Preschool Storytime](https://go.dcl.org/event/14286811)

### Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Oct 5 · 6:30–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

All ages. Listed for ages 0–5.

[Evening storytime](https://go.dcl.org/event/14287141)

## Sunday

### Festival Park Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 11 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Festival Park, 300 Second St</p>

Free. The town calendar lists this Sunday, 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., and the market page says the Sunday run ends Oct. 11. The Fall Festival at the same park is Sunday, Oct. 18, after this week.

[Oct. 11 listing](https://www.crgov.com/calendar.aspx?EID=10771)
