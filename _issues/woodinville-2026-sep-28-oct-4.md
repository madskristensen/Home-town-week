Saturday afternoon is a whipped-soap workshop at the library. The kids' storytimes on the branch calendar are still next week, and the farmers market does not come back in October.

## Saturday at the library

### Whipped Butter Soap Workshop
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 1:00–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Woodinville Library, 17105 Avondale Road NE</p>

Lonnie Craft & Co. leads a baking-inspired workshop. You leave with whipped butter soap that looks like frosting, and you are asked to bring your own mug, cup, or small bowl to use as the container. The listing is filed for adults. It does not state a children's age, and the description does not add a registration line.

Saturday hours are 11:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.

[Whipped Butter Soap Workshop](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a85e0a457c264087ad23999)

## Grown-up evenings

### Woodinville Men's Book Group
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 · 6:30–7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Woodinville Library</p>

The book is Michelle Obama's *Becoming*. The group meets on the last Tuesday of the month. New members are welcome. Registration is not required. This is an adult circle, not a family program.

[Men's Book Group](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/696593014a9fd98b7f773b67)

### Talk Time
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 6:30–7:45 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Woodinville Library</p>

English conversation practice for learners, with trained helpers. All levels. Registration is not required. Wednesday the library is open noon–8:00 p.m.

[Talk Time](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/695dd5546724c63d00eab1a8)

## No October market

The chamber's market listing stops the season at the end of September. Do not plan on Festival Street this Saturday.

Baby and toddler storytime is Thu Oct 8 at 10:30 a.m. Family storytime is Fri Oct 9 at 11:00 a.m. Harvest Fest at 21 Acres is Sat Oct 10, 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.
