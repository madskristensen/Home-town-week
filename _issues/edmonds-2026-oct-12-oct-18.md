## Edmonds Center for the Arts

### Michael Douglas
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 14 · 7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Center for the Arts, 410 Fourth Ave N</p>

"One Helluva Ride," on the ECA calendar at 7:00 p.m. An evening talk, in the same lane as the Kyle MacLachlan memoir night. Not a family matinee.

### Swan Lake: Symphony of Lights
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 16 · 7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Center for the Arts, 410 Fourth Ave N</p>

The same production that played Kirkland the weekend of Oct 3. Full-length ballet with light design. Better for older kids and ballet-curious families than for preschoolers. ECA lists 7:00 p.m. Ticket prices for the Kirkland dates do not automatically transfer. Check ECA.

### Booker T. Jones
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 17 · 7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Center for the Arts, 410 Fourth Ave N</p>

On the calendar at 7:30 p.m. The market is over by 2:00, so the concert is a separate night out. Adult show.

[ECA events](https://www.edmondscenterforthearts.org/events/)

Driftwood's Christie closed Oct 11. Cascadia's Dusanne exhibition is still Wednesday through Sunday, 11:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., through Jan 3, 2027.
