Monday evening is storytime. Saturday is Bark for Books, and a storytime at the same time.

The branch is at 100 S Wilcox Street. Phone: 303-791-7323. Storytimes are allergen-free.

## Monday

### Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 6:30–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

All ages. Ages 0–5 are the listed group.

## Friday

### Storytime Discover
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 11:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

All ages. Art, music, dance, yoga, or the storyteller's choice.

[Storytime Discover](https://go.dcl.org/event/14010332)

## Saturday

### Bark for Books
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:15–11:45 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

Ages 6–12 read aloud to a therapy dog. Sign up for one 15-minute session.

### Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

All ages. It overlaps Bark for Books, so a child who is reading to a dog in that half hour would miss part of storytime.

[Saturday listing](https://go.dcl.org/event/14286733)
