Sunday is the Festival Park market. Friday starts downtown restaurant week. Saturday is Bark for Books at the library.

The branch is at 100 S Wilcox Street. Phone: 303-791-7323. Storytimes are allergen-free.

## Monday

### Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 6:30–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

All ages. Ages 0–5 are the listed group.

[Monday storytime](https://go.dcl.org/event/14287140)

## Friday

### Storytime Discover
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 11:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

All ages. Art, music, dance, yoga, or the storyteller's choice.

[Storytime Discover](https://go.dcl.org/event/14010332)

## Saturday

### Bark for Books
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:15–11:45 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

Ages 6–12 read aloud to a therapy dog. Sign up for one 15-minute session.

### Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

All ages. It overlaps Bark for Books, so a child who is reading to a dog in that half hour would miss part of storytime.

[Saturday listing](https://go.dcl.org/event/14286733)

## Friday

Downtown Restaurant Week starts Friday, Oct. 2, and runs through Sunday, Oct. 11. The town calendar does not give it a clock. Participating restaurants add one special item that is not on the regular menu.

[Restaurant Week](https://www.crgov.com/Calendar.aspx?EID=10860)

## Sunday

### Festival Park Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Festival Park, 300 Second St</p>

Free. Same Sunday market, inside the town's May 24 through Oct. 11 run.

[Market page](https://www.crgov.com/3178/Festival-Park-Farmers-Market)
