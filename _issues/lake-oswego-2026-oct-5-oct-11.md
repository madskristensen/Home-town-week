Tuesday is a drop-in banned-book page magnet craft at the library. Saturday is the farmers market, with Farm Fresh Kids.

Freedom to Read Month runs through Oct. 19. The library is at 706 4th Street.

## Tuesday

### Banned book page magnet craft
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 · drop in</p>
<p class="event-place">Lake Oswego Public Library, 706 4th Street</p>

The Freedom to Read page says to drop by the library for a banned book page magnet craft. It does not post a start and end clock, so this issue does not invent one. Phone for the kids desk is 503-697-6580. The main line is 503-636-7628. Posted hours are Monday through Thursday 10:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday 10:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., and Sunday 1:00–5:00 p.m.

[Freedom to Read Month](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/library/freedom-read-month-lopl)

## Saturday

### Lake Oswego Farmers' Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 8:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Millennium Plaza Park</p>

Music 10:30 a.m.–12:30 p.m. Farm Fresh Kids is at the information booth.

[Farmers' Market](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/lofm)

## The following week

The same Freedom to Read page lists a censorship panel on Oct. 13 and a book discussion of *All Boys Aren't Blue* on Oct. 19 at the Lake Oswego Heritage House. Neither date is in this Monday-through-Sunday span.
