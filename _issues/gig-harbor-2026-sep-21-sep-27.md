Tuesday evening is the fall farmers market. The library stays closed through Saturday and reopens Sunday.

## Tuesday market

### Gig Harbor Fall Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 4:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">B's Bagels parking lot, 3308 Uddenberg Lane</p>

Gig Harbor Now lists the fall market on Tuesdays from 4:00 to 7:00 p.m. through Sep. 29, in the parking lot at B's Bagels (B's Butters and Bagels). B's stays open until 7:00 p.m. on market days. September 22 is one of the last two Tuesdays. September 29 is the other.

[Fall market](https://www.gigharbornow.org/news/arts-entertainment/gig-harbor-fall-farmers-market-tuesdays-in-september-4-to-7-pm/)

## Library closed, then Sunday

Gig Harbor Library, 4424 Point Fosdick Drive NW, is closed Monday, Sep. 14, through Saturday, Sep. 26, for maintenance. The closure note covers electrical work, computers, and cleaning. Do not plan a storytime in the building this week.

The event-page hours grid says the branch reopens Sunday, Sep. 27, from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. That grid is the source for Sunday only. It is not a full weekly schedule to copy forward.

### Chess Club, moved
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 5:45–6:45 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">PenMet Recreation Center, 2524 14th Ave NW</p>

While the library is closed, the Sep. 24 chess club meets at PenMet Recreation Center, not on Point Fosdick. The same move covered Sep. 17. The closure article is the source for the room change.

[Library closure](https://www.gigharbornow.org/news/education/gig-harbor-library-closed-september-14-through-september-26/)

### Kids D&D, back at the library
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 1:30–4:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Gig Harbor Library, 4424 Point Fosdick Drive NW</p>

Sunday's D&D session is back in the library, on the day the building reopens. The posted end is 4:30 p.m. An earlier session this month, on Sep. 20, was at Cutter's Point Coffee and ended at 3:30 p.m. That coffee-shop clock does not apply on Sep. 27.

[Kids D&D](https://piercecounty.libnet.info/event/16598553)

## Next week

The last market night is Tue Sep 29, 4:00–7:00 p.m. Toddler and preschool storytimes return that morning, in the library.
