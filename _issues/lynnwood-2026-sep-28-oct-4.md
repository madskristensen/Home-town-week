Wednesday morning is preschool storytime at Lynnwood Library, with play time after. The lake is the rest of the week.

## Wednesday

### Preschool Storytime with Adrianna
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 9:30–10:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lynnwood Library, 19200 44th Ave W</p>

   src="/assets/images/lynnwood/lynnwood-library.webp"

Books, songs, and a creative activity, then play time. For preschoolers. Doors close if the room is full. The branch is open 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. that day, so you are not waiting on the unlock. Sign language interpreters need about two weeks' notice through the library form.

Sno-Isle is the source for this session. A separate calendar has also listed an evening family storytime with Adrianna on Tuesday, Sep 29, at 6:30 p.m. That Tuesday time was not on the official page used for the Wednesday session. Confirm Tuesday on the branch calendar before you plan dinner around it.

Cafecito y Conversación is listed for Thursday, Oct 1, at 5:00 p.m. at the library. That is a Spanish conversation hour, not a kids' storytime. The book group for An Immense World is Monday, Oct 12, outside this week.
