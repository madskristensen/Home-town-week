Monday is toddler storytime and a science lab at Lynnwood Library. Tuesday morning is baby storytime, and Tuesday evening is family storytime. Wednesday is preschool storytime, Thursday is homework help, and Friday is storytime in Spanish.

## Monday and Tuesday

### Toddler Storytime with Nathalie
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 9:30–10:00 a.m. and 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lynnwood Library, 19200 44th Ave W</p>

Two sessions on the Sno-Isle branch calendar. The page lists the title and the clocks. It does not add a registration line in the short listing.

### Little Science Lab with Imagine Children's Museum
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 2:00–2:30 p.m. and 3:00–3:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lynnwood Library, 19200 44th Ave W</p>

Also on the branch calendar, two short sessions the same afternoon. The one-line listing does not say whether you need a ticket beyond showing up. Check the event page if the room caps.

### Baby Storytime with Ella
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 · 9:30–10:00 a.m. and 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lynnwood Library, 19200 44th Ave W</p>

Two morning sessions. The evening family storytime is a separate program later the same day.

[Lynnwood Library](https://www.sno-isle.org/locations/lynnwood/)

## Tuesday night, Thursday homework, Friday in Spanish

### Evening Family Storytime with Adrianna
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 · 6:30–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lynnwood Library, 19200 44th Ave W</p>

Stories, songs, rhymes, and a creative activity, then play time. The Sno-Isle events calendar lists this Tuesday evening session. Doors close if the room is full.

[Evening Family Storytime](https://sno-isle.bibliocommons.com/events/6a3c226448c98c3600a2103c)

### Homework Help
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1 · 5:30–7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lynnwood Library, 19200 44th Ave W</p>

Drop in for grades K–12. Bring the assignment. Volunteers help first come, first served. Registration is not required.

[Homework Help](https://sno-isle.bibliocommons.com/events/6a42f5ad48c98c3600a34783)

### La Hora del Cuento con Marco Cortes
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lynnwood Library, 19200 44th Ave W</p>

Storytime in Spanish for children under 5, with an adult. Songs, stories, and rhymes, then play or a craft.

[La Hora del Cuento](https://sno-isle.bibliocommons.com/events/6a640158c7e02e3d006b8867)
