Saturday starts with Russian storytime at the Main library, then the farmers market on Hall. Sunday afternoon is Arabic storytime.

The Main library is at 12375 SW 5th Street. Monday through Wednesday it is open 10:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m. Thursday through Saturday it closes at 6:00 p.m. Sunday is 1:00–6:00 p.m. Murray Scholls is at 11200 SW Murray Scholls Place, Suite 102, and is closed Sunday and Monday.

## At the Main library

### Toddler Storytime
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 10:15–10:45 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

Ages 2–3. The storytime page lists this on Tuesdays from Sep. 8 through Dec. 10, except Sep. 10 and Nov. 26. Toddler Stay & Play follows at 10:45 a.m.

[Storytimes](https://www.beavertonlibrary.org/381/Storytimes)

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 10:15–10:45 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

Ages 3–6. Preschool Stay & Play is at 10:45 a.m. Toddler Storytime the same day is at noon.

### Boba and buttons
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 4:00–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

Grades 6–12. The September and October youth flyer lists boba while making buttons.

[Sept. and Oct. youth flyer](https://www.beavertonlibrary.org/DocumentCenter/View/13360/2026-09-10-Sept-Oct-ADA)

### Russian Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 10:15–10:45 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main storytime room</p>

Ages 6 and under with family. The select Saturdays are Sep. 26, Oct. 24, Nov. 21, and Dec. 19.

### Family Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

All ages. Listed Saturdays from Sep. 12 through Dec. 12.

### Arabic Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main storytime room</p>

Ages 6 and under with family. The select Sundays include Sep. 27 and Oct. 25.

## Murray Scholls

### Japanese Storytime
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Murray Scholls children's room</p>

Ages 6 and under with family. The select Fridays are Sep. 25, Oct. 9, Nov. 9, and Dec. 4.

## Saturday market

The farmers market lists every Saturday from April through Nov. 21, 8:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m., at 12375 SW 5th Street, on SW Hall Boulevard between 3rd and 5th. That is next to the Main library.

[Beaverton Farmers Market](https://www.beavertonfarmersmarket.com/)
