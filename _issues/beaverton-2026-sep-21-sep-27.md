Schools are out Monday for a staff day, and the Nature Center has a reptile camp. Tuesday night is free ice cream and a K-9 demo at Schiffler Park. Saturday is the market, Russian storytime, and Chuseok at The Round.

## Monday, no school

Beaverton School District lists Monday, Sep. 21, as a staff development day, so students are out. The Tualatin Hills Nature Center is running a day-off camp that day.

### Reptile day-off camp
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 21 · 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Tualatin Hills Nature Center, 15655 SW Millikan Way</p>

The Nature Center describes day-off camps as ages 7–13. This session is snakes and reptiles in the park, a hike, and a chance to see garter snake habitat. Bring lunch, water, snacks, and a backpack. In-district registration is $92. Out of district is $115.

[THPRD nature camps](https://www.thprd.org/activities/keywords/THNCNEC)

## Tuesday evening

### National Night Out
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 5:30–7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Evelyn Schiffler Memorial Park, 5495 SW Erickson Ave</p>

The August date was canceled for heat and smoke. Beaverton Police rescheduled it to this Tuesday. The K-9 demonstration is at 6:00 p.m. Also listed: free ice cream, games, prizes, and booths where kids can meet officers.

[Beaverton Police](https://www.beavertonpolice.org/234/National-Night-Out)

### Toddler Storytime
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 10:15–10:45 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library, 12375 SW 5th Street</p>

Ages 2–3, before the park night. Toddler Stay and Play follows at 10:45 a.m.

[Storytimes](https://www.beavertonlibrary.org/381/Storytimes)

## Thursday downtown

### Downtown Sounds: Kick the Bucket Jazz Band
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 6:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">1st Street Dining Commons</p>

Free outdoor jazz from the Downtown Association. Vendors are usually out with the music. The commons is at SW 1st and Washington.

[Downtown events](https://downtownbeaverton.org/events/2026-09-24/)

### Boba and buttons

<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

Grades 6–12. Boba while making buttons. The same afternoon, the library book and media sale opens and runs through Sunday, Oct. 4.

[Sept. and Oct. youth flyer](https://www.beavertonlibrary.org/DocumentCenter/View/13360/2026-09-10-Sept-Oct-ADA)

### Preschool Storytime

Ages 3–6. Preschool Stay and Play is at 10:45 a.m. Toddler Storytime the same day is at noon.
