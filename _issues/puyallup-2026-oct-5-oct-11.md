Saturday is the last farmers market of the season in Pioneer Park. Thursday storytime repeats, and the library has an ESL hour the same morning as the market.

## Thursday storytime

The Thursday series continues in the Puyallup Public Library meeting room. The Oct. 1 event pages name Oct. 8 as the next date. Those pages do not publish a separate link for Oct. 8 in the short listing used here, so confirm the room on the library calendar if you need a second page.

### Toddler Treasures
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 8 · 9:45–10:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Puyallup Public Library meeting room</p>

Ages 1–3. Same clock as Oct. 1.

[Series listing](https://puyalluplibrary.libcal.com/event/16206996)

### Little Learners
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 8 · 10:45–11:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Puyallup Public Library meeting room</p>

Ages 3–6.

[Series listing](https://puyalluplibrary.libcal.com/event/16207003)

## Saturday, two stops

### ESL
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 9:00–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Puyallup Public Library meeting room</p>

Free practice for grade-school children and adults, with teachers from Clover Park and the Puyallup School District. This is at the library, not in the park. Market parking notes mention the library lot, which is a reason to treat the two as different stops on the same morning.

[ESL](https://puyalluplibrary.libcal.com/event/15664152)

### Puyallup Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Pioneer Park and Pavilion</p>

The market site ends the season on Oct. 10. Music is 11:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m., posted as @uncledougmusic1. The market site says 300 S Meridian. The city calendar says 330 S Meridian.

[Puyallup Farmers Market](https://puyallupfarmersmarket.com/) · [Market experience](https://puyallupfarmersmarket.com/market-experience/)
