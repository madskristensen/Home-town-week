Saturday is Children's Day at Huntamer Park. The same morning, Birth to Five storytime and a public book sale are at the library.

Lacey Library is at 500 College Street SE.

## Monday games, and one off-site hour

### All-Ages Board Game Night
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 4:30–5:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lacey Library</p>

Games and snacks.

[Board Game Night](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/69309a227ed7c62f00a89540)

The same Monday, Bibliotopia is 4:00–5:00 p.m. at Eagles Landing, 2257 E Eagle Ln SW, Tumwater. It is a book conversation on the last Monday of the month, and it is not at the Lacey library. It overlaps the start of board games. Pick one.

[Bibliotopia](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/69fa58f9f56bd86e00adc16c)

## Baby storytime

### Baby Storytime
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 and Thu Oct 1 · 10:30–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lacey Library</p>

Ages birth to 18 months. Older siblings are welcome. The hour is designed for babies.

[Tuesday](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7267c2f213992f00c99ad3) · [Thursday](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7267c2f213992f00c99ad4)

## The book sale

### Friends book sale, members
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 1:30–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lacey Library</p>

Members only. Memberships are sold at the door.

[Members' sale](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/693c56b176261f28005ec8f0)

### Friends book sale, public
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · from 9:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lacey Library</p>

The public sale opens at 9:30 a.m. The listing used here does not add a closing time.

[Public sale](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/693c56ca1e64afd01e4eb6e4) · [Lacey Friends](http://laceyfriends.org/)

## Saturday, library then park

### Birth to Five Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lacey Library</p>

Ages birth to 5. This overlaps the first hour of Children's Day. The library and the park are different stops.

[Birth to Five](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7266ba74e3b8b75db7951a)

### Children's Day
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Huntamer Park</p>

Free, rain or shine. Lacey Parks posts food vendors, a mythical theme, inflatables, and activity booths. The kids' market is full. The parks page does not add a street address for Huntamer, so this note stops at the park name.

[Children's Day](https://laceyparks.org/childrens-day/) · [City events](https://cityoflacey.org/news/community-events/)
