The Wednesday farmers market's own 2026 clock stopped before this week. Beaver Lake and Pine Lake are the plan, and the library is the indoor hour.

## No Wednesday market

### Sammamish Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Not this week</p>
<p class="event-place">Sammamish Commons, 801 228th Ave SE</p>

The market's own page lists 2026 hours as 4:00–8:00 p.m. from May 6 through August 26, and 4:00–7:00 p.m. on September 2 and September 9. It now points to opening day on May 5, 2027. A standing line on the same site still says "May through September." The dated hours are the ones to trust. Do not go to the Commons on Wednesday, Sep 16, expecting stalls.

The city's 2026 recreation guide also says Wednesdays, May through September, 4:00–8:00 p.m. That guide does not list a September closing date. The market site does. If the two disagree, use the market site.

[Market day](https://www.sammamishfarmersmarket.org/market-day/)
