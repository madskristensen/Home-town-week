Friday Lake City Playhouse opens Finding Neverland. Saturday and Sunday are the Celtic Festival, and Sunday also has a 2:00 p.m. matinee.

The library still keeps the stuffed animals overnight after Friday storytime.

## Weekend

### Coeur d'Alene Celtic Festival
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 and Sun Sep 27</p>
<p class="event-place">Kootenai County Fairgrounds, 4056 N Government Way</p>

Music, Highland dance, storytelling, Celtic games, and a Kids Sword Arena. Adults are $10, ages 6–17 are $5, and 5 and under are free.

The festival page lists Sunday, Sep. 27, from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. It also says the Saturday gate stays open until 9:00 p.m., and the beer garden and Celtic Café until 8:30 p.m. The Coeur d'Alene Press, on Sep. 25, reported Saturday 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and the same Sunday hours. If you are planning the Saturday close, use the festival page.

[The Bruce Festival](https://www.thebrucefestival.com/celtic-festival-2/) · [Coeur d'Alene Press](https://cdapress.com/news/2026/sep/25/coeur-dalene-celtic-festival-this-weekend/)

## At Lake City Playhouse

### Finding Neverland
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 7:30 p.m. · Sat Sep 26 · 2:00 p.m. · Sun Sep 27 · 2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lake City Playhouse, 1320 E Garden Ave</p>

The musical about how Peter Pan got to the stage. The Spokesman-Review lists Thursdays and Fridays at 7:30 p.m. and Saturdays and Sundays at 2:00 p.m., with Saturday, Oct. 3, moved to 7:30 p.m. The Press says the run opens Sep. 25 and closes Oct. 11. Tickets are $25 for adults, $23 for seniors and military, and $21.50 for students. The ticket list confirms Sunday, Sep. 27, at 2:00 p.m.

[Spokesman-Review](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/sep/24/hope-soars-in-finding-neverland/) · [Tickets](https://lakecityplayhouse.ticketgoat.com/)

The children's newsletter lists autumn story times at 9:00 and 11:00 a.m. Baby storytime (ages 0–18 months) is Tuesdays, Sep. 8 through Nov. 17. Toddler storytime (18 months to 3 years) is Thursdays, Sep. 10 through Nov. 19. Preschool (ages 3–5) is not on the Friday list for Sep. 25. The listed Fridays are Sep. 11, Sep. 18, Oct. 2, Oct. 9, Oct. 16, Oct. 30, and three November dates. The newsletter does not assign 9:00 or 11:00 to a single age group, so this issue does not guess which clock is which.

## Tuesday

### From Books to Bricks
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Coeur d'Alene Public Library</p>

Ages 6–12. A book sets a LEGO challenge, then the rest of the hour is building. One creation goes in the glass case. The series is Tuesdays, Sep. 8 through Nov. 17.

## Thursday

### Savanna ZOOniversity
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Coeur d'Alene Public Library</p>

Ages 6–8. September's two Thursdays are Sep. 10 and Sep. 24, on savanna herbivores: stories, facts, a craft, and an activity.

## Friday and Saturday

### Pajama Storytime
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 6:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Coeur d'Alene Public Library</p>

All ages. Wear sleepwear. This is the send-off before the stuffie sleepover: books, rhymes, and songs.

### Stuffie sleepover
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 drop-off, before 7:00 p.m. · Sat Sep 26 pickup, before 5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Coeur d'Alene Public Library</p>

Drop off a stuffed animal Friday any time before 7:00 p.m. A permission slip needs the stuffie's name, description, dislikes, preferences, and a contact. Pick up Saturday before 5:00 p.m.
