Monday is a short craft for ages 3 to 5. Storytime repeats midweek. Saturday is the market.

Maple Valley Library is at 21844 SE 248th Street. Monday hours are 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. Tuesday and Wednesday are noon–8:00 p.m. Thursday opens at 10:00 a.m.

## Monday craft

### Crafts for 3s, 4s and 5s
<p class="event-when">Mon Oct 5 · 10:30–11:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Maple Valley Library</p>

Registration is required. The listing asks you to count only the children who will do the craft, not the adults who come with them. The event page says the location is Maple Valley. The description says the hour is sponsored by the Friends of the Kent Libraries. Confirm the branch when you register, so a Kent sponsorship line does not send you to the wrong building.

[Crafts for 3s, 4s and 5s](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a80de593b6c71003e591fce)

## Storytime, then a teen writing hour

### Pajama Story Time
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 7:00–7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Maple Valley Library</p>

The same Wednesday evening session as last week. Newborn through age 7 with an adult. A stuffed animal or blanket is welcome. Registration is not required.

[Pajama Story Time](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a5a99914523092f0030cce7)

### Family Story Time
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 8 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Maple Valley Library</p>

Ages 3–6, with younger and older siblings welcome. Stay for play. Registration is not required.

[Family Story Time](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a5a998f0e562e28009a98f0)

### Pen 2 Paper
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 · 5:45–7:15 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Maple Valley Library</p>

A writing hour for middle and high school. Supplies and snacks are provided. Registration is not required. Maple Valley Library support is named on the listing. This is not the Monday preschool craft.

[Pen 2 Paper](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a76aedfe30fe4845967ddad)

### Tutors
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 and Wed Oct 7 · 5:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Maple Valley Library</p>

Drop-in homework help, grades K–12. Tuesday's tutor block overlaps the start of Pen 2 Paper. They are different programs in the same building.

[Tuesday tutors](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7a362488e9bf280036f383) · [Wednesday tutors](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7a362488e9bf280036f384)

## Saturday market

### Maple Valley Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Legacy Site, 25719 Maple Valley Black Diamond Road SE</p>

Saturdays continue through Oct. 31. This is not the last market of the season.

[Maple Valley Farmers Market](https://www.maplevalleyfarmersmarket.com/)
