Thursday evening is the Tanasbourne market. Saturday is downtown. Sunday is Orenco Station.

Hillsboro Farmers' Markets lists three weekly stops that land in this span. Downtown Saturdays run Apr. 25 through Oct. 24, 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. Orenco Station Sundays run Apr. 26 through Oct. 25, 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. The Streets at Tanasbourne is the 2nd and 4th Thursdays, June 11 through Oct. 22, 4:00–8:00 p.m. Sep. 24 is the 4th Thursday.

Reed's Crossing is the 1st Wednesday only. The Wednesday in this span is Sep. 23, so that market is not this week. The next one is the Harvest Fest on Oct. 7.

## Brookwood Library

Brookwood is at 2850 NE Brookwood Pkwy. The library's storytime series describes Toddler Storytime as weekly on Tuesdays, 10:15–11:00 a.m., ages 2–3, with books, action songs, and rhymes. Preschool Storytime is weekly on Thursdays, 10:15–11:00 a.m., ages 3–6. Sep. 22 is a Tuesday and Sep. 24 is a Thursday.
