Wednesday and Saturday are storytimes at Island Books. Sunday is the last farmers market of the season.

## Island Books

### Storytime for Kids
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Island Books, 3014 78th Ave SE</p>

A bookseller reads picture books. The store describes the Wednesday slot as ideal for toddlers through kindergarten. The October calendar lists this date at 10:30–11:00 a.m.

[Wednesday storytime](https://islandbooks.com/event/2026-10-07/storytime-kids)

### Saturday Morning Story Time with Nancy Stewart
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 9:30–10:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Island Books, 3014 78th Ave SE</p>

Songs and stories for ages 1 to 4. The October calendar gives 9:30–10:00 a.m. for Oct 3 and Oct 10. Store hours that day are 9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.

Tuesday, Oct 6, is an adult author event at 6:30 p.m., Jennifer Marcou on a book for introverts. That is not the kids' hour.

[October events](https://islandbooks.com/events/2026/10)

## Last market

### Mercer Island Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 11 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">7700 SE 32nd St</p>

The city says the 2026 season is May 31 through Oct 11, every Sunday, 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. This is the last one. It does not return on Oct 18.

[City page](https://www.mercerisland.gov/community/page/mercer-island-farmers-market-0)

The library branch list used for September did not publish an Oct 11 storytime. Do not assume one. Luther Burbank is the walk after the tents if you still want the water.
