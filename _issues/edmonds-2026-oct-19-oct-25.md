## Saturday night, two tickets

### 20th Anniversary Gala
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 24 · 5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Center for the Arts, 410 Fourth Ave N</p>

On the ECA calendar. A gala, not a drop-in family show. The market ends at 2:00, so there is a gap if someone is doing both.

### Yesterday & Today
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 24 · 8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Center for the Arts, 410 Fourth Ave N</p>

"The Interactive Beatles Experience," at 8:00 p.m. the same night as the gala. They overlap if the gala runs long. Treat them as two separate tickets until ECA says otherwise. Adult evening.

[ECA events](https://www.edmondscenterforthearts.org/events/)
