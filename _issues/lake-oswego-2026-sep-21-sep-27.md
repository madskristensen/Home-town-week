Sunday is the free Cultural Xchange Festival at Millennium Plaza Park. Saturday has the farmers market on that lawn, and a matinee of You Can't Take It With You at Lakewood. Sunday afternoon is also Korean storytime.

## At Lakewood

### You Can't Take It With You
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 23 – Sat Sep 26 · evenings 7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lakewood Center for the Arts, 368 S State Street</p>

Moss Hart and George S. Kaufman's family comedy runs Sep. 11 through Oct. 18. Evenings are Wednesday through Saturday at 7:30 p.m. This Saturday also has a 2:00 p.m. matinee. The site lists Sunday matinees in the run. Check the ticket page for Sunday, Sep. 27, before you count on it.

[Lakewood](https://lakewood-center.org/)

The Lake Oswego Public Library is at 706 4th Street. Freedom to Read Month on the library's page runs Sep. 22 through Oct. 19. Banned Books Week, Sep. 22–28, is the kickoff: a display of materials that have been banned or challenged, plus free buttons, stickers, and copies of the American Library Association's book report while supplies last.

[Freedom to Read Month](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/library/freedom-read-month-lopl)

## Saturday market

### Lake Oswego Farmers' Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 8:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Millennium Plaza Park</p>

The city's 2026 season is Saturdays, May 9 through Oct. 31, with no market on July 4. Live music is 10:30 a.m.–12:30 p.m. SNAP matching is up to $20.

Farm Fresh Kids is the same morning. Children pick up a passport at the Market Information Booth, finish three activities (a question, a search for the apple mascots Betty and Roni, and a booth activity), and can earn up to $3 in tokens for fruit and vegetables.

[Farmers' Market](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/lofm) · [Farm Fresh Kids](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/lofm/farm-fresh-kids)

The same morning, the police department holds a car seat clinic at the Main Fire Station, 300 B Avenue, 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. Appointments are on the city page. It is for parents checking the seat, not a drop-in play hour.

[Car seat clinic](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/community/car-seat-clinic-0)

### Lego Club
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 3:00–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Children's Library, lower level, 706 Fourth Street</p>

Drop-in building on the second and fourth Saturdays. Bricks are provided. The club page prints 3:00–4:00 p.m. The city calendar places this week's session on Sep. 26.

[Lego Club](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/kids/lego-club-0)

## Sunday

### Cultural Xchange Festival
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 11:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Millennium Plaza Park</p>

Free. Two stages, food, and performances through the afternoon, including a lion dance, a dance school set, and a pipe band. The parks page names Millennium Plaza Park, the same lawn as the Saturday market.

[Cultural Xchange](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/parksrec/2026-cultural-xchange-festival)

### Korean Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 1:30–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lake Oswego Public Library, 706 Fourth Street</p>

Fourth Sunday of the month. Songs, stories, and a craft, recommended for ages 6 and under with families. Free, no registration. It sits inside the festival hours, so it is a library stop on the way to or from the park, not a second all-day plan.

[Korean Storytime](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/kids/korean-storytime-0)

## Thursday at LORAC

### Live and Local
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 4:00–8:00 p.m. · music 5:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lake Oswego Recreation and Aquatic Center patio, 17525 Stafford Rd</p>

The last date in this pilot. The Lakeridge High School Jazz Band plays. Food is part of the evening. The same afternoon, an emergency preparedness fair runs 4:00–6:00 p.m. at City Hall, 380 A Avenue, with water-storage supplies for Lake Oswego households. Those are two different addresses.

[Live and Local](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/LORAC/live-local-lorac) · [Preparedness fair](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/community/2026-emergency-preparedness-fair)

Indoor Playground at the same recreation center is drop-in on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. The fee is $4, plus $2 for an additional sibling. Parents stay with the kids.

[Indoor Playground](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/LORAC/indoor-playground-lorac-0)

The storytime guide says dates live on the library calendar, and that a session uses preschool on Tuesdays at 10:30 a.m., toddlers on Wednesdays at 10:30 a.m., Fun for Ones on Thursdays at 10:30 a.m., and babies on Thursdays at 3:00 p.m. This issue does not add those clocks as dated sessions, because the guide points families to the calendar for which weeks are in session.

[Storytimes](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/kids/storytimes-and-programs)
