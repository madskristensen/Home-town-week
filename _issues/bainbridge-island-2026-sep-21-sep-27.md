Saturday is the farmers market at Town Square by City Hall. The next storytimes at the library are Wednesday, Sep. 30.

## Saturday market

### Bainbridge Island Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Town Square by City Hall, 598 Madison Avenue North</p>

The market runs Saturdays from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. through Oct. 24, 2026. September 26, Oct. 3, and Oct. 10 are all inside that season. A July 3 exception on the market site is outside this window.

[Bainbridge Island Farmers Market](https://www.bainbridgeislandfarmersmarket.com/) · [Town Square listing](https://www.bainbridgeisland.com/event/farmers-market/)

## At the library

Bainbridge Island Library is at 1270 Madison Ave N, a short walk from the market's Madison address. No storytime is posted inside Sept. 21–27. Wednesday, Sep. 30, is toddler storytime at 10:30 a.m. and preschool storytime at 11:30 a.m. The Friends book sale on the calendar before this week was Sep. 20. The next sale is Thursday, Oct. 1.

Library hours are not copied here. They were not on the event pages used for this week.

## Outside

Eagle Harbor is the walk after the market: boats, the shore, and the ferry terminal in the distance. No ticketed harbor program is posted for this week.

## Next week

Builders' Club on Wednesday afternoon is themed "I Spy a Lighthouse." The market returns Saturday, Oct. 3.
