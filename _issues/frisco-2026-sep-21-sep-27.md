Saturday morning is Safety Town, then family story time for walkers through age 5. Evening is the Gallegos Park reopening. That night FC Dallas hosts LAFC at Toyota Stadium.

The library is at 8000 Dallas Parkway. This week's posted hours are Sunday 1:00–6:00 p.m., Monday through Thursday 9:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m., Friday 9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., and Saturday 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.

### Family Story Time
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Storytime Room, Frisco Public Library</p>

Songs, rhymes, and books. Space is limited. A dated listing updated Sep. 21 puts this Saturday at 10:30 a.m. The series page describes the same program and shows 10:00 a.m. on other dates, so use the desk tickets that morning if the clock on the series page differs.

[Family Story Time](https://friscolibrary.bibliocommons.com/events/6a57b29bf4e5db3d00c1e6c1)

## Saturday morning and evening

### Safety Saturday
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 9:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Frisco Fire Safety Town, 8601 Gary Burns Dr</p>

Hands-on safety activities for families. A weather talk is at 10:00 a.m. Jeep and bike rides need a reservation and have age rules on the event page. Parking is first come around the safety town and Central Fire Station.

[Safety Saturday](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=7713)

### Gallegos Park Re-Opening Celebration
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 5:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Gallegos Park, 6698 Pecan Street</p>

The city is marking the end of the reinvestment project, inside Community Awareness Night, which the calendar runs 5:00–9:00 p.m. in Frisco neighborhoods. Street parking is limited. A public garage in the Rail District is a few blocks away.

[Gallegos Park](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=7743)

## Saturday night

### FC Dallas vs. LAFC
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26</p>
<p class="event-place">Toyota Stadium</p>

Visit Frisco lists this as a home match. It does not print a kickoff time, so check the club before you leave. General parking is $20 and cashless. Clear bags are the rule.

[Visit Frisco fall guide](https://www.visitfrisco.com/blog/fall-frisco-your-guide-to-autumn-events)
