Poulsbo Library is at 700 NE Lincoln Road.

### Preschool Storytime

<p class="event-place">Poulsbo Library</p>

Ages 3–5.

### Baby Storytime

## Wednesday

### Toddler Storytime
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 10:15–10:45 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Poulsbo Library</p>

Ages 18 months to 3.

[Toddler Storytime](https://krl.bibliocommons.com/events/6a67b2690d65ac3600429595)

### Poulsbo Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">18901 8th Ave NE</p>
