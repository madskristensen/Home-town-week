Saturday night is the free Otsukimi moon-viewing festival at Haggard Park. Monday is preschool storytime at Davis.

## Saturday night

### Otsukimi Japanese Moon Viewing Festival
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 6:00–10:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Haggard Park</p>

Free. The Japan America Society of DFW lists taiko, a Ghibli music set, calligraphy, flower arranging, tea, goldfish scooping, face painting, and bounce houses. An RSVP enters a prize drawing and is not required to walk in.

[Japan America Society of DFW](https://jasdfw.org/free-otsukimi-festival-brings-anime-star-emi-lo-live-performances-and-japanese-inspired-marketplace-to-plano/)

## Monday

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 21 · 11:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Davis Library children's program room, 7501 Independence Parkway</p>

Ages 3–5. Longer stories and movement. About 25–30 minutes. Davis phone: 972-208-8000.

[Preschool Storytime](https://plano.libnet.info/event/16728329)

## Tuesday

### Breathe and Read Storytime
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 10:30–11:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Parr Library program room, 6200 Windhaven Parkway</p>

Ages 3–5. Books, gentle movement, and noticing the room, for calming and focus. Parr phone: 972-769-4300.

[Breathe and Read](https://plano.libnet.info/event/16719919)

## Weekend shows and trains

### Fall Plano Train Show
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 10:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. · Sun Sep 27 · 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Plano Event Center, 2000 Convention Center Blvd</p>

Layouts and hands-on trains. Visit Plano lists $11 a person. Children 12 and under are free. Cash at the door is $1 off.

[Train show](https://events.visitplano.com/event/fall-plano-train-show-2026)

### Beetlejuice JR
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 2:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m. · Sun Sep 27 · 2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">North Texas Performing Arts, Willow Bend Center of the Arts, 6121 W Park Blvd</p>

A youth-company musical. Visit Plano lists $13 in advance, $15 from Sep. 20, and $25 for front-row seats. More performances are Thu Oct 1, Fri Oct 2, and Sat Oct 3.

[Beetlejuice JR](https://ntpa.org/event/beetlejuice-jr-plano/)

### Around the World in 80 Days
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 2:00 p.m. and 6:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Courtyard Theater, downtown Plano</p>

Accolade Theatre. Phileas Fogg's bet, played as a family show. The Visit Plano listing does not print a street number, so use the ticket page for the door.

[Accolade Theatre](https://accoladetheatre.org/shows/around-the-world-in-80-days)
