Wednesday evening is pajama storytime at the Central Library. Saturday is the farmers market. Sunday is the Bellingham Symphony at the Mount Baker Theatre.

## The library week

### Little Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 and Tue Sep 29 · 10:05, 10:35, and 11:35 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library, 210 Central Ave</p>

Birth to age 3 with an adult. Same three morning slots as last week.

### Library Explorers
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 3:30–4:15 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lecture Room, Central Library</p>

Ages 6–10, families welcome. The book sale is over, so the Lecture Room is back to programs.

### Bellis Fair
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30</p>
<p class="event-place">Multipurpose Room, Bellis Fair branch</p>

Little Storytime at 10:05, 10:45, and 11:25 a.m. Preschool Storytime at 12:05–12:35 p.m. Baby Time at 2:30–3:30 p.m. if you registered for the class. The library skips Little Storytime, Preschool Storytime, and Baby Time at this branch on Oct 7, which is next week, not this one. Mall doors before 11:00 a.m. are the Target entrance or the food court.

### Pajama Storytime
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 5:15–5:45 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library, 210 Central Ave</p>

Ages 3–8, with siblings and family. Bring a stuffed animal. Books, songs, and felt. Wear pajamas if you want. This is the downtown evening. It does not overlap the Bellis Fair afternoon sessions if you pick one branch.

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library</p>

Ages 3–5 with an adult, plus a simple craft.

### Baby Time
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 10:30–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library</p>

Birth to 12 months with a caregiver. Registration required for the Friday series.

[September library programs](https://bellinghampubliclibrary.org/news/september-2026-bellingham-public-library-programs-and-events)

## Mount Baker Theatre

### Maya de Vitry
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2</p>
<p class="event-place">Mount Baker Theatre, 104 N Commercial St</p>

   src="/assets/images/bellingham/mount-baker-theatre.webp"
   alt="The Mount Baker Theatre marquee and stone front on Commercial Street in Bellingham."
   credit="Photo: Singalto1, CC BY-SA 4.0"

A Lookout Sessions night. The season page lists the date and does not put a start time in the short blurb. A parent show, not a kids' matinee. Ticket office (360) 734-6080. Friday morning Baby Time is the other end of this day.

### BSO: Star-Cross'd Dreams
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4</p>
<p class="event-place">Mount Baker Theatre, 104 N Commercial St</p>

Bellingham Symphony Orchestra. The theatre lists the concert for Sunday. The start time is on the ticket page, not in the season headline. Better for kids who can sit through an orchestra concert. Saturday's market is over by 2:00, so Sunday is the separate trip.

[What's on](https://www.mountbakertheatre.com/events-tickets/)

## A bite downtown

The market has the Saturday food. If Sunday is the concert, Fairhaven's Colophon Cafe is the earlier, kid-friendlier stop. Hours are on the cafe site. No special is posted for this week.

[Colophon Cafe](https://www.colophoncafe.com/)
