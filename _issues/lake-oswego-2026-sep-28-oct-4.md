Evenings at Lakewood are still You Can't Take It With You. Saturday is the usual market at Millennium Plaza Park. Sunday is the First Sunday Market in Lake Grove, themed as a back-to-school block party, and the library has a Día de los Muertos craft the same afternoon.

## At Lakewood

### You Can't Take It With You
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 – Sat Oct 3 · 7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lakewood Center for the Arts, 368 S State Street</p>

The comedy keeps the Wednesday-through-Saturday evening clock. This week does not have the extra Saturday matinee. That one is Oct. 10. Sunday matinees are listed for the run. Confirm Oct. 4 on the ticket page.

[Lakewood](https://lakewood-center.org/)

## Saturday

### Lake Oswego Farmers' Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 8:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Millennium Plaza Park</p>

Music is 10:30 a.m.–12:30 p.m. Farm Fresh Kids passports are at the information booth. The season runs through Oct. 31.

[Farmers' Market](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/lofm)

## Sunday in Lake Grove

### First Sunday Market: Back to School Block Party
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 11:00 a.m.–2:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Hallmark Drive, Lake Oswego</p>

The city's 2026 themes name Oct. 4 as the Back to School Block Party: carnival games, face painting, and the regular vendors. Live music is 12:00–2:00 p.m. This is not a smaller copy of the Saturday market. Nov. 1 is Pet-a-Palooza, which is outside this span.

[First Sunday Market](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/lgfm)

The same Sunday, the library hosts a Día de los Muertos celebration from 1:00 to 3:00 p.m. The block party ends at 2:30, so the second hour of the craft is after the market. Folklórico artist Luisa Valentín Pelagio talks about the tradition, then families make papel picado. The library is at 706 Fourth Street.

[Día de los Muertos](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/library/dia-de-los-muertos-celebration)

## Library, Monday and Friday

### Music and Movement in Mandarin
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 28 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lake Oswego Public Library, 706 Fourth Street</p>

Sharon Gan of Heart Strings Mandarin sings, moves, and tells stories with young learners. Beginners and native speakers are both welcome. The kids calendar repeats it on Mondays.

[Music and Movement in Mandarin](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/kids/music-and-movement-mandarin-4)

### Baby Sign Language
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lower level, Lake Oswego Public Library</p>

First Friday, for babies about 0 to 18 months and their caregivers. Christina Schabow of the Portland Early Learning Project leads it. Free, no registration.

[Baby Sign Language](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/kids/baby-sign-language-0)

Indoor Playground at the Lake Oswego Recreation and Aquatic Center, 17525 Stafford Rd, is drop-in Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. The fee is $4, plus $2 for an additional sibling.

[Indoor Playground](https://www.ci.oswego.or.us/LORAC/indoor-playground-lorac-0)
