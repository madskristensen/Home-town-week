Saturday is Harvest Fest at Heritage Park, with 1,500 pumpkins for kids and crafts from the library. Thursday night is storytime. Friday the Friends book sale opens at Community Hall.

## Thursday

### Storytime with Stuffies
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 8 · 6:30–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Library Corner, Eagle Public Library, 100 N Stierman Way</p>

Ages birth to 5. The library asks families to build literacy skills through stories and songs. A stuffed animal is optional.

[Oct. 8 storytime](https://boisemom.com/event/storytime-with-stuffies-eagle-public-library/2026-10-08/)

## Friday into Saturday

### Friends of the Eagle Library Book Sale
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 11:00 a.m. through Sat Oct 10 · 3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Community Hall</p>

The city calendar runs the fall Friends sale across those hours, so it overlaps Harvest Fest on Saturday. The listing names Community Hall and does not add a street number on the line used here.

[City calendar](https://cityofeagle.org/calendar.aspx)

## Saturday

### Eagle Harvest Fest
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Heritage Park, 185 E State Street</p>

The Great Pumpkin Giveaway is 1,500 pumpkins for kids to decorate and take home. The city says to come early. Also listed: kids' crafts from the Eagle Public Library, fiddle music, blacksmith demos, food vendors, and a visit from the Idaho Liberty Bell. The Saturday market itself is over. This is the fall festival in the same park.

[Harvest Fest](https://cityofeagle.org/303/Harvest-Fest)
