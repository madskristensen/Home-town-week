Wednesday morning is lap-sit story time for babies who are not walking yet. Sunday afternoon is a come-and-go craft for grades K to 5.

Frisco Public Library is at 8000 Dallas Parkway. Sunday hours are 1:00–6:00 p.m., so the craft is after the building opens. Wednesday is a regular 9:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m. day.

## Wednesday

### Baby (Non-Walkers) Lap-Sit Story Time
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 10:00–10:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Storytime Room</p>

Stories, songs, and a lap. For infants who are not walking yet, with a caregiver. Tickets are available an hour before, and space is limited. The series lists Oct. 7, Oct. 14, Oct. 21, and Oct. 28 at this clock.

[Baby lap-sit series](https://friscolibrary.bibliocommons.com/v2/events?series=6a57b3330e562e28009a0435)

## Sunday

### Arts and crafts for kids with special needs
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 11 · 2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Storytime Room</p>

Grades K–5. Come and go. The listing is for children with special needs. For an accommodation under the ADA, the library asks for five business days' notice at (972) 292-5669 or library@friscotexas.gov.

[Library events](https://friscolibrary.bibliocommons.com/v2/events)
