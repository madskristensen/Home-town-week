Saturday's Fall Market has pumpkin decorating until 1:00 p.m. Friday is a spooky movie in the PGA District. Wednesday morning is lap-sit story time for babies who are not walking yet. Sunday afternoon is a come-and-go craft for kids with special needs.

Frisco Public Library is at 8000 Dallas Parkway. Sunday hours are 1:00–6:00 p.m., so the craft is after the building opens. Wednesday is a regular 9:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m. day.

## Wednesday

### Baby (Non-Walkers) Lap-Sit Story Time
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 10:00–10:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Storytime Room</p>

Stories, songs, and a lap. For infants who are not walking yet, with a caregiver. Tickets are available an hour before, and space is limited. The series lists Oct. 7, Oct. 14, Oct. 21, and Oct. 28 at this clock.

[Baby lap-sit series](https://friscolibrary.bibliocommons.com/v2/events?series=6a57b3330e562e28009a0435)

## Friday and Saturday out

### Movie night in the PGA District
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">PGA District</p>

The same spooky Friday series as last week. The guide does not pin one movie title to this date.

### Fall Market at PGA Frisco
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">PGA District</p>

More than 50 vendors. Kids can sign up for pumpkin decorating from 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Parking in the district is free.

[Visit Frisco](https://www.visitfrisco.com/blog/fall-frisco-your-guide-to-autumn-events)

## Sunday

### Arts and crafts for kids with special needs
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 11 · 2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Storytime Room</p>

Grades K–5. Come and go. The listing is for children with special needs. For an accommodation under the ADA, the library asks for five business days' notice at (972) 292-5669 or library@friscotexas.gov.

[Library events](https://friscolibrary.bibliocommons.com/v2/events)

## Wednesday plaza, Tuesday at the library

### Plaza Live
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 5:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">4th Street Plaza, 8800 4th St</p>

Local musicians in the plaza while you eat and shop. The series is the first and third Wednesdays. Oct. 7 is the first Wednesday of the month.

[Plaza Live](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=7708)

### Game Design Basics
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 · 4:30–5:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Community Event Room A, Frisco Public Library</p>

Basics for tabletop and video games. The listing includes teens and adults.

[Game Design Basics](https://friscolibrary.bibliocommons.com/events/6a85e0a8d4b10d00300791ac)

### Puzzle Pop-In
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 · 5:30–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Frisco Public Library, 8000 Dallas Parkway</p>

Come and go. Use a library puzzle or bring one. Ages on the listing run from about 6 through teens and adults, so it can follow the game-design hour or stand on its own.

[Puzzle Pop-In](https://friscolibrary.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7bcc4ad4b10d003005e5d3)
