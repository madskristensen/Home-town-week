Tuesday is the farmers market. Friday morning is storytime at Renton Highlands. Sunday is Pokémon Club at the downtown library. One market Tuesday is left after this week.

## Tuesday market

### Renton Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 3:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Piazza Park, 233 Burnett Ave S</p>

The next-to-last Tuesday. Entertainment 4:00–6:00 p.m. was still TBA on the season chart. Kid's Patch and Master Gardeners are the standing kids' piece. Closing day is Tue Sep 29.

[This season](https://rentonfarmersmarket.com/thisseason.php) · [Renton Reporter](https://www.rentonreporter.com/2026/09/17/whats-going-on-around-renton-sept-17-edition/)

## Highlands

### Reading Buddies
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 4:30–6:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Renton Highlands Library</p>

On the branch calendar for Thursday afternoon. The short listing does not spell out ages. It is a reading program, not a concert. Read the event note if you need the grade range.

### Toddler Story Time
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 11:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Renton Highlands Library</p>

The Friday toddler slot on the branch page.

### Baby Story Time
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 12:00–12:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Renton Highlands Library</p>

Nursery rhymes and songs. The branch lists 12:00–12:30 p.m. A longer program description has also shown a noon hour for babies, newborn to 18 months, with your own mat. Use the branch calendar's clock the day you go. No registration on the description page. An adult stays.

[Renton Highlands](https://kcls.org/locations/renton-highlands/)
