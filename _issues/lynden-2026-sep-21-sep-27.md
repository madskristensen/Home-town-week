## Friday storytime

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lynden Library, 216 4th Street, large meeting room</p>

Ages 3–5. The library calendar lists this Friday series, including Sep. 25, Oct. 2, and Oct. 9.

[Preschool Storytime](https://wcls.libcal.com/event/16953421)

## Tuesday at the Jansen

### Acoustic music meetup
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 6:00–7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Jansen Art Center, Ensemble Room, 321 Front St</p>

A jam for teens and adults, ages 13 and up. The suggested donation is $5. Bring an instrument. This is not a kids' class. The same series lists Oct. 13, which is outside this week and the two after it.

The Jansen's fine-art page lists building hours Tuesday through Saturday, 9:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m., closed Sunday and Monday. The meetup starts after that close, so use the event listing for the evening door, not the gallery hours. No children's class is posted at the Jansen inside this span.

[Acoustic meetup](https://www.jansenartcenter.org/events/acoustic-music-meet-up/)

## Next Friday

Preschool storytime returns Oct. 2 at 10:30 a.m. The same afternoon, All About Bats is 3:30–4:30 p.m. for grades K–5.
