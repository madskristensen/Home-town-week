Friday night is the free downtown ArtWalk. Wednesday is pumpkin storytime. Sunday is a contra dance.

## Friday night

### 2nd Friday ArtWalk
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 5:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown Coeur d'Alene</p>

Free, every second Friday. Galleries, pop-up art, live music, and shops. Sponsored by STCU. This Friday is the October date.

[Visit Coeur d'Alene](https://coeurdalene.org/events/2nd-friday-artwalk/2026-10-09/)

Tuesday is still baby storytime, plus LEGO club at 4:00 p.m. for ages 6–12. Thursday is toddler storytime. Friday, Oct. 9, is on the preschool list.

## Wednesday

### I Survived: The Black Death
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 1:00 p.m. and 4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Children's library, Coeur d'Alene Public Library</p>

Ages 6–12. Activities from Lauren Tarshis's book. October's session is medieval medicine, not a scary storytime for preschoolers.

### Pumpkin Patch Storytime
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 2:00–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Prairie Home Farm, 7790 Atlas Rd, Coeur d'Alene</p>

Ages 3–5. Outside, so dress warmly. The Community Library Network lists it under the Hayden library, and the address is in Coeur d'Alene. The 2:00 p.m. start overlaps the 1:00 p.m. I Survived session. The 4:00 p.m. I Survived session is after the patch.

[Pumpkin Patch Storytime](https://communitylibrary.libcal.com/event/17330645)

## Thursday

### Ocean ZOOniversity
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 8 · 4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Coeur d'Alene Public Library</p>

Ages 6–8. October's dates are Oct. 8 and Oct. 22.

[Autumn children's programs](https://cdalibrarykids.substack.com/p/autumn-childrens-programming-at-the)

## Weekend

### Paws to Read
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Children's library</p>

All ages. Read to a therapy dog. The other Saturdays in the series are Sep. 12 and Nov. 14.

### Ukulele Club
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 3:00–4:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Nelson Room</p>

All ages, with Brittany Clapp-Abdella. Beginners can learn tuning and a few chords. The same Saturday slot returns Nov. 14.

### Sunday Matinee Contra Dance
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 11 · 2:00–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Coeur d'Alene Public Library</p>

All ages. Free, with the Spokane Folklore Society. This Sunday is Arvid Lundin and Deep Roots. A caller walks the lines. No partner required in the usual contra setup, but the newsletter does not spell out a dress code.
