Thanksgiving Point's Spooktober nights start this week. Cornbelly's is still open. Wednesday is Chapter Chats at the library.

## Thanksgiving Point

Spooktober runs the month of October. These are the sessions posted inside this week. Members are free. Everyone else pays regular admission. Scarecrow Festival at Ashton Gardens is Oct. 12–17, just after this span.

### Barnyard Boo
<p class="event-when">Mon Oct 5 · 10:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Curiosity Farms, Thanksgiving Point</p>

Costumes, the train, pumpkin painting, a scavenger hunt, and pumpkin bowling. Barnyard Bash dance parties are at 10:30 a.m. and 1:00 p.m. Later Mondays in the series are Oct. 12, 19, and 26.

### Pumpkin Glow Nights
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 5:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Museum of Natural Curiosity</p>

Costume night with pumpkin activities, crafts, and stories, tied to the Thomas and Friends exhibit. Later Wednesdays are Oct. 14, 21, and 28.

### Dinos after Dark
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 8 · 5:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Museum of Natural Curiosity</p>

Club Cryptid: glow sticks, a hunt, and dance parties at 6:30 and 7:30 p.m. Later Thursdays are Oct. 15, 22, and 29.

### Creatures of the Night
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 5:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Butterfly Biosphere</p>

Roach races, a tarantula feeding, and a Courage Cave challenge. Costume dance parties are at 6:30 and 7:45 p.m. Later Fridays are Oct. 16, 23, and 30.

[Spooktober](https://thanksgivingpoint.org/event/spooktober/)

### Cornbelly's Corn Maze and Pumpkin Fest
<p class="event-when">Open this week · season through Oct. 31</p>
<p class="event-place">3003 N Thanksgiving Way</p>

Still the ticketed maze and pumpkin festival. Check that day's hours. Admission closes one hour early.

[Lehi calendar](https://cornbellys.com/pages/calendar)

## Wednesday

### Chapter Chats
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 4:00–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Programming Space, 131 N 100 E</p>

Grades 4–6. Bring last month's book. Registration is required, and it opens at 10:00 a.m. on Monday, Oct. 5. Park in the lot south of the building. The library notes that photos may be taken at activities.

[Chapter Chats](https://lehicity.libcal.com/event/17546541)

## Friday

### Music and Movement Storytime
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 10:30–11:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lehi Public Library</p>

Kids on the Move lists Fridays through Nov. 20 except Oct. 16. Oct. 9 is on. Oct. 16 is off.

[Kids on the Move](https://kotm.org/programs/learning-opportunities/)
