Sunday is Tbilisoba at Centennial Center Park. Kids under 12 are free. Wednesday is still Spanish family storytime at Smoky Hill.

## Sunday

### Tbilisoba Festival
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 12:00–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Centennial Center Park, 13050 E Peakview Ave</p>

A Georgian culture day from the Colorado Georgian Community Association: music, dance, and food. The concert block is 3:00–5:00 p.m. Children under 12 are free. The ticket page lists general admission at $15 online and $20 at the door. Georgian wine is part of the afternoon, so it is a family festival with an adult table.

[Tickets](https://ticketstripe.com/events/5665108868207288)

### Spanish Family Storytime / Hora de cuentos para la familia
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 4:00–4:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Smoky Hill Library, 5430 S Biscay Circle</p>

Reading, singing, rhymes, and movement. Planned for ages 0–5. Older children are welcome. Children under 8 need an adult. The event page says the library is open 9:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m. that Wednesday. Child and Family Library Services: 303-542-7279.

[Spanish Family Storytime](https://arapahoelibraries.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6ab13eaa19094f0c6939215d)
