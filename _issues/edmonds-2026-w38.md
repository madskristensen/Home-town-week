Saturday stacks the museum market with a downtown sidewalk sale. Friday night, Driftwood Players open the season.

## Saturday downtown

### Edmonds Museum Summer Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 19 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">118 5th Ave N, in front of the Edmonds Historical Museum</p>

The Edmonds Historical Museum hosts this street market on Saturdays from May through October. About 90 vendors. The museum says each one is a family-owned business, and that farmers grow or raise what they bring. Produce, crafts, art, and food.

Rain or shine is the usual pattern for this market. Two Saturdays remain in September after this one, and the season continues into October.

[Edmonds Historical Museum](https://historicedmonds.org/edmonds-museum-summer-market/)

### Edmonds Brouhaha sidewalk sale
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 19 and Sun Sep 20</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown Edmonds sidewalks</p>

The Edmonds Downtown Alliance calls this the Brouhaha Backroom Blowout: past-season discounts from nearly 20 local shops. Named stops include Walnut Street Coffee, Molly Moon Ice Cream, Musicology Co, and The Wooden Spoon, plus galleries and gift shops.

Explore Edmonds lists Saturday as 10:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. The alliance says to check each store for hours, including Sunday. Do the market on 5th in the morning, then walk the sale.

[Downtown Alliance](https://edmondsdowntown.org/edmonds-brouhaha/)

## Beach after the market

Brackett's Landing is the beach at the end of Main Street, next to the ferry terminal. No program is posted for this week. It is the short walk if the sidewalk sale is enough shopping.

## Shows

### Queen: It's A Kinda Magic
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 16 · 7:30–9:45 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Center for the Arts, 410 Fourth Ave N</p>

A Queen tribute from Australia, with live vocals, piano, and guitar. Explore Edmonds lists the show through 9:45 p.m. This is a loud rock night, not a children's concert.

[Edmonds Center for the Arts](https://www.edmondscenterforthearts.org/)

### Jon Lovitz
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 17 · 7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Edmonds Center for the Arts, 410 Fourth Ave N</p>

Stand-up from the Saturday Night Live alum. Explore Edmonds describes satire and characters, and lists the evening through 9:30 p.m. Adult comedy. The box office is (425) 275-9595.

[Event page](https://www.edmondscenterforthearts.org/events/jon-lovitz/)

### A Murder Is Announced
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 18 and Sat Sep 19 · 8:00 p.m., Sun Sep 20 · 2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Wade James Theatre, 950 Main St</p>

Edmonds Driftwood Players open their 68th season with Leslie Darbon's adaptation of the Agatha Christie novel. The run is Sep 18–Oct 11. Regular performances are Thursday, Friday, and Saturday at 8:00 p.m., and Sunday at 2:00 p.m.

About 2 hours, including a 15-minute intermission. Driftwood says the show has some mild adult themes and is best for teens and up. Mainstage tickets are $31 or $28, plus a $3 fee per ticket. TeenTix is $5. The lobby opens one hour before curtain.

[Driftwood Players](https://edmondsdriftwoodplayers.org/shows-tickets/)

## Museum hours

Cascadia Art Museum is open Wednesday through Sunday, 11:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., at 190 Sunset Ave S. Adults $15, seniors 65+ $10, and youth 18 and under and students free. The next exhibition, Zoë Dusanne: The Art of Seeing, opens Wed Sep 23, so it is not up yet this week.

[Cascadia Art Museum](https://www.cascadiaartmuseum.org/upcoming-exhibitions/)

## Later this month

The Saturday market returns Sep 26 and Oct 3. Dolly Day is Fri Sep 25 downtown, mostly in pubs, with a book drive for school libraries. Artisan Alley sets up inside the Old Opera House on Sat Sep 26. Music in the Museum is that evening at Cascadia. Write on the Sound, the city's writers' conference, is Oct 2–4 at the Frances Anderson Center, with an online pre-conference on Sep 26.
