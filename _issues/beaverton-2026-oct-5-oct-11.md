Friday is a no-school day, with a photography camp at the Nature Center. Saturday stacks the Family Resource Fair, a spider class for little kids, storytime, and a free night market.

## A no-school Friday

Beaverton schools are closed Friday, Oct. 9, for a staff day.

### Nature photography day-off camp
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Tualatin Hills Nature Center, 15655 SW Millikan Way</p>

Day-off camps are listed for ages 7–13. This one is fall color in the park, nature photos, and a frame for a favorite picture. Lunch, water, and snacks. In-district is $92.

[THPRD nature camps](https://www.thprd.org/activities/keywords/THNCNEC)

### Artist in DAM: Palmarin Merges
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 12:00–1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library makerspace</p>

All ages. Make a moveable collage figure. Supplies are limited. DAM open lab that day is 1:00–5:00 p.m.

### Friday Art Fun
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 4:00–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Murray Scholls</p>

Ages 2–8 with family.

### Japanese Storytime
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Murray Scholls children's room</p>

Ages 6 and under with family.

### Mandarin Chinese Storytime
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 9 · 11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main storytime room</p>

Ages 6 and under with family. Main is at 12375 SW 5th Street. This is a different branch from the Japanese session, so you cannot do both.

[Storytimes](https://www.beavertonlibrary.org/381/Storytimes) · [Youth flyer](https://www.beavertonlibrary.org/DocumentCenter/View/13360/2026-09-10-Sept-Oct-ADA)

## Saturday in town

### Family Resource Fair
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

Free. Organizations that serve families with kids 10 and under, plus activities, free books, and a raffle. It overlaps the farmers market next door, so you can do both.

[Family Resource Fair](https://www.beavertonlibrary.org/264/Family-Resource-Fair)

### Creature Features: Silly Spiders
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:00–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Nature Center courtyard</p>

Ages 2–5 with an adult. Story, a simple craft, and a short walk. Siblings under 2 can come at no extra cost. In-district is $12. This is the same hour as the resource fair, at a different place.

[THPRD Creature Features](https://www.thprd.org/activities/keywords/thnccf/asc)

### Beaverton Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 8:30 a.m.–1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">SW Hall Blvd between 3rd and 5th</p>

No band is listed. The music page says later October markets usually skip live music.

[Farmers Market](https://www.beavertonfarmersmarket.com/)

### Vietnamese Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main storytime room</p>

Ages 6 and under with family.

### Danza Azteca storytelling
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 1:00–1:45 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Murray Scholls</p>

"The Corn People," for children and families. Rattles and a chance to join the story. Family Storytime is the same hour at Main, so pick a branch.

### Danza Azteca at Main
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 4:00–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main library</p>

Dancers, feathered regalia, and a huehuetl drum. The audience is invited in at the end. All ages, and the flyer says every library event is free.

### Everything Funky night market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 5:00–9:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Farmers market grounds, 12375 SW 5th Street</p>

The October theme is Stranger Things. The Downtown Association lists local vendors, food, live music, and family entertainment. Admission is free. The event page also shows a series span from the September market, so use this Saturday's theme and the 5:00 p.m. start in the description.

[Night market](https://downtownbeaverton.org/event/everything-funky-neighborhood-night-market/)
