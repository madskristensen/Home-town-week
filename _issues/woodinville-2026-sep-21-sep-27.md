Saturday is the last farmers market of the season on Festival Street. The library's baby and family storytimes on the calendar land the week after next.

## Saturday market

### Woodinville Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Festival Street, 13205 NE 175th St</p>

The Woodinville Chamber lists this Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. The same listing says the market runs Saturdays, May through September. September 26 is the last Saturday in that window, so treat it as the close of the season. The chamber page does not add an October date.

[Chamber listing](https://woodinvillechamber.org/calendar/woodinville-farmers-market/2026-09-26/)

## At the library

Woodinville Library is at 17105 Avondale Road NE. The branch page lists Monday 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., Tuesday and Wednesday noon–8:00 p.m., Thursday and Friday 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., Saturday 11:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., and Sunday closed.

The next baby and toddler storytime on that calendar is Thursday, Oct. 8. Family storytime is Friday, Oct. 9. Neither falls inside Sept. 21–27.

[Woodinville Library](https://kcls.org/locations/woodinville/)

## Outside

Cottage Lake Park is the easy walk if the market is enough downtown time. No ticketed program is posted there this week.

## The week after next

Baby and toddler storytime is Thu Oct 8, 10:30–11:30 a.m., for newborns to 24 months with a caregiver. Family storytime is Fri Oct 9, 11:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m., all ages with an adult. Saturday, Oct. 10, is Harvest Fest at 21 Acres.
