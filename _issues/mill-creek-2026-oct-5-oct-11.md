Saturday morning is a Halloween edition of Baby Dance Club. The weekday storytimes repeat, and Friday afternoon is a teen craft.

Mill Creek Library is at 15429 Bothell-Everett Hwy. Parking may be limited through November 2026. Early storytimes still use the 9:15–9:35 a.m. door for families, before the 10:00 a.m. open.

## Saturday

### Baby Dance Club
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 9:30–10:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Mill Creek Library</p>

A Halloween edition, presented by Cat. Costumes are encouraged for babies and caregivers. It is a babywearing dance for parents and caregivers of babies from birth to 18 months, or as long as the baby is comfortable being carried. The hour is 30 minutes of dance and 30 minutes of play. Saturday's regular open is 10:00 a.m., so the start is before that. Read the event page for the door time. The description was cut off at "doors" in the short feed.
