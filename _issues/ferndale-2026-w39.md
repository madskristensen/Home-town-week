Saturday afternoon is free painting at the library. The same weekend, the rock club sells specimens at Pioneer Pavilion.

## Saturday at the library

### Tiny Canvas Painting
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 2:00–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Ferndale Library, 2125 Main St</p>

A drop-in, self-guided painting session on tiny canvases. All ages. Children need an adult with them. Materials are provided, one canvas per person. Free. Registration is encouraged so the Friends of the Ferndale Library, who sponsor it, have enough supplies. It is not required.

Finished paintings go into the Tiny Art Show at the library for October and can be picked up the first week of November.

The library's posted hours are Monday–Thursday 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m., Friday 9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., Saturday 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., and Sunday 1:00–5:00 p.m. The live grid sometimes closes about 15 minutes sooner.

[Chamber listing](http://business.ferndale-chamber.com/events/details/tiny-canvas-painting-11359) · [Library event](https://wcls.libcal.com/event/17002446)

## Rock weekend

### Rockhound Rendezvous
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 and Sun Sep 27</p>
<p class="event-place">Pioneer Pavilion Community Center, 2007 Cherry St</p>

The Mount Baker Rock & Gem Club says the autumn sale is Sep 26–27 at the pavilion, in front of Pioneer Park. Members sell or swap rocks, jewelry, tools, and other rock-related items. The public is invited to shop. Sales are usually cash, paid to the member at the table.

The club says there is no food for sale, so bring a lunch. Public hours are not posted the way the March gem show posts Saturday 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. and Sunday 10:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Do not assume those hours. The city's facility calendar shows the pavilion reserved from Fri Sep 25, 1:00 p.m., through Sun Sep 27, 6:00 p.m., which can include setup. Check the club site before you go.

## Storytime and practice

### Toddler Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 21 · 9:30–10:00 a.m. and 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Ferndale Library large meeting room</p>

Ages 1–3, about 20 minutes. The two Monday sessions are the same program: concept books, simple stories, and movement.

[9:30 session](https://wcls.libcal.com/event/16945225) · [10:30 session](https://wcls.libcal.com/event/16945254)

### Witches Dance
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 6:30–7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Pioneer Pavilion, 2007 Cherry St</p>

Free weekly practice through Oct 27. Registration required. Ages 12 and under stay with a parent or guardian. Four practices qualifies you to perform later. Performing is optional.

The pavilion is also the rock sale site later this week. Tuesday practice is before the weekend reservation.

[Witches Dance](https://cityofferndale.org/472/Ferndale-Witches-Dance)

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Ferndale Library large meeting room</p>

Ages 3–5, 30 minutes. Longer stories, rhymes, and songs. The next Friday is Oct 2.

[Preschool Storytime](https://wcls.libcal.com/event/16953399)

## Outside
