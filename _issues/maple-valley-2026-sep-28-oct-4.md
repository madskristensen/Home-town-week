Wednesday evening is pajama storytime. Thursday morning is family storytime. Saturday is the market at the Legacy Site.

Maple Valley Library is at 21844 SE 248th Street. Wednesday the branch is open noon–8:00 p.m., so a 7:00 p.m. storytime is inside regular hours. Thursday opens at 10:00 a.m.

## Storytime

### Pajama Story Time
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 7:00–7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Maple Valley Library</p>

Librarian Sharon reads for newborns through age 7, with an adult. Bring a stuffed animal or a blanket. Registration is not required.

[Pajama Story Time](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a5a99914523092f0030cce6)

### Family Story Time
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Maple Valley Library</p>

Stories for ages 3–6. Younger and older siblings are welcome. Stay for play after. Registration is not required. Thursday opens at 10:00 a.m., so this starts half an hour after the doors.

[Family Story Time](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a5a998f0e562e28009a98ef)

## Homework help

### Tutors
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 and Wed Sep 30 · 5:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Maple Valley Library</p>

Drop-in homework help for grades K–12. Tuesday and Wednesday the library is open noon–8:00 p.m.

[Tuesday tutors](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7a362488e9bf280036f381) · [Wednesday tutors](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7a362488e9bf280036f382)

## Saturday market

### Maple Valley Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Legacy Site, 25719 Maple Valley Black Diamond Road SE</p>

The season runs Saturdays through Oct. 31. This is one of the October dates.

[Maple Valley Farmers Market](https://www.maplevalleyfarmersmarket.com/)
