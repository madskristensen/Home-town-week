Monday morning is preschool storytime, twice. Tuesday night is pajamas, milk, and cookies. Saturday is the market at City Hall.

Pinnacle Library is at 1375 E Phenomenal Street. The district phone on the storytime pages is (208) 888-4451.

## Monday

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 21 · 10:00–10:30 a.m. and 11:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Pinnacle Program Room A+B</p>

Early literacy: read, write, talk, sing, play, and count. The series lists Mondays including Sep. 21, Sep. 28, and Oct. 5.

[10:00 session](https://www.mld.org/event/preschool-storytime-68974) · [11:00 session](https://www.mld.org/event/preschool-storytime-68962)

## Tuesday

### Pajama Storytime
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 7:00–7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Pinnacle Program Room A+B</p>

Wear pajamas, bring a stuffed friend, and the library lists milk and cookies. Weekly on Tuesdays, including Sep. 22, Sep. 29, and Oct. 6.

[Pajama Storytime](https://www.mld.org/event/pajama-time-68274)

## Saturday

### Meridian Main Street Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 9:00 a.m.–1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">33 E Broadway Avenue, by City Hall</p>

Saturdays, Apr. 11 through Oct. 17. Produce, handmade goods, food, and live music. Rain or shine unless there is thunder or lightning. The market asks families to expect produce later in summer than in spring.

[Main Street Market](https://meridianmainstreetmarket.com/home)
