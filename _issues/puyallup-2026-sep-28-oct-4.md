Thursday morning is the first week of toddler and preschool storytime. Saturday is the market, with music in the park.

## Thursday at the library

Both sessions are in the Puyallup Public Library meeting room. The series runs Thursdays from Oct. 1 through Nov. 12. The street address is not on these event pages.

### Toddler Treasures
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1 · 9:45–10:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Puyallup Public Library meeting room</p>

Stories for ages 1–3. The Oct. 1 listing is the first date in the Thursday series, which also names Oct. 8.

[Toddler Treasures](https://puyalluplibrary.libcal.com/event/16206996)

### Little Learners
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1 · 10:45–11:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Puyallup Public Library meeting room</p>

Stories for ages 3–6, half an hour after Toddler Treasures ends. Same series, same room.

[Little Learners](https://puyalluplibrary.libcal.com/event/16207003)

## Saturday market

### Puyallup Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Pioneer Park and Pavilion</p>

The season runs through Oct. 10, so this is the second-to-last Saturday. Music is 11:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. The market experience page posts this date as @robertleslie.

The market site says 300 S Meridian. The city calendar says 330 S Meridian. Both are Pioneer Park.

[Puyallup Farmers Market](https://puyallupfarmersmarket.com/) · [Market experience](https://puyallupfarmersmarket.com/market-experience/)

## Next Saturday

Oct. 10 is the last market of the season. The same morning, the library lists an ESL hour from 9:00 to 11:00 a.m. Those are different stops.
