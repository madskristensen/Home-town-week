Storytimes resume at the Central Library and at Bellis Fair. The Friends book sale fills the Lecture Room Thursday through Saturday. Friday night is a parent concert at the Mount Baker Theatre.

## Storytimes, from Monday

### Little Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 21 and Tue Sep 22 · 10:05, 10:35, and 11:35 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library, 210 Central Ave</p>

Birth to age 3 with an adult. Simple stories, songs, fingerplays, and movement. The series runs Mondays through Nov 16 and Tuesdays through Nov 17. Pick one session. Free.

### Library Explorers
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 21 · 3:30–4:15 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lecture Room, Central Library</p>

Ages 6–10, families welcome. Stories, experiments, and crafts. Mondays through Nov 16, except this room is the book sale Thu Sep 24 through Sat Sep 26, so Monday is the Explorers day this week.

[September library programs](https://bellinghampubliclibrary.org/news/september-2026-bellingham-public-library-programs-and-events)

### Bellis Fair storytimes
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 23</p>
<p class="event-place">Multipurpose Room, Bellis Fair branch</p>

- Little Storytime, 10:05, 10:45, and 11:25 a.m., birth to age 3 with an adult.
- Preschool Storytime, 12:05–12:35 p.m., ages 3–5 with an adult, plus a simple craft.
- Baby Time, 2:30–3:30 p.m., birth to 12 months with a caregiver. This one is a six-week class and registration is required. No class on Oct 7.

If you enter the mall before 11:00 a.m., use the door by Target or the food court.

The same Wednesday, Builders Club is at the Fairhaven branch, 3:00–5:00 p.m. in the Fireplace Room. Ages 4–12. Legos, straw connectors, and Duplos are provided.

### Preschool Storytime downtown
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library</p>

Ages 3–5 with an adult, plus a simple craft. Thursdays through Nov 19.

### Baby Time downtown
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 10:30–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library</p>

Birth to 12 months with a caregiver. Songs, rhymes, stories, and baby play at the end. Registration is required. Fridays through Oct 30.

[September library programs](https://bellinghampubliclibrary.org/news/september-2026-bellingham-public-library-programs-and-events)

## The book sale

### Friends of the Library book sale
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.; Fri Sep 25 · 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., half price; Sat Sep 26 · 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m., $5 a bag</p>
<p class="event-place">Lecture Room, Central Library, 210 Central Ave</p>

Books, DVDs, and kids' titles. Cash and cards. Proceeds go to the library. Thursday morning preschool storytime is in the Dodson Room, not the Lecture Room, so the two can share the building.

Saturday's $5-bag hour ends at 3:00. The farmers market that morning is 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. at Depot Market Square, 1100 Railroad Ave, a short walk if you do the stalls first.

[September library programs](https://bellinghampubliclibrary.org/news/september-2026-bellingham-public-library-programs-and-events) · [Bellingham events](https://www.bellingham.org/events)
