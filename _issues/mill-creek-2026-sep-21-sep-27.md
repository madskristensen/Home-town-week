Tuesday and Thursday storytimes start before the library's regular open. Wednesday evening is family storytime, with Reading with Rover just before it.

## At the library

Mill Creek Library is at 15429 Bothell-Everett Hwy. The branch page lists Monday through Thursday 10:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m., Friday 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., Saturday 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., and Sunday 1:00–5:00 p.m. Parking may be limited through November 2026 because of construction.

The branch page listed these sessions for this span. The individual event pages have since rolled forward to the next week, so the clocks below are from that branch listing, and the links go to the same programs on their next dates.

### Toddler Storytime with Heather
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22 · 9:30–10:00 a.m. and 11:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Mill Creek Library</p>

Stories, music, and movement for toddlers, with play or art after. The continuing listing says doors open 9:15–9:35 a.m. for storytime families only, which is before the 10:00 a.m. open.
