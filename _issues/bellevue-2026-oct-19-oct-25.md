Wednesday morning is an owl class for preschoolers at the botanical garden, and this one still has seats. Wednesday evening is a kapa printmaking workshop for adults.

## Wednesday morning

### Kids in the Garden: Owls
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 21 · 10:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Bellevue Botanical Garden, 12001 Main St</p>

{% include event-photo.html
   src="/assets/images/bellevue/botanical-garden.webp"
   alt="The entrance sign and plantings at Bellevue Botanical Garden."
   credit="Photo: Heptazane, public domain"

Ages 3–5 with an adult. Who flies in the garden at night. Story, art, a song, snack, and time outside. Twelve spots. Adults free. A single session is $25 per child, or $17.50 for Bellevue Botanical Garden Society members. A second child is $18, or $12.60 for members.

Oct 7 and Oct 14 in this series were already marked full. The owl page still said spots were available. Register. Do not plan on walking in.

The next session, Winter is Coming, is Wednesday, Oct 28, which is the following week.

[Owls](https://65203.blackbaudhosting.com/65203/Kids-in-the-Garden-7-3-Owls) · [Series](https://bellevuebotanical.org/kids-in-the-garden/)

## Wednesday evening

### The Art of Hawaiian Kapa
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 21 · 6:00–7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Bellevue Library, Room 1, 1111 110th Ave NE</p>

Artist Malia Peoples talks about kapa, the traditional fabric of Hawaii, then participants stamp their own prints. Supplies are provided. Please register. The page said registration opens Oct 14 at 6:00 p.m., with 20 seats. Supported by the Bellevue Arts Commission and in part by 4Culture.

The listing is filed for adults. It is the parent workshop, not a second preschool class. It does not overlap the morning owl session.

[Kapa workshop](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6aa1ea1a129d8e0031f0fb66)

No farmers market this week. The library's Monday toddler storytime was on the calendar for Oct 5 and Oct 12. It was not on the pages used for Oct 19. Check the branch list before you count on it.
