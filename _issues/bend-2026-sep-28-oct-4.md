Northwest Crossing's Saturday market ended Sep. 26. Wednesday is still Brooks Alley, and Thursday morning is storytime at the Central Library.

## Wednesday

### Bend Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Brooks Alley, downtown Bend</p>

Same place and rules as last week. The season line says through Oct. 14. The hours block says through Oct. 7. Sep. 30 is inside both. Pets stay home. The $20 SNAP produce match is at the information booth.

[Bend Farmers Market](https://www.bendfarmersmarket.com/)

The district's storytime pages list these Thursday sessions at Central Library, on the 2nd floor youth programming space. Phone on those pages is 541-241-2630.
