Northwest Crossing's Saturday market ended Sep. 26. This weekend downtown is the Fall Festival, with a kids' theater set Saturday and Sunday morning. Wednesday is still the farmers market.

## Fall Festival

### Bend Fall Festival
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 5:00–10:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown Bend</p>

Friday is music and food only. The Hasbens play 5:30–6:30 p.m., Sun Dive at 7:00 p.m., and a Fleetwood Mac tribute at 8:30 p.m.

### Bend Fall Festival
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:00 a.m.–10:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown Bend</p>

BEAT Children's Theater is 11:00 a.m.–noon. School of Rock follows at 12:30 p.m. The rest of the day is improv, trivia, and bands through Low Down Brass Band at 8:30 p.m. Art in Action has slime, a pen bar, and pumpkin painting. The Harvest Bake-Off is new this year, with pie, bread, and chili.

### Bend Fall Festival
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 11:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Downtown Bend</p>

BEAT Children's Theater is back at 11:00 a.m. Lava City Cirque is 2:00–3:00 p.m. The festival site lists the hours and the lineup. It does not print a ticket price on the home page, so check the FAQ if you need one.

[Bend Fall Festival](https://bendfallfestival.com/) · [Old Mill festival list](https://www.oldmilldistrict.com/bend-events/festivals/)

## Wednesday

### Bend Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Brooks Alley, downtown Bend</p>

Same place and rules as last week. The season line says through Oct. 14. The hours block says through Oct. 7. Sep. 30 is inside both. Pets stay home. The $20 SNAP produce match is at the information booth.

[Bend Farmers Market](https://www.bendfarmersmarket.com/)

## Thursday at the Central Library

The district's storytime pages list these Thursday sessions at Central Library, on the 2nd floor youth programming space. Phone on those pages is 541-241-2630.

### Family Story Time
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1 · 9:30–9:50 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Central Library, youth programming, 2nd floor</p>

Books, songs, and rhymes. Ages 0–5. About 25 minutes. The Oct. 19 family storytime page lists this Thursday session in its calendar.

[Family Story Time listing](https://dpl.libnet.info/event/16790309)

### Toddler Story Time
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1 · 10:30–10:55 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Central Library, youth programming, 2nd floor</p>

Stories, songs, and movement rhymes. Ages 18–36 months. About 25 minutes.

[Toddler Story Time](https://dpl.libnet.info/event/16862793)
