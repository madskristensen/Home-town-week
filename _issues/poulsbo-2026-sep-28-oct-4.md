Tuesday and Wednesday are storytime. Saturday is the market, with the last Kids Club of the year, and a Fix-it Fair at the library the same morning.

Poulsbo Library is at 700 NE Lincoln Road.

## Storytime

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Poulsbo Library</p>

Ages 3–5.

[Preschool Storytime](https://krl.bibliocommons.com/events/6a67b129cca66c2f00a65b9b)

### Baby Storytime
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 · 1:00–1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Poulsbo Library</p>

Ages birth to 18 months. Same day as preschool storytime, after lunch.

[Baby Storytime](https://krl.bibliocommons.com/events/6a67b17ccca66c2f00a65bb8)

### Toddler Storytime
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 10:15–10:45 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Poulsbo Library</p>

Ages 18 months to 3.

[Toddler Storytime](https://krl.bibliocommons.com/events/6a67b2690d65ac3600429594)

Teen Space is the same Wednesday, 2:00–4:00 p.m. It is a teen hour, not a second storytime.

## Saturday, two places

### Poulsbo Farmers Market and Kids Club
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">18901 8th Ave NE</p>

Market hours are 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. Kids Club is free on the first Saturday, April through October, and Oct. 3 is the last one of the year. No separate Kids Club clock is posted, so plan it inside market hours. Town & Country sponsors the club. Live music runs through October.

[Market](https://www.poulsbofarmersmarket.org/) · [Kids Club](https://www.poulsbofarmersmarket.org/kidsclub)

### Fix-it Fair
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Poulsbo Library, 700 NE Lincoln Road</p>

Small appliances, lamps, and clothing. The last items are accepted at 1:30 p.m., half an hour before the posted end. This is at the library, not at 8th Ave NE. If you want both, they overlap from 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.

[Fix-it Fair](https://krl.bibliocommons.com/events/68d84fb5a0570c396d570f14)

## Next week

Toddler storytime returns Wednesday, Oct. 7, and LEGO Lounge is that afternoon, 3:30–4:30 p.m. The market on Oct. 10 does not include Kids Club.
