Friday morning is two short music sessions at the library with Lana Van Boven. Tuesday evening is drop-in homework help.

## Friday storytime

### Music and Movement with Lana Van Boven
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 10:00–10:30 a.m. and 10:45–11:15 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kenmore Library, 6531 NE 181st Street</p>

Lana Van Boven, a speech language pathologist and musician, uses music and movement to make stories come alive. All ages with an adult. Registration is not required. Space is limited. The two sessions are listed separately, so pick one. Friday the library opens at 10:00 a.m., which is the start of the first session.

[10:00 session](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a921c7a2dac6e0037228009) · [10:45 session](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a921c7a2dac6e003722800a)

## Homework, then English practice

### Tutors
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 29 · 6:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kenmore Library</p>

Volunteer tutors for homework, grades K–12. Students may drop in during the hours. Look for the tutor T-shirt. Tuesday the library is open noon–8:00 p.m.

[Tutors](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a76b24b3f18b57d52344d52)

### Talk Time
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 1 · 10:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kenmore Library</p>

English conversation practice. All levels. Registration is not required. This is an adult learner hour, not a storytime. Thursday opens at 10:00 a.m.

[Talk Time](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/events/69151a57886bb73600ba8630)
