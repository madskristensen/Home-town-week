The market homepage says the season runs May 2 through Oct 31, 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. The entertainment calendar names Larry Murante for 11:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. on Oct 17. That is a dated line, not the undated "this week" banner on the homepage.

Free to walk. The address is 9900 Willows Rd NE.

## What is not on the calendar

KCLS Redmond's branch page did not show upcoming storytimes or kids' workshops for these dates. Bigfoot Kids' Book Festival was the previous Saturday, Oct 10, not this one. Ragtime's Issaquah run ends Oct 18, at the Francis J. Gaudette Theatre, if you want a parent night in the next town. Visit Issaquah notes language and brief violence, and a run of about 2 hours 50 minutes. Not a preschool show. The captioned Issaquah performance is Sat Oct 17 at 2:00 p.m., which overlaps the market.

[Redmond Library](https://kcls.org/locations/redmond/) · [Ragtime](https://villagetheatre.org/mainstage/ragtime/)

Downtown Park and Marymoor are the weekday outing. No city festival is posted on top of them for this Monday–Sunday span.
