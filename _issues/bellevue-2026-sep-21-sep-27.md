Thursday is the farmers market. Saturday afternoon is a building playshop at KidsQuest. Sunday night, Meydenbauer hosts the Moon Festival Melody concert.

## Thursday market

### Bellevue Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 3:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">1717 Bellevue Way NE</p>

One Thursday left after this one. The main season ends Oct 1, still 3:00–7:00 p.m. Same lot, same information booth for SNAP and Market Match.

Downtown Park, at 10201 NE 4th St, is the playground stop if you are downtown before the stalls open. No separate park program is posted for Thursday.

[Bellevue Farmers Market](https://bellevuefarmersmarket.org/what-to-expect)

## Saturday at the museum

### Weekend Playshop
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">KidsQuest Children's Museum, 1116 108th Ave NE</p>

KidsQuest lists an educator-led playshop on building and creating: real tools and engineering with recycled materials. Geared to ages 5 and up. Free with museum admission or membership. An end time was not on the listing. Confirm the day's floor hours on the museum calendar before you buy tickets. The museum is a short walk from the Bellevue Transit Center.

[KidsQuest](https://www.kidsquestmuseum.org/)

## Meydenbauer

### Devi Mahotsavam
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · time on the calendar</p>
<p class="event-place">Meydenbauer Center, 11100 NE 6th St</p>

On the Meydenbauer theatre calendar for Saturday. The short listing does not put a start time or a price in the blurb used here. This is a ticketed performance, not a drop-in kids' program. Read the age note on the ticket page.

[Meydenbauer calendar](https://meydenbauer.com/theatre/attend-a-show/calendar-ticket-information/)

### Moon Festival Melody
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27</p>
<p class="event-place">Meydenbauer Center Performing Arts Theatre, 11100 NE 6th St</p>

The 12th Moon Festival Melody concert. This year's theme on the calendar is "In the Footsteps of Music: From East to West, From Classical to Modern." The program notes Moon Reflected on Second Spring, music from Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon, Piazzolla, and Tchaikovsky. Cellist Kai Chen, pianist Andrew Yang, percussionist Chan Tang, and the Seattle Festival Orchestra, with Yuchi Chou conducting.

The calendar blurb names the day and the theatre. It does not put a start time in that description. Check the ticket page. Better for older kids who can sit through a concert than for preschoolers. Saturday's playshop and Sunday's concert do not overlap.

[Meydenbauer calendar](https://meydenbauer.com/theatre/attend-a-show/calendar-ticket-information/)

Downtown is the easy night if the concert is the plan.

<p class="event-when">Hours on the restaurant site</p>
<p class="event-place">Lincoln Square, downtown Bellevue</p>

Dumplings, and a kids' menu on the Bellevue location page. No separate special was posted for this week. Go before the concert only if the ticket time leaves a gap. Confirm both clocks.

[Din Tai Fung Bellevue](https://dtf.com/en-us/locations/bellevue)
