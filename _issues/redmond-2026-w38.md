### Ragtime
<p class="event-when">Wed–Thu Sep 16–17 · 7:30 p.m. previews, Fri Sep 18 · 7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Francis J. Gaudette Theatre, Issaquah</p>

{% include event-photo.html
   src="/assets/images/redmond/ragtime.webp"
   alt="Three Ragtime performers on stage in period costume, with a woman in a feathered hat in front."
   credit="Photo: Village Theatre."
   source="https://villagetheatre.org/mainstage/ragtime/" %}

Village Theatre mainstage. Previews are Wednesday and Thursday, and opening night is Friday. The run continues through Oct 18. Confirm times with the box office. Pay-what-you-can seats are listed for some dates. TeenTix says all ages are welcome. Themes include immigration, class, and racial injustice, so it may not suit young children. Tickets from about $42 online.  
[Ragtime](https://villagetheatre.org/mainstage/ragtime/) · [Issaquah dates](https://villagetheatre.org/mainstage/ragtime-iss/) · [TeenTix](https://www.teentix.org/calendar/event/ragtime/)
