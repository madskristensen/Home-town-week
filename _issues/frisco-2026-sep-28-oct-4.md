Wednesday is a building-block hour at the Heritage Center. Friday is a spooky movie in the PGA District. Saturday is Oktoberfest at The Star, Pop Up STEM, and a one-night camp-out at Frisco Commons. Sunday the Allen Philharmonic plays a family concert at the library.

## Friday

### Movie night in the PGA District
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 2 · 7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">PGA District</p>

Visit Frisco's fall guide puts spooky movie nights on Oct. 2, 9, 16, 23, and 30, and it puts the Friday series at 7:00 p.m. It names Hocus Pocus and The Haunted Mansion as part of the October mix, not as a title for this exact Friday. Blankets help. Parking in the district is free.

[Visit Frisco](https://www.visitfrisco.com/blog/fall-frisco-your-guide-to-autumn-events)

## Saturday at The Star

### Frisco Oktoberfest
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 11:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">The Star</p>

Seventh year. Live music, dancing, and beer. Visit Frisco calls it a fall tradition. Parking in the Star District is free. Beer is part of the day, so it is a family outing with an adult side.

[Visit Frisco](https://www.visitfrisco.com/blog/fall-frisco-your-guide-to-autumn-events)

The library is at 8000 Dallas Parkway. Saturday hours that week are 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.

### Pop Up STEM: Habitats
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Community Event Room A, Frisco Public Library</p>

Grades K–5. Learn about habitats and ecosystems. The listing does not print an end time, so this issue does not add one. Come and go.

The family story time series page shows many September dates and then jumps ahead. It does not show a family story time on Oct. 3 in the dates reviewed for this issue.

[Frisco library events](https://friscolibrary.bibliocommons.com/v2/events)

## Wednesday blocks, Saturday night outside, Sunday music

### Walnut Wednesday: Stack Attack
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 30 · 10:00–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">The Depot, Frisco Heritage Center</p>

Build with blocks from prompts. The city page does not print an age range. Heritage Center programs the same weekend use 6455 Page Street.

[Stack Attack](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=7706)

### Frisco Camp Out
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 3 · 2:00 p.m. through Sun Oct 4 · 10:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Frisco Commons Park, 8000 McKinney Road</p>

One night outside: movies, s'mores, and the city says dinner and a light breakfast are covered. It starts the same afternoon as Oktoberfest and Pop Up STEM, so pick a lane or go to the park after the library hour.

[Camp Out](https://www.friscotexas.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=7671)

### Haunted Harmonies
<p class="event-when">Sun Oct 4 · 1:00–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Frisco Public Library, 8000 Dallas Parkway</p>

The Allen Philharmonic Orchestra. Live music and pre-concert activities. The library says there will not be jump scares. Sunday hours are 1:00–6:00 p.m., so this starts at the open.

[Haunted Harmonies](https://friscolibrary.bibliocommons.com/events/6aa076482dac6e003724ea40)
