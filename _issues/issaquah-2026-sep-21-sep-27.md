Saturday is the last farmers market of the season, with story time in the meadow and studios open around town. Sunday is a 5K, a 10K, and a kids' fun run.

## Last Saturday market

### Issaquah Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Pickering Barn, 1730 10th Ave NW</p>

Last Saturday of the 2026 barn season. Rain or shine, 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. The next downtown produce market is the Fall Farm Fresh series, which starts Oct 10, not this week. A holiday market is listed for Dec 12.  
[City calendar](https://www.issaquahwa.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=14513) · [2026 market dates](https://www.issaquahwa.gov/DocumentCenter/View/10059/2026-Issaquah-Farmers-Market-Rules-and-Regulations)

### Story Time at the market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 12:00–12:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Meadow near Pickering Barn</p>

Librarian Miss Mack, stories and songs. Dress for the weather and bring something to sit on. If it rains, story time moves to the Hay Barn. No registration.  
[City calendar](https://www.issaquahwa.gov/Calendar.aspx?EID=14631)

The city calendar also lists a Food Waste Prevention Workshop at Pickering Barn the same day, 10:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m.  
[City calendar list](https://www.issaquahwa.gov/Calendar.aspx?view=list&startDate=9/21/2026&enddate=9/27/2026)

Saturday is a [Discover Pass free day](https://www.visitissaquahwa.com/calendar/) at Washington State Parks. Sunday's race is not on that free day.

## Studios and the story walk

### Issaquah Open Studio Tour
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 10:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Studios around Issaquah, plus Blakely Hall</p>

Free, self-guided, third year. A jury picks artists who open home studios and other workspaces. New this year, an alumni art show. The market is at the barn in the morning. Studios are scattered, so treat them as the rest of the day.  
[City calendar](https://www.issaquahwa.gov/m/calendar/event/detail/14711) · [Art Commons](https://www.artcommons.org/openstudiotour) · [Visit Issaquah](https://www.visitissaquahwa.com/event/issaquah-open-studio-tour-2/)

### Story Stroll: Watercress
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26, and up through Oct 3</p>
<p class="event-place">Starts at Issaquah Library, 10 W Sunset Way</p>

Self-led pages of Watercress, by Andrea Wang and Jason Chin, from the library through historic downtown. KCLS lists Saturday as an all-day marker. The Downtown Issaquah Association has the stroll up Sep 5 through Oct 3.  
[KCLS event](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6a80b39d2dac6e00371f4f84)

### Rise & Shine Story Time
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Issaquah Library, 10 W Sunset Way</p>

Kids Out and About lists this date for the library series: stories, music, movement, and rhymes for newborns to 5 years, with an adult, plus play time after. No registration, space limited. The KCLS page confirmed for the following Thursday is linked on next week's issue. Confirm Sep 24 on the branch calendar if you are making a special trip.  
[Kids Out and About](https://seattle.kidsoutandabout.com/content/rise-and-shine-story-time-issaquah-library-1) · [Issaquah Library](https://kcls.org/locations/issaquah/)

## Sunday at the lake

### Run with the Fishes
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 5K and 10K at 9:30 a.m., kids' 1K at 10:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lake Sammamish State Park, 2182 NW Sammamish Rd</p>

Issaquah Rotary's family race. The organizer lists 5K and 10K at $50 from Aug 1 through race day, and the kids' 1K at $20 with a shirt. Early bird prices were lower and have passed. Beer garden, food trucks, and live music run 9:30 a.m.–12:00 p.m. A Discover Pass is required for parking. This is not the free state parks day.  
[Run with the Fishes](https://issaquahrunwiththefishes.com/)

### Ride the trolley
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 and Sun Sep 27 · 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Issaquah Train Depot, 78 1st Ave NE</p>

Last weekend in the Visit Issaquah listing, which runs weekends from August 1 through September. Salmon Days weekend is not on that list.  
[Visit Issaquah calendar](https://www.visitissaquahwa.com/calendar/)

## For adults

### The Legacy of Homer's Odyssey
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 4:00–5:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Issaquah Library, 10 W Sunset Way</p>

For adults. Lance Rhoades on the Odyssey and later versions of the story, including film. Friends of the Issaquah Library sponsors it. The description says registration is not required, and that you can register if you want a reminder. The page also shows a registration control.  
[KCLS event](https://kcls.bibliocommons.com/v2/events/6a7e2bf4be14820029873020)

### After 4 Band at Vino Bella
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 7:30–11:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Vino Bella, 99 Front St N</p>

Live music after the market and the studio tour. A wine bar, so this is a parent night out.  
[Visit Issaquah](https://www.visitissaquahwa.com/calendar/)

## On stage

### Ragtime
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 2:00 and 7:30 p.m., Sun Sep 27 · 2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Francis J. Gaudette Theatre, 303 Front St N</p>

The Sunday matinee overlaps the tail of Run with the Fishes. Visit Issaquah notes language and brief violence, and a run time of about 2 hours 50 minutes with intermission. May not suit young children. TeenTix lists these times among other dates through Oct 18. Confirm prices with the box office.  
[Ragtime](https://villagetheatre.org/mainstage/ragtime/) · [TeenTix](https://www.teentix.org/calendar/event/ragtime/)

Salmon are in the creek this month if you want a free stop at the hatchery, 125 W Sunset Way, on the way between errands. The festival weekend is next week.  
[Salmon Days](https://www.salmondays.org/)
