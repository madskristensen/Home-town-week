Monday night the Tower screens a ski film at 6:00 and 8:00 p.m., with a child's ticket. Wednesday is the farmers market on Brooks Alley. Saturday is the last Northwest Crossing market.

## Monday at the Tower

### Actually, It Is Rocket Science
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 21 · 6:00 p.m. and 8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Tower Theatre, 835 NW Wall St</p>

Teton Gravity Research's ski and snowboard film, not a kids' play. The presenter lists an early showing at 6:00 p.m., doors at 5:00, and a late showing at 8:00 p.m., doors at 7:30. The Tower's events list has both of those clocks on Sep. 21. The Tower's 8:00 p.m. page prices general admission at $22 for adults and $12 for a child, plus an $8 historic preservation fee. Ages 3 and under are free.

[8:00 p.m. listing](https://www.towertheatre.org/event/actually-it-is-rocket-science-9-21-26-8pm) · [Teton Gravity Research](https://tetongravity.myeventscenter.com/event/Bend-Or-Tower-Theatre-128536)

## Any day this week

### High Desert Museum
<p class="event-when">Open daily · 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">59800 S Highway 97</p>

March through October the museum is open 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Miguel Almeida's The Hands That Feed is on view through Oct. 11: murals, sculptures, and animations about the people who harvest food for local markets. Free with museum admission, not a separate timed ticket.

[Almeida exhibit](https://highdesertmuseum.org/events/exhibition-opening-miguel-almeida/)

## Wednesday

### Bend Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Wed Sep 23 · 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Brooks Alley, downtown Bend</p>

The market page's season line says every Wednesday, May 6 through Oct. 14. The hours block on the same page says May 6 through Oct. 7, 2026. Sep. 23 is inside both. It is a producers' market: produce, protein, and farm products, not jewelry or crafts. Pets are not allowed. SNAP shoppers can get a $20 Double Up Food Bucks match at the information booth.

Parking: the North and South Mirror Pond lots are behind the alley. The library staff ask market shoppers not to use designated library spots. Bike racks are by those lots and in The Commons Cafe courtyard. Routes 3 and 11 on Cascades East Transit serve downtown.

[Bend Farmers Market](https://www.bendfarmersmarket.com/)

## Saturday

### Northwest Crossing Saturday Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Northwest Crossing Drive, between Mt. Washington Drive and Compass Park</p>

The market's own page says the 2026 season is May 30 through Sep. 26. This Saturday is the last day. Dogs stay home. The page cites crowds and hot pavement.

[Northwest Crossing Farmers Market](https://www.nwxfarmersmarket.com/)
