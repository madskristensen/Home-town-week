Thursday night is storytime with stuffed animals. Saturday is the last market of the season at Heritage Park.

## Thursday

### Storytime with Stuffies
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 · 6:30–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Library Corner, Eagle Public Library, 100 N Stierman Way</p>

Ages birth to 5, with a caregiver. Stories and songs. Pajamas, a stuffed animal, and a snack are optional. The series is Thursday evenings. The library phone is (208) 939-6814.

[Storytime with Stuffies](https://boisemom.com/series/storytime-with-stuffies-eagle-public-library/)

## Saturday

### Eagle Saturday Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Heritage Park, 185 E State Street</p>

The city lists Saturdays, May 2 through Sep. 26, 2026, with no market on June 27 or July 4. This Saturday is the last day. Live music begins at 10:00 a.m. Produce, flowers, and handmade goods. The recreation desk is (208) 489-8763.

[Eagle Saturday Market](https://cityofeagle.org/170/Eagle-Saturday-Market)
