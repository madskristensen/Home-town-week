Thursday, Friday, and Saturday are the last three days of a free pom-pom critter craft at the Lacey Museum.

## At the museum

### Creativity Corner: pom-pom critters
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 24 and Fri Sep 25 · 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. · Sat Sep 26 · 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lacey Museum and Cultural Center</p>

Lacey Parks lists Creativity Corner from Thursday, Sep. 3, through Saturday, Sep. 26. The hours on that listing are Thursday and Friday 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m., and Saturday 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. It is free and posted for all ages. This week is the last Thursday, Friday, and Saturday of the run. The parks page does not add a street address for the museum, so this note stops at the museum name.

[Lacey Parks events](https://laceyparks.org/events/)

## Outside

Long Lake Park is the walk if you want time outdoors after the craft. No ticketed program is posted at the park this week.

## Next week

Children's Day is Saturday, Oct. 3, 11:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m. at Huntamer Park. The same morning, Birth to Five storytime is at Lacey Library, 11:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. Those are different places.
