## Saturday market

### Puyallup Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Pioneer Park and Pavilion</p>

The market site lists Saturdays, 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., from Apr. 18 through Oct. 10, 2026, closed June 13 for Meeker Days. Music is posted from 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. September 26 names Dustin Lafferty.

The address is written two ways. The market site says 300 S Meridian. The city calendar says 330 S Meridian. Both point at Pioneer Park and the pavilion. Use the park, and check the sign at the gate if a street number matters for parking.

[Puyallup Farmers Market](https://puyallupfarmersmarket.com/) · [Market experience](https://puyallupfarmersmarket.com/market-experience/)

### Zine and Comic Workshop
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 11:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Puyallup Public Library meeting room</p>

Drop in and make a zine or a comic with the library's supplies. The listing is open to anyone, not a graded kids' class, and it overlaps the market's music hour. The library's street address is not on the event line used here.

[City calendar](https://www.puyallupwa.gov/calendar.aspx)

## Storytime waits

The library's toddler and preschool storytimes are a Thursday series that starts Oct. 1 and runs through Nov. 12, in the Puyallup Public Library meeting room. Nothing in that series falls on Sep. 24. Do not plan a Thursday storytime this week.

The library's street address is not on the event pages used here, so this note stops at the meeting room.

## Next week

Toddler Treasures is Thu Oct 1, 9:45–10:15 a.m., ages 1–3. Little Learners follows at 10:45–11:15 a.m., ages 3–6. The market returns Sat Oct 3.
