Fair on 44th was last Saturday. This week the plan is the library next to City Hall and a loop at Scriber Lake Park.

## What already happened

### Fair on 44th
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 12 · 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">44th Ave W, in front of the civic campus, 19100 44th Ave W</p>

The city closed 44th from 188th St SW to 194th St SW for a free block party: trucks to climb on, a food drive, crafts, and department booths. That date is the Saturday before this issue. It does not repeat this weekend. If a flyer is still in a window, it is last week's.

[Fair on 44th](https://www.lynnwoodwa.gov/Community/Events-Calendar/Fair-on-44th)

### Scriber Lake Park
<p class="event-when">Daylight hours</p>
<p class="event-place">Scriber Lake Park</p>

{% include event-photo.html
   src="/assets/images/lynnwood/scriber-creek.webp"

   credit="Photo: PinchyCC, CC BY 4.0"

No festival is posted at the lake this week. The loop, the boardwalk, and the creek are the outing. Trails can be muddy after rain. The city parks page is the map.

[Lynnwood parks](https://www.lynnwoodwa.gov/Community/Play-Lynnwood)

The recreation center is on the civic campus, with the entrance from 188th. Drop-in pool and gym hours change by season. They are on the city's recreation page, not a one-day special this week.

[Recreation classes](https://www.lynnwoodwa.gov/Community/Play-Lynnwood/Lynnwood-Recreation-Classes-Activities)
