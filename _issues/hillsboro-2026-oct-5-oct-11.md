Wednesday evening is Harvest Fest at Reed's Crossing. Saturday adds Vietnamese storytime at Brookwood before the downtown market.

## Harvest Fest

### Reed's Crossing at Tamarack Park
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 4:30–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Tamarack Park, 7250 SE Tamarack Street</p>

This is the 1st Wednesday market, and the season page runs those Wednesdays from May 6 through Oct. 7. The Harvest Fest listing adds pumpkin games, hot cocoa, and a scavenger hunt alongside the vendors.

[Harvest Fest](https://hillsboromarkets.org/harvestfest)

### The Streets at Tanasbourne
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 8 · 4:00–8:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">The Streets at Tanasbourne</p>

Oct. 8 is the 2nd Thursday. The Thursday market runs through Oct. 22.

[Hillsboro Farmers' Markets](https://hillsboromarkets.org/)

## Brookwood Library

Brookwood is at 2850 NE Brookwood Pkwy.

### Toddler Storytime
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 · 10:15–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Brookwood Library</p>

Ages 2–3.

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 8 · 10:15–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Brookwood Library</p>

Ages 3–6. This is the same morning as the library's usual open, and it is before the Tanasbourne market that evening.

### Vietnamese Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:15–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Brookwood Library</p>

About 30 to 40 minutes of stories, fingerplays, and movement. The parenting calendar lists this date for ages 0–6, in Vietnamese and English notes.

[Vietnamese Storytime](https://parentingtogetherwc.org/event/vietnamese-storytime/2026-10-10/)
