Wednesday morning is family storytime, then baby storytime, then the market on Brooks Alley. Saturday is family storytime again.

## Wednesday

### Family Story Time
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 9:30–9:55 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Central Library, youth programming, 2nd floor</p>

Books, songs, and rhymes. Ages 0–5.

### Baby Story Time
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Central Library, youth programming, 2nd floor</p>

A gentle storytime for an infant and a caregiver. Ages 0–18 months.

The Saturday family storytime page lists both of these Wednesday sessions.

[Family Story Time](https://dpl.libnet.info/event/16862846)

### Bend Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Brooks Alley, downtown Bend</p>

Oct. 7 is the last Wednesday named in the hours block. The season line on the same site still says Oct. 14. If you are going the following Wednesday, check the market site that week. Pets are not allowed. Do not park in the library's designated spots.

[Bend Farmers Market](https://www.bendfarmersmarket.com/)

## Saturday

### Family Story Time
<p class="event-when">Sat Oct 10 · 10:30–10:55 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Central Library, youth programming, 2nd floor</p>

Ages 0–5. About 25 minutes. The same day's listing also shows a Pokémon GO meetup at 10:00–11:00 a.m. in Meeting 3 on the 3rd floor. That one overlaps the storytime, so a family would pick one.

[Saturday Family Story Time](https://dpl.libnet.info/event/16862846)
