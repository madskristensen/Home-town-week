### Parachute Party

<p class="event-place">Haggard Library program room, 2501 Coit Road</p>

Ages 3–5. Rhymes and songs with giant parachutes. Haggard phone: 972-769-4250.

[Parachute Party](https://plano.libnet.info/event/16734013)

### Puppet Show: Big Pumpkin
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Parr Library program room, 6200 Windhaven Parkway</p>

Ages 0–5. A witch cannot pick a big pumpkin alone, and friends help. Parr phone: 972-769-4300.

[Big Pumpkin](https://plano.libnet.info/event/16722488)
