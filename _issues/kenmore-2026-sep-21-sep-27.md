No in-person kids' program is posted at Kenmore Library for Sept. 21–27. The week is the pier at Log Boom Park and the beach at Saint Edward.

## Library, then Friday next week

Kenmore Library is at 6531 NE 181st Street. The branch page lists Monday 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., Tuesday and Wednesday noon–8:00 p.m., Thursday and Friday 10:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., Saturday 11:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., and Sunday closed.

The next in-person kids' listing on that calendar is Music and Movement with Lana Van Boven on Friday, Oct. 2, in two short sessions at 10:00 and 10:45 a.m. Tutors, a drop-in homework hour for grades K–12, start Tuesday, Sep. 29, at 6:00 p.m. Both are outside this span.

Some titles on the branch widget are marked online, including a Korean storytime on Sep. 28. Those are not a reason to drive to 181st.

That series does not include a date inside this week. No other ticketed program is posted at either park for Sept. 21–27.
