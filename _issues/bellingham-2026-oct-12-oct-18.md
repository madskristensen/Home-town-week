The library's fall storytimes keep their usual clocks. Friday night is Randy Rainbow on the Mount Baker Theatre main stage. Saturday is the market.

## Library clocks

### Little Storytime
<p class="event-when">Mon Oct 12 and Tue Oct 13 · 10:05, 10:35, and 11:05 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library, 210 Central Ave</p>

Birth to age 3 with an adult. The storytimes page lists 11:05 a.m. for the third sitting. A September programs note said 11:35 a.m. Confirm the last slot if you are coming only for that one.

### Library Explorers
<p class="event-when">Mon Oct 12 · 3:30–4:15 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lecture Room, Central Library</p>

Ages 6–10, families welcome. The Monday series continues through Nov 16.

### Bellis Fair
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 14</p>
<p class="event-place">Multipurpose Room, Bellis Fair branch</p>

This Wednesday is back on. Oct 7 was the skip. Little Storytime at 10:05, 10:45, and 11:25 a.m. Preschool Storytime at 12:05–12:35 p.m. Baby Time at 2:30–3:30 p.m. if you registered. Before 11:00 a.m., use the Target entrance or the food court.

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Thu Oct 15 · 10:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library</p>

Ages 3–5 with an adult and a simple craft. LibCal lists 10:30–11:00 a.m. The storytimes overview lists 10:30–11:30 a.m.

[Preschool Storytime](https://bellinghampubliclibrary.libcal.com/event/17269877)

### Baby Time
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 16 · 10:30–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Dodson Room, Central Library</p>

The registered Friday class for babies through 12 months. It continues through Oct 30.

[Storytimes](https://bellinghampubliclibrary.org/storytimes)

## Night out

### Randy Rainbow
<p class="event-when">Fri Oct 16 · 7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Main stage, Mount Baker Theatre, 104 N Commercial St</p>

{% include event-photo.html
   src="/assets/images/bellingham/mount-baker-theatre.webp"
   alt="The Mount Baker Theatre marquee and stone front on Commercial Street in Bellingham."
   credit="Photo: Singalto1, CC BY-SA 4.0"
   source="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Baker_Theatre,_Bellingham,_WA_2016.jpg" %}

Musical comedy. The theatre marks it for mature audiences. Patrons under 18 need a chaperone. Tickets start at $49.75, including the historic-venue fee.

Maria Bamford is on the season list for Sunday, Oct 18. That index does not print a start time.

[Randy Rainbow](https://www.mountbakertheatre.com/events-tickets/randy-rainbow/) · [What's on](https://www.mountbakertheatre.com/events-tickets/)

Boulevard Park is the daytime backup if the rain starts after storytime. No special program is posted on the shore this week.
