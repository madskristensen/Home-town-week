Preschool storytime is Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday at 10:30 a.m. in the Storytelling Wing.

The library is at 58 N State Street. Monday through Friday 9:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m. Saturday 9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. Sunday closed. Phone: (801) 229-7050.

The preschool series lists Sep. 22, Sep. 23, and Sep. 24, and it keeps going on those weekdays into November. It is 30 minutes, ages 3–5, with stories, puppets, and activities.
