Noises Off is at the SCERA Monday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday at 7:30 p.m. Preschool storytime is still Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday morning.

## Evenings at the SCERA

### Noises Off
<p class="event-when">Mon Sep 21, Thu Sep 24, Fri Sep 25, and Sat Sep 26 · 7:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">SCERA Center for the Arts, 745 S State Street</p>

Michael Frayn's backstage farce. The SCERA lists Mondays, Thursdays, Fridays, and Saturdays at 7:30 p.m. from Sep. 18 through Oct. 10. Pick a date when you buy. Adult tickets are $16. Children ages 3–11 and seniors 65+ are $12. It is a full-length comedy with slamming doors, not a preschool show. The children's play If You Give a Mouse a Cookie opens Oct. 19, after these three weeks.

[Noises Off](https://scera.org/events/noises-off/)

The library is at 58 N State Street. Monday through Friday 9:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m. Saturday 9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. Sunday closed. Phone: (801) 229-7050.

The preschool series lists Sep. 22, Sep. 23, and Sep. 24, and it keeps going on those weekdays into November. It is 30 minutes, ages 3–5, with stories, puppets, and activities.

### Preschool Storytime
<p class="event-when">Tue Sep 22, Wed Sep 23, and Thu Sep 24 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Storytelling Wing, Orem Public Library</p>
