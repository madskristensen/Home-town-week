Wednesday is storytime, then an animal club. Monday is board games. Saturday afternoon is a small habitat workshop in the meeting room.

Lacey Library is at 500 College Street SE.

## Games and storytime

### All-Ages Board Game Night
<p class="event-when">Mon Oct 5 · 4:30–5:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lacey Library</p>

Games, with snacks from the Friends of the Library.

[Board Game Night](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/6a8f1baa0116850960382e87)

### Baby Storytime
<p class="event-when">Tue Oct 6 and Thu Oct 8 · 10:30–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lacey Library</p>

Ages birth to 18 months. Older siblings are welcome.

[Tuesday](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7267c2f213992f00c99ad5) · [Thursday](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7267c2f213992f00c99ad6)

### Pre-reader Storytime
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 10:30–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lacey Library</p>

Ages 18 months to 5.

[Pre-reader Storytime](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/6a72678c3f18b57d52336295)

### Kaleidoscope Play & Learn
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 11:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lacey Library</p>

Birth to age 6. The start overlaps the last half hour of pre-reader storytime. Walking Wednesdays, a short walk for all ages, also meets at 11:00 a.m. in the library lobby, rain or shine. Three things share that hour. Read the pages and pick one.

[Kaleidoscope](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7a45a9e30fe484596879e6) · [Walking Wednesdays](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/6a6a27a4898f0d2051150c8c)

### Animal Allies Club
<p class="event-when">Wed Oct 7 · 4:30–5:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Lacey Library</p>

Crafts for local pets. The listing says a visiting animal sometimes comes. Snacks are provided.

[Animal Allies Club](https://timberland.bibliocommons.com/events/6a7651c386ec588db26f745a)
