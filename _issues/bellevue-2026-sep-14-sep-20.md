Thursday morning is a bird walk at the botanical garden. Thursday afternoon is the farmers market on Bellevue Way. Sunday is storytime back at the garden.

## Thursday at the garden, then the market

### Eastside Audubon bird walk
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 17 · 9:00–10:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Bellevue Botanical Garden, 12001 Main St</p>

The garden calendar lists Eastside Audubon's monthly walk for this Thursday morning. Confirm the meeting spot on the garden's event page before you go. The garden itself is open dawn to dusk, and admission is free. Dahlias are the September display.

[Bellevue Botanical Garden](https://bellevuebotanical.org/)

### Bellevue Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Thu Sep 17 · 3:00–7:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">1717 Bellevue Way NE, in the Bellevue Presbyterian Church parking lot</p>

The main season is Thursdays, May 21 through October 1. May through August the market ran until 7:30 p.m. September and October close at 7:00. Washington-grown or Washington-made goods, live music, and kids' activities. Free parking on site. Routes 249 and 246 stop a block away. The market also names Bellevue's BellHop shuttle and the light-rail shuttle.

SNAP, Market Match, WIC, and Senior FMNP are at the information booth. Well-behaved leashed dogs are allowed, away from the food tables. Pavement can still be warm in September.

Two Thursday markets remain after this one, including the last day of the main season on Oct 1. The market has also posted two extra Saturdays later in the fall: a harvest market with the date still to be set, and a holiday market on Nov 21, both 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.

[Bellevue Farmers Market](https://bellevuefarmersmarket.org/what-to-expect)

## Sunday storytime

### Storytime at the Garden for K–2
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 20 · 1:00–1:30 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Bellevue Botanical Garden, 12001 Main St</p>

The garden calendar lists a garden-related book and a short activity for families with kindergarten through second grade. Confirm the meeting place on the garden calendar. The rest of the afternoon is the grounds: the ravine bridge, the dahlia beds, and the loop paths. Free admission. The parking lot fills on sunny Sundays.

[Bellevue Botanical Garden](https://bellevuebotanical.org/)

## If you want a playground before the market

Downtown Park is the easy stop between the garden and Bellevue Way. The playground, the canal, and the lawn are free. No ticketed program is posted there this week.
