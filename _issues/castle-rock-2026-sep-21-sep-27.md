Sunday is the farmers market at Festival Park. Friday and Saturday are still storytime at the library.

Philip & Jerry Miller Library in Castle Rock is at 100 S Wilcox Street. Kids Corner asks families to keep storytime allergen-free. Questions: 303-791-7323.

## Friday

### Baby Storytime
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 9:00–9:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

Ideal for ages 0–2. Listed on the Friday Storytime Discover page.

### Storytime Discover
<p class="event-when">Fri Sep 25 · 11:00–11:30 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

All ages. One of these activities: art, music, dance, yoga, or the storyteller's choice.

[Friday sessions](https://go.dcl.org/event/14010331)

## Saturday

### Storytime
<p class="event-when">Sat Sep 26 · 10:30–11:00 a.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Kids Corner</p>

All ages, listed for ages 0–5.

[Saturday storytime](https://go.dcl.org/event/14286732)

## Sunday

### Festival Park Farmers Market
<p class="event-when">Sun Sep 27 · 9:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.</p>
<p class="event-place">Festival Park, 300 Second St</p>

Free. The town lists every Sunday from May 24 through Oct. 11, 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Produce, eggs, meat, baked goods, and makers. The Sep. 27 listing is on the town calendar.

[Town calendar](https://www.crgov.com/calendar.aspx) · [Market page](https://www.crgov.com/3178/Festival-Park-Farmers-Market)
